This fish has a mouth full of teeth, and it drinks *blood*.
Meet the *sea lamprey*.
It doesn't have jaws.
Instead, it has a round, sucking mouth lined with rows of sharp, hooked *teeth*.
It latches onto other fish, scrapes a hole with its tongue, and feeds on their *blood* and body fluids.
It produces chemicals that keep the blood from clotting.
A single lamprey can kill around *18 kilograms* of fish in its lifetime.
In the 1900s, sea lampreys invaded North America's *Great Lakes* through shipping canals.
They devastated fish like lake trout. [lake trout | Salvelinus namaycush]
Today, scientists control them with special chemicals, barriers, and even *traps*.
Lampreys have been around for over *360 million years*, older than the dinosaurs.
And in medieval England, they were a royal delicacy.
King Henry the First reportedly died after eating too many of them.
