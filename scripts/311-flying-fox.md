title: The Bat With A Wingspan Of 1.5 Meters
subject: large flying fox | Pteropus vampyrus
mood: curious
tags: flying fox, fruit bat, bats, asia, australia
---
Some bats have wings that stretch around *1.5 meters* across.
These are *flying foxes*, the largest bats in the world.
And despite their size, they're gentle *fruit-eaters*.
Flying foxes don't use sound to find their way like many bats.
They have big eyes and an excellent sense of *smell*.
They live in huge groups called camps, sometimes with *thousands* of bats.
Flying foxes are important for forests.
They spread seeds, and pollinate flowers of many trees as they travel between them.
Some fly over *50 kilometers* in a single night looking for food.
In hot weather, they lick their wings and fan themselves to cool down.
Sadly, in extreme heatwaves in Australia, thousands of flying foxes have died.
They hang upside down to rest, and their feet lock in place, so they don't need energy to hold on.
Giant, gentle gardeners of the night.
