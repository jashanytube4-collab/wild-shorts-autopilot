mood: danger

This fish has a head like an *alligator*, and it's been around since the *dinosaurs*.
Meet the *alligator gar*, one of the largest freshwater fish in North America.
It can grow up to around *three meters* long, and weigh over *130 kilograms*.
It has two rows of sharp teeth in its upper jaw.
It's covered in hard, diamond-shaped scales that interlock like *armor*.
Native Americans once used the scales as *arrowheads*.
It can gulp air at the surface, using a special swim bladder like a *lung*.
That lets it survive in warm, low-oxygen water where other fish can't.
Its eggs are *toxic* to humans and many other animals.
Despite its scary look, it's not known to attack people.
Gars have been around in some form for over *100 million years*.
For years, people killed them, thinking they were pests.
Today, scientists know they help keep ecosystems *balanced*.
