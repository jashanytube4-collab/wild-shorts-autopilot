This bird can stay in the air for *ten months* without landing.
Meet the *common swift*.
In 2016, scientists tracked swifts migrating between Europe and Africa.
Some didn't land at all for around *ten months*.
They eat, drink and even *sleep* while flying.
They catch insects in the air, and drink raindrops.
Swifts have tiny legs that are so weak they can barely walk.
Their scientific name, *Apus*, comes from a Greek word meaning *without feet*.
They only really land to nest.
And young swifts may stay in the air for years before they land to breed.
Over its lifetime, a swift might fly millions of kilometers.
Swifts can reach speeds of over *100 kilometers per hour* in display flights.
They nest in old buildings, under roofs.
Sadly, they're declining, partly because modern buildings have fewer *gaps* to nest in.
