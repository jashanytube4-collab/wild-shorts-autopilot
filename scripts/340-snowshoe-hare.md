This hare turns *white* every winter, and brown every summer.
Meet the *snowshoe hare*.
Its color changes are triggered by the length of the *day*, not by snow.
That's a problem when snow arrives late, or melts early.
A white hare on brown ground is easy to spot.
Scientists call this a *camouflage mismatch*, and it's getting more common as winters change.
Its huge back feet work like *snowshoes*, spreading its weight so it doesn't sink.
It can run at around *45 kilometers per hour*, and leap around three meters in one jump.
Snowshoe hares are the main food of the *Canada lynx*. [Canada lynx | Lynx canadensis]
Their populations rise and fall together, in cycles of around *ten years*.
When hares are many, lynx numbers rise. When hares crash, so do the lynx.
It's one of the most famous predator-prey cycles in science.
A hare whose life depends on the weather.
