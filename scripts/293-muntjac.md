This deer *barks* like a dog.
Meet the *muntjac*, also called the *barking deer*.
When it's alarmed, it can bark for an hour or more.
Muntjacs are small, about the size of a medium dog.
Males have short antlers, and also small *fangs*.
They have glands on their faces, under their eyes, that look like a second pair of *eyes*.
They use them to mark their territory.
Reeves's muntjac comes from China, but was brought to England, where it escaped and spread.
Today, there are thousands living wild in English woodlands.
One muntjac species has one of the lowest chromosome numbers of any mammal.
Females have just *six*, and males seven.
Scientists study them to understand how chromosomes evolve.
A tiny, barking, fanged deer.
