This might be the grumpiest-looking cat in the world.
Meet the *Pallas's cat*, also called the *manul*.
It lives in the cold, rocky mountains of Central Asia, where winter temperatures can drop below *minus 40*.
That's why it has the longest, densest fur of any wild cat.
It looks fat, but most of it is *fluff*.
Its round pupils, unlike the slit eyes of house cats, help it hunt in daylight and twilight.
And its ears sit low on the sides of its head, so it can peek over rocks *without being seen*.
It's terrible at running, so it hunts by *ambush*, waiting by burrows for pikas and voles. [American pika | Ochotona princeps]
Pallas's cats are famously hard to keep in zoos.
Their immune systems evolved in such a remote, isolated place that ordinary infections can be deadly for them.
And when they're annoyed, they make that famous *face*.
Honestly, it's always that face.
