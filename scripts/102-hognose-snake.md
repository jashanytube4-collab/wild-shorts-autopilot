title: The Snake That Fakes Its Own Death
subject: eastern hognose snake | Heterodon platirhinos
mood: curious

This snake is the drama queen of the animal world.
Meet the *eastern hognose snake*.
When threatened, it first tries to look scary.
It flattens its neck like a *cobra*, hisses loudly, and pretends to strike, often with its mouth *closed*.
If that doesn't work, it moves on to plan B.
It rolls onto its back, opens its mouth, sticks out its tongue, and goes *limp*.
It may even release a foul smell and a little *blood* from its mouth.
To a predator, it looks *dead*.
But here's the funny part.
If you flip it right side up, it immediately rolls onto its back *again*.
As if to say, "No, I'm definitely dead."
Its upturned snout is used like a shovel for digging up *toads*, its favorite food. [American toad | Anaxyrus americanus]
It's even resistant to toad toxins.
An Oscar-worthy performance, every single time.
