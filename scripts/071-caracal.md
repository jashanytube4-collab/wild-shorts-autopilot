This cat can leap *three meters* up and swat birds out of the sky.
Meet the *caracal*.
It's a medium-sized wild cat found across Africa, the Middle East and India.
Its back legs are long and packed with muscle, perfect for explosive *jumps*.
When a flock of birds takes off, it springs straight up and knocks them down with its paws.
Sometimes it grabs *more than one* in a single leap.
And those ears? Those black tufts may help it with hearing and *communication*.
Caracals can move them independently, and pick up the faint sounds of prey.
Their name comes from a Turkish word meaning *black ear*.
In ancient India and Persia, caracals were trained to hunt birds for *royalty*.
People would even bet on which caracal could knock down the most pigeons.
That's where the phrase "to put the cat among the pigeons" may come from.
