title: Sloths Are So Slow That Algae Grows On Them
subject: brown-throated sloth | Bradypus variegatus
mood: curious
tags: sloth, slow animals, rainforest, costa rica, weird animals
---
This animal is so slow that *plants grow on it*.
Meet the *sloth*.
It moves so little that *algae* grows in its fur, giving it a greenish tint that helps it hide.
Its fur is home to moths, beetles and fungi, a whole tiny *ecosystem*.
Sloths spend almost their entire lives hanging upside down.
They eat, sleep, and even give birth *in the trees*.
But once a week, they do something risky.
They climb all the way down to the ground to *poop*.
Nobody knows exactly why, but it's when they're most likely to be caught by predators.
Their digestion is the slowest of any mammal; a single meal can take *weeks* to digest.
And here's a surprise: sloths are great *swimmers*.
In water, they move about *three times faster* than on land.
They can hold their breath for up to *40 minutes* by slowing their heart rate.
Slow, but surprisingly *survival-ready*.
