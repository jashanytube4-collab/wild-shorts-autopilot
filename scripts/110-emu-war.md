title: The War Australia Lost To Birds
subject: emu | Dromaius novaehollandiae
mood: epic
tags: emu war, emu, australia, history, funny animals
---
In 1932, Australia went to war against *birds*.
And the birds *won*.
After World War One, many veterans were given farmland in Western Australia.
Then around *20,000 emus* moved in, eating and trampling their wheat.
So the government sent soldiers, armed with *machine guns*.
It didn't go as planned.
The emus scattered in every direction whenever the soldiers opened fire.
They could run at around *50 kilometers per hour*, and they were surprisingly hard to hit.
One officer said that if he had a division of soldiers as tough as emus, he could face any army in the world.
After about a month, and thousands of bullets, the soldiers were *withdrawn*.
The newspapers mocked it as the *Great Emu War*.
Emus are the second-largest birds on Earth, standing up to *1.9 meters* tall.
And just like the cassowary, the *dads* sit on the eggs, barely eating for around *eight weeks*.
