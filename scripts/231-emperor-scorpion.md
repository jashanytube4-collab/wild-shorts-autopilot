Under ultraviolet light, scorpions *glow*.
This is the *emperor scorpion*, one of the largest scorpions in the world, around *20 centimeters* long.
Shine a UV light on it, and its body lights up a bright blue-green.
Scientists still aren't sure why.
One idea is that it helps scorpions detect light, so they know when to hide.
Emperor scorpions live in the rainforests of *West Africa*.
They mostly use their huge claws to catch prey, rather than their sting.
And their venom is *mild*, often compared to a bee sting.
Mothers give birth to live babies, which climb onto her *back*.
She carries them for weeks, protecting them until they're ready to survive alone.
Scorpions can survive for months without eating.
Some scorpions have been frozen overnight, then thawed out the next day and walked away.
They've been around for over *400 million years*.
Ancient, armored, and glowing in the dark.
