title: The Snake That Spits Venom In Your Eyes
subject: black-necked spitting cobra | Naja nigricollis
mood: danger
tags: spitting cobra, cobra, venomous snakes, africa, snakes
---
This snake can spit venom straight into your *eyes* from over two meters away.
Meet the *spitting cobra*.
When threatened, it rears up, spreads its hood, and fires two streams of venom from tiny holes in its fangs.
And it doesn't just spray randomly.
It tracks your face, and *aims* for the eyes.
Scientists found it even *predicts* where your head will move next, adjusting its aim.
The venom causes extreme pain, and can lead to *blindness* if it's not washed out quickly.
Why spit instead of bite?
Scientists believe the ability evolved for *defense*, possibly against our own human ancestors.
They found the venom of spitting cobras is especially good at causing *pain*, the perfect way to drive off a primate.
Spitting evolved separately at least *three times* in different cobras.
Its bite is still dangerous, too.
So if you meet one, cover your eyes and *back away*.
