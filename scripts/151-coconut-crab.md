This crab can crack open *coconuts*, and it hunts *birds*.
Meet the *coconut crab*, the largest land invertebrate in the world.
Its legs can span about *a meter*, and it can weigh around *four kilograms*.
Its claws have one of the strongest pinches of any animal, comparable to the bite of a *lion*.
It uses them to rip open coconuts, husk and all.
And it climbs trees easily.
In 2016, a scientist filmed a coconut crab climbing up to a sleeping *seabird* at night. [red-footed booby | Sula sula]
It broke the bird's wing, dragged it down, and other crabs came to share the meal.
Coconut crabs are actually giant *hermit crabs*.
When young, they carry shells on their backs, but adults grow their own hard armor.
They can live over *60 years*.
Some people think coconut crabs may have eaten the remains of *Amelia Earhart*, if she crashed on a remote Pacific island.
That's never been proven.
