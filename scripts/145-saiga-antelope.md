This antelope looks like it came from a *Star Wars* planet.
Meet the *saiga*, an antelope that lives on the steppes of Central Asia.
Its huge, floppy nose works like a built-in *air filter*.
In summer, it filters out the dust kicked up by the herd.
In winter, it *warms up* the freezing air before it reaches the lungs.
Saigas have been around since the *Ice Age*, when they lived alongside mammoths.
But in 2015, something terrifying happened.
In just a few weeks, around *200,000* saigas suddenly died.
Scientists later linked it to a bacteria that turned deadly in unusually *warm and humid* weather.
Poaching for their horns had already made them *critically endangered*.
But in a huge success story, their numbers bounced back to over a *million* in Kazakhstan.
In 2023, they were moved off the critically endangered list.
A strange nose, and a comeback for the ages.
