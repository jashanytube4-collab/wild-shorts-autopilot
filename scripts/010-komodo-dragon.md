title: Why You Should Never Get Bitten By A Komodo Dragon

This lizard can eat *80 percent* of its body weight in a single meal.
Meet the *Komodo dragon*, the largest lizard on Earth.
It can grow up to *three meters* long and weigh more than *70 kilograms*.
For years, people believed its bite killed with *dirty bacteria*.
But scientists discovered it actually has *venom glands* in its lower jaw.
The venom stops blood from clotting and sends prey into *shock*.
So a Komodo only has to bite once, then follow its victim, sometimes for *days*, using its forked tongue to *smell* the way.
It can take down *deer*, *pigs*, and even *water buffalo*. [water buffalo | Bubalus bubalis]
Adults are also *cannibals*.
That's why baby Komodos spend their first years living up in the *trees*.
And here's the wildest part.
Females can lay eggs that hatch *without a male*.
In 2006, two zoo dragons in England did exactly that, surprising scientists around the world.
