title: The Animal That Can Regrow Its Brain
subject: axolotl | Ambystoma mexicanum
mood: curious
tags: axolotl, regeneration, salamander, mexico, weird animals
---
This animal can regrow its *legs*, its *heart*, and even parts of its *brain*.
Meet the *axolotl*, a salamander from the lakes of *Mexico City*.
Cut off a leg, and it grows back perfectly, bones, muscles, nerves and all, with *no scar*.
It can do this again and again.
Scientists have even watched axolotls repair damaged *spinal cords* and *eyes*.
But the axolotl has another strange trick: it *never grows up*.
Most salamanders lose their gills and move onto land as adults.
The axolotl keeps its baby features, including those feathery *gills*, its whole life.
Those frilly things on its head? They're how it breathes underwater.
It even has a smile that looks permanently *happy*.
Sadly, in the wild, it's *critically endangered*, surviving mainly in a few canals around Mexico City.
Millions live in labs and aquariums around the world.
But almost none are left in the place they come from.
