title: The Gecko That Looks Like A Dead Leaf
subject: satanic leaf-tailed gecko | Uroplatus phantasticus
mood: curious
tags: leaf tailed gecko, camouflage, madagascar, geckos, reptiles
---
This isn't a dead leaf. It's a *gecko*.
Meet the *satanic leaf-tailed gecko* from Madagascar.
Its tail is flat and shaped exactly like a dried *leaf*, complete with notches that look like bite marks.
Its body is brown and wrinkled, and it can even change shade to match its surroundings.
During the day, it hangs motionless among the leaves.
Predators simply don't see it.
At night, it comes alive, hunting insects with its huge *eyes*.
Those eyes have no eyelids, so it licks them clean with its *tongue*.
When threatened, it flattens its body, opens its bright red mouth, and lets out a *scream*.
Its name sounds scary, but it comes from its spiky horns above its eyes.
Like many animals in Madagascar, it lives nowhere else on Earth.
Sadly, it's threatened by forest loss and the pet trade.
Perfect camouflage, from head to tail.
