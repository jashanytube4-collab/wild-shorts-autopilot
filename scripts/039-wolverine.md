This animal is the size of a dog, yet it can drive off *bears*.
Meet the *wolverine*, the largest land member of the *weasel family*.
It weighs around *15 kilograms*, but it's built like a tank.
It has huge paws that work like *snowshoes*, and powerful jaws that can crush *frozen meat* and bone.
Wolverines have been seen standing up to *wolves* and even *grizzly bears* to keep their food. [grizzly bear | Ursus arctos horribilis]
They roam huge territories, sometimes traveling *40 kilometers* in a single day, straight up mountains.
One wolverine was tracked climbing over *1,500 meters* in under two hours.
They store leftovers in the snow, like a natural *freezer*.
Their fur is so frost-resistant that people once used it to line the hoods of *Arctic parkas*.
They're rarely seen, and tend to stay far from people.
Small, fierce, and absolutely relentless.
