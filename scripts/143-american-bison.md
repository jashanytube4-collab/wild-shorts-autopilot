title: The Animal That Almost Disappeared From America
subject: American bison | Bison bison
mood: epic

Once, *tens of millions* of bison roamed North America.
By the 1880s, fewer than *1,000* were left.
Hunters had killed them by the millions, for hides, sport, and to starve out Native American tribes.
The *American bison* came frighteningly close to extinction.
Today, thanks to conservation, there are hundreds of thousands again.
It's the largest land animal in North America.
A big male can weigh over *900 kilograms*.
And yet, it can run at around *55 kilometers per hour*, and jump fences nearly *two meters* high.
In *Yellowstone*, bison injure more visitors than any other animal.
Most of them were trying to get a *selfie*.
When a bison raises its tail, it's either about to charge, or about to *poop*.
Either way, you should move away.
In 2016, the bison became the national mammal of the *United States*.
