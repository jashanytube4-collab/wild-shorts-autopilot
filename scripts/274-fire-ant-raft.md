title: Fire Ants Build Living Rafts
subject: red imported fire ant | Solenopsis invicta
mood: curious
tags: fire ants, ant raft, floods, insects, teamwork
---
When floods come, fire ants build *living rafts*.
Thousands of ants link their legs and jaws together, forming a floating mat.
The queen and the young are placed in the middle, where it's safest.
Their bodies trap tiny air bubbles, keeping the raft *afloat*.
These rafts can float for *days* or even weeks, until they reach dry land.
And the raft constantly moves, with ants on the bottom swapping places with those on top.
*Red imported fire ants* are native to South America.
They arrived in the US in the 1930s, probably on cargo ships.
Today, they've spread across the southern US, and to other countries too.
Their sting burns like *fire*, which is how they got their name.
And they attack as a group, stinging again and again.
Scientists study fire ant rafts to design self-assembling *robots*.
Teamwork that refuses to sink.
