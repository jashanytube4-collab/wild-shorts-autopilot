title: The Red Devil Squid
subject: Humboldt squid | Dosidicus gigas
mood: danger
tags: humboldt squid, red devil, squid, ocean, dangerous animals
---
Fishermen call this squid the *red devil*.
Meet the *Humboldt squid*, from the eastern Pacific Ocean.
It can grow around *two meters* long.
And it hunts in huge groups, sometimes over a *thousand* squid.
Its arms and tentacles are covered in suckers lined with sharp, tooth-like *rings*.
Its beak is strong enough to tear through flesh.
Humboldt squid can flash colors, from white to deep *red*, very fast.
Scientists think it may be a way of *communicating* with each other.
They're also aggressive, sometimes attacking divers.
And they've been seen eating each other, especially when one gets hurt.
Humboldt squid can shoot through the water using jet propulsion at around *24 kilometers per hour*.
In recent decades, they've spread north into new areas.
A fast, flashing, and fearless hunter.
