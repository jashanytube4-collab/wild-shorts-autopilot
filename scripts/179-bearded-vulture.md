title: The Bird That Eats Bones
subject: bearded vulture | Gypaetus barbatus
mood: epic
tags: bearded vulture, lammergeier, birds of prey, bones, mountains
---
This bird's diet is around *80 percent* bone.
Meet the *bearded vulture*, also called the *lammergeier*.
It lives high in the mountains of Europe, Africa and Asia.
When other scavengers finish a carcass, it takes what's left: the *bones*.
Its stomach acid is extremely strong, able to dissolve bone in about a day.
But some bones are too big to swallow.
So it carries them high into the air, and *drops* them onto rocks to smash them into pieces.
It does this again and again until they break.
Its feathers are naturally white, but it rubs itself in red, iron-rich mud, turning *orange*.
Scientists think it may be a sign of status.
Legend says the ancient Greek playwright *Aeschylus* was killed when an eagle dropped a tortoise on his bald head, mistaking it for a rock.
If that story is true, the culprit may have been a bearded vulture.
