title: The Bird That Recognizes Itself In A Mirror
subject: Eurasian magpie | Pica pica
mood: curious
tags: magpie, smart birds, mirror test, corvids, birds
---
This bird recognizes itself in a *mirror*.
In 2008, scientists put a small colored sticker on the throats of *Eurasian magpies*.
They could only see it in a mirror.
When the magpies looked at their reflection, they tried to scratch the sticker *off*.
At the time, it was the first non-mammal known to pass this famous test.
Magpies are part of the *crow* family, which includes some of the smartest birds on Earth.
They can recognize individual human faces.
They store food, and remember where they hid it.
Magpies have even been seen holding what look like *funerals*, gathering around a dead magpie.
There's an old myth that magpies steal shiny things.
But one study found they were actually *nervous* around shiny objects.
In Korea and China, magpies are seen as symbols of *good luck*.
Clever, curious, and full of character.
