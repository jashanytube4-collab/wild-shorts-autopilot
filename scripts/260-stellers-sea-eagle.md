title: The Biggest Eagle In The World
subject: Steller's sea eagle | Haliaeetus pelagicus
mood: epic
tags: stellers sea eagle, biggest eagle, russia, japan, birds of prey

This might be the *heaviest eagle* in the world.
Meet the *Steller's sea eagle*.
Females can weigh around *nine kilograms*, with wings spanning over *two meters*.
It has a massive, bright orange-yellow *beak*, one of the biggest of any eagle.
It lives along the coasts of eastern Russia, and spends winters in places like northern *Japan*.
It hunts mostly *fish*, especially salmon, snatching them from rivers and the sea.
In winter, hundreds gather on drift ice off Japan's *Hokkaido* coast.
Photographers travel from around the world to see them.
In 2020, one Steller's sea eagle was spotted far from home, in *Alaska*, then *Texas*, then eastern Canada and Maine.
Birdwatchers followed it across North America for months.
Sadly, there may be only around *4,000* left in the wild.
A giant with a golden beak, ruling the frozen coasts.
