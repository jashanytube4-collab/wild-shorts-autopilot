mood: danger

This beautiful wasp turns cockroaches into *zombies*.
Meet the *jewel wasp*.
When she finds a cockroach, she stings it *twice*. [American cockroach | Periplaneta americana]
The first sting paralyzes its front legs for a moment.
The second sting goes straight into its *brain*.
That venom takes away the cockroach's will to escape.
The wasp then bites off half of its *antennae* and drinks some of its blood.
Then she leads the cockroach, like a dog on a leash, back to her burrow.
The cockroach is much bigger than her, but it just *follows*.
She lays an egg on it, seals it in, and leaves.
When the larva hatches, it eats the still-living cockroach from the *inside*.
Then it emerges as a shiny, metallic green wasp.
Nature isn't always pretty, but it is *creative*.
