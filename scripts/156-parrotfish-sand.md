Some of those white sand beaches you love are made of *fish poop*.
That's thanks to the *parrotfish*.
It has teeth fused together into a beak, just like a *parrot*.
It uses that beak to scrape algae off coral and rocks.
Along the way, it swallows bits of coral.
Special teeth in its throat grind it into fine *sand*.
Then it comes out the other end.
A large parrotfish can produce hundreds of kilograms of sand every *year*.
On some tropical islands, parrotfish are thought to make a large share of the white sand.
Some parrotfish also sleep inside a bubble of *mucus* they create every night.
It may help hide their scent from predators and keep away parasites.
And many parrotfish change from female to *male* as they grow, changing color too.
So the next time you're walking on a tropical beach, remember who made it.
