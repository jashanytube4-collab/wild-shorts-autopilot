title: The Fish That Heats Its Own Eyes
subject: swordfish | Xiphias gladius
mood: epic

This fish has a special organ that *heats its eyes and brain*.
Meet the *swordfish*.
It dives into deep, cold, dark water to hunt.
But cold eyes see poorly.
So it has a special heater organ near its eyes, made of modified muscle.
It keeps its eyes and brain warmer than the water around them.
That lets it see fast-moving prey much more clearly.
Its long, flat "sword" isn't used to stab.
It's used to *slash* at schools of fish, stunning or injuring them.
Swordfish have even been found with their swords stuck in *boats* and whales.
Adults have no scales and no teeth.
They can grow over *four meters* long, and weigh over *500 kilograms*.
Some swordfish swim long distances, between warm waters to feed, and cooler waters at depth.
A deep sea hunter, with built-in heated *goggles*.
