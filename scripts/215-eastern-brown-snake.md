This snake causes more deaths in Australia than any other snake.
Meet the *eastern brown snake*.
It doesn't look like much, plain brown and slender.
But its venom is among the most toxic of any land snake, second only to the inland taipan in some tests.
It contains toxins that cause *bleeding* and stop the blood from clotting.
Unlike the shy inland taipan, it lives where people do: farms, suburbs and backyards.
It's attracted by mice that live around houses and barns. [house mouse | Mus musculus]
It's fast, and when cornered, it rears up in an S shape, ready to *strike*.
Its bite can be almost painless, so some people don't realize how serious it is.
But with first aid and antivenom, most people *survive*.
Australian first aid is simple: bandage the whole limb tightly, keep still, and call for help.
And if you see one, back away. Most bites happen when people try to *kill* the snake.
