title: The Animal Lions Fear The Most
subject: African buffalo | Syncerus caffer
mood: danger

Hunters call it the *black death*.
The *Cape buffalo* weighs up to *900 kilograms*, and it's known for one thing: it *never forgets*.
Wounded buffalo have been known to circle back and *ambush* the people chasing them.
Its massive horns fuse into a bony shield across its forehead called a *boss*.
It's one of Africa's *Big Five*, and it's been blamed for killing many hunters over the years.
Even *lions* have to be careful. [lion | Panthera leo]
It can take a whole pride to bring one down.
And if a buffalo is attacked, the herd often comes *back* to help.
There's famous footage of buffaloes chasing lions away from a calf, and even tossing a lion into the air.
Here's a strange fact: buffalo herds seem to *vote* on where to go.
Females stand up, stare in one direction, and the herd moves toward the most popular choice.
Democracy, with horns.
