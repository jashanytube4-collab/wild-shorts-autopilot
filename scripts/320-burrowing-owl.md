This owl lives *underground*.
Meet the *burrowing owl*.
Unlike most owls, it nests in holes in the ground.
It often moves into old burrows dug by prairie dogs, tortoises, or ground squirrels. [black-tailed prairie dog | Cynomys ludovicianus]
And it's active during the *day* too.
When threatened inside its burrow, it can make a sound like a *rattlesnake*.
That can scare predators away.
Burrowing owls also collect animal *dung*, and spread it around their burrow entrance.
It attracts dung beetles, which the owls eat.
They're small, with long legs, perfect for running on the ground.
They often bob their heads up and down when they see something interesting.
In Florida, people put up "owl crossing" signs, and protect their burrows.
Cute, clever, and living underground.
