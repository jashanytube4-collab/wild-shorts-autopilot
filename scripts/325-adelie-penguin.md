Penguins *propose* with pebbles.
*Adélie penguins* in Antarctica build their nests out of small stones.
Good pebbles are rare, so they're very valuable.
Males search for the best stones, and present them to females.
If she accepts, they build a nest together.
But pebbles are so valuable that penguins often *steal* them from their neighbors' nests.
Some penguins even sneak a pebble away while the owner isn't looking.
Adélie penguins are small, about *70 centimeters* tall, with a white ring around their eyes.
They can swim down to around *175 meters* deep, chasing krill and fish.
Every year, they walk long distances across the ice to reach their breeding grounds.
Early explorers noted that some penguin behaviors were surprisingly *rowdy*.
Their colonies can have hundreds of thousands of birds.
A love story, built one stolen pebble at a time.
