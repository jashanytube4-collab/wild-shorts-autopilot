One condor flew around *170 kilometers* without flapping its wings once.
Meet the *Andean condor*, one of the heaviest flying birds on Earth.
It can weigh around *15 kilograms*, with wings stretching over *three meters*.
Flapping such big wings takes a lot of energy.
So condors barely flap at all.
In one study, scientists found condors flapped only around *one percent* of their flight time, mostly during takeoff.
They ride rising columns of warm air, called *thermals*, and winds pushed up by mountains.
Condors are scavengers, feeding mostly on dead animals.
They can live over *70 years* in captivity.
Pairs usually raise just *one chick* every two years.
The Andean condor is a national symbol of several countries, including *Chile*, Bolivia, Colombia and Ecuador.
In Andean legends, it was believed to carry the sun into the sky.
A master of the mountain winds.
