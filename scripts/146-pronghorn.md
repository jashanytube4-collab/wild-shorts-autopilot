This animal runs as if it's still being chased by a *cheetah*.
Meet the *pronghorn*, the fastest land animal in North America.
It can hit speeds of nearly *90 kilometers per hour*.
And unlike a cheetah, it can keep going for *kilometers*.
But nothing in North America today can come close to catching it.
So why is it so fast?
Scientists believe it evolved to outrun the *American cheetah*, which went extinct about *12,000 years* ago.
The predator is gone, but the speed stayed.
Pronghorns have huge eyes, which can spot movement from kilometers away.
Its horns are unique, a bony core with a sheath that's *shed every year*.
Despite the name and look, it isn't a true antelope.
Its closest living relatives are *giraffes* and okapis.
The last survivor of an ancient family, still running from a ghost.
