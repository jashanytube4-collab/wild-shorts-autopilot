title: Africa's Biggest Eagle
subject: martial eagle | Polemaetus bellicosus
mood: epic

This eagle's grip is said to be strong enough to break a man's arm.
Meet the *martial eagle*, Africa's largest eagle.
Its wings can span over *two meters*.
It can spot prey from several *kilometers* away.
It hunts from high in the sky, dropping down on its prey in a fast, powerful dive.
It catches guinea fowl, monitor lizards, hares, and even small *antelopes*. [helmeted guineafowl | Numida meleagris]
Some have been seen attacking young warthogs and jackals.
Martial eagles mate for life, and build huge nests.
They usually raise just one chick every two years.
Sadly, they're *endangered*.
Farmers sometimes poison or shoot them, thinking they kill livestock.
Power lines are another big danger.
A powerful, rare king of the African skies.
