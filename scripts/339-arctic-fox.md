This fox can survive temperatures as low as *minus 50 degrees*.
Meet the *Arctic fox*.
It has the warmest fur of any mammal tested, and it doesn't start shivering until it's extremely cold.
Its fur changes with the seasons.
In winter, it turns snowy *white*. In summer, it turns brown or grey, to match the rocks.
Its ears are short and its legs stubby, to lose less heat.
It even has fur on the bottom of its *paws*.
In winter, Arctic foxes often follow *polar bears*, eating leftovers from their kills. [polar bear | Ursus maritimus]
They listen for lemmings under the snow, then pounce, diving headfirst.
In 2019, a young female Arctic fox walked from Norway to Canada, around *3,500 kilometers*, in just *76 days*.
She crossed sea ice, glaciers and the open Arctic.
Arctic foxes can curl their bushy tails around their bodies, like a blanket.
Built for the coldest place on Earth.
