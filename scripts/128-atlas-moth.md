title: The Moth That Never Eats
subject: Atlas moth | Attacus atlas
mood: curious

This giant moth *never eats*.
Meet the *Atlas moth*, one of the largest moths in the world.
Its wings can span around *25 centimeters*, the size of a dinner plate.
But as an adult, it doesn't even have a working *mouth*.
It lives off fat stored when it was a caterpillar.
So it only lives for around *one to two weeks*, just long enough to find a mate.
Look closely at the tips of its wings.
They look like the heads of *snakes*.
When threatened, the moth drops to the ground and flaps slowly, making those "snake heads" move.
Its caterpillars spin silk that's used in some places to make small purses.
In Cantonese, its name means *snake's head moth*.
And females release a scent that males can detect from kilometers away, using their huge feathery *antennae*.
A beautiful giant with a very short life.
