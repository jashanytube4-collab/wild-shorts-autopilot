One of the most expensive coffees in the world comes from an animal's *poop*.
It's called *kopi luwak*.
The *Asian palm civet* eats ripe coffee cherries. [coffee | Coffea arabica]
It digests the fruit, but the coffee beans pass through.
Farmers collect the beans from the droppings, clean them, and roast them.
People say the digestion changes the flavor, making it smoother.
Some kopi luwak sells for hundreds of dollars per kilogram.
But there's a dark side.
To make more money, many civets are kept in tiny cages and force-fed coffee cherries.
Animal welfare groups say this is *cruel*, and urge people to avoid it.
Wild civets are nocturnal, and live in forests across South and Southeast Asia.
They also eat fruit, insects, and small animals.
So if you want to try it, make sure it's truly *wild-sourced*.
Or maybe just stick to regular coffee.
