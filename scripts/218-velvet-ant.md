title: The Ant That Isn't An Ant, Called The Cow Killer
subject: eastern velvet ant | Dasymutilla occidentalis
mood: danger
tags: velvet ant, cow killer, painful sting, wasp, insects
---
This fuzzy insect is nicknamed the *cow killer*.
Meet the *velvet ant*.
It looks like a giant, fluffy red ant, but it's actually a wingless female *wasp*.
And its sting is one of the most painful of any insect.
People joked that it was painful enough to kill a cow.
It can't, but the pain is intense and can last for a while.
Velvet ants are almost impossible for predators to eat.
Their bodies are covered in an incredibly tough armor, so hard that scientists struggled to pierce it with steel pins.
They also *squeak* loudly when grabbed, and give off a warning smell.
And their bright colors scream "stay away."
One scientist called velvet ants "indestructible" after testing them against lizards, birds and shrews.
Almost every predator gave up.
Females lay their eggs inside the nests of bees and wasps, and their larvae eat the young.
Fuzzy, beautiful, and best left alone.
