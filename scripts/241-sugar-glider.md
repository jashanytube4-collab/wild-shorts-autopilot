This tiny animal can glide around *50 meters* through the air.
Meet the *sugar glider*.
It's a small marsupial from Australia and New Guinea, about the size of a *hand*.
Between its front and back legs is a thin, stretchy membrane.
When it leaps, it spreads its limbs, and that membrane becomes a *wing*.
It steers using its legs and its long, fluffy tail.
It's called a sugar glider because it loves sweet things, like tree *sap* and nectar.
It even chews holes in tree bark to get the sweet gum flowing.
Sugar gliders live in groups of up to around *seven* adults, and huddle together to stay warm.
Scientists found that they glow *pink* under ultraviolet light.
In 2020, it was discovered that what we called the sugar glider is actually *three* different species.
