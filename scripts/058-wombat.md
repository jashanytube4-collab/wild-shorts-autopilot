title: The Animal That Poops Cubes
subject: common wombat | Vombatus ursinus
mood: curious

This is the only animal in the world known to poop *cubes*.
Meet the *wombat*.
Each night, it can leave behind around *100* little cube-shaped droppings.
How? Scientists found that its intestines have stiff and stretchy sections that shape the poop into *corners*.
That research even won an *Ig Nobel Prize* in 2019.
Wombats stack them on rocks and logs to mark their *territory*.
And since cubes don't roll, they stay put.
Their pouch faces *backwards*, so it doesn't fill with dirt when they dig.
Their backside is mostly hard *cartilage*.
When a predator follows them into a burrow, they block the tunnel with their rump.
And despite looking chubby, they can run at around *40 kilometers per hour*.
Australia's cutest bulldozer.
