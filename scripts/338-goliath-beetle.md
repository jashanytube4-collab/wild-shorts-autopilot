This beetle is one of the heaviest insects in the world.
Meet the *Goliath beetle*, from the forests of Africa.
Adults can grow over *ten centimeters* long.
And as larvae, they can weigh around *100 grams*.
That's as heavy as a small apple.
Adult Goliath beetles feed on tree sap and fruit.
Males have a Y-shaped horn on their heads, which they use to fight other males.
They have strong claws for climbing trees.
And despite their size, they can *fly*.
When they take off, they make a loud buzzing sound, like a small *helicopter*.
Their bodies have bold black and white patterns.
In some places, they're collected and sold as *pets*.
