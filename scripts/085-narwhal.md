title: The Unicorn Of The Sea Is Real
subject: narwhal | Monodon monoceros
mood: curious

The *unicorn of the sea* is real.
Meet the *narwhal*, a whale that lives in the icy waters of the *Arctic*.
That long spiral "horn" isn't a horn at all. It's a *tooth*.
It grows out through the narwhal's upper lip, and it can reach around *three meters* long.
And it twists in a *spiral*, always to the left.
Scientists found it's packed with millions of *nerve endings*, so it might help sense changes in the water.
Narwhals have also been filmed using their tusks to tap and *stun fish*.
Mostly, it's the males that grow tusks.
In medieval Europe, narwhal tusks were sold as *unicorn horns*, believed to cure poison.
They were worth more than their weight in *gold*.
Queen Elizabeth the First reportedly owned one worth as much as a *castle*.
Narwhals dive over *1,500 meters* deep, hunting fish and squid under the sea ice.
And they've never survived long in captivity.
