title: The Seal That Lives Under The Antarctic Ice
subject: Weddell seal | Leptonychotes weddellii

This seal spends the dark Antarctic winter *under the ice*.
Meet the *Weddell seal*.
It lives farther south than any other mammal on Earth.
In winter, when the sea freezes over, it stays in the water below.
To breathe, it uses its teeth to grind breathing holes in the *ice*.
Over time, this wears down its teeth.
Weddell seals can dive down to around *600 meters*, and stay under for over an *hour*.
They sing strange, sci-fi sounding songs under the ice, with trills, whistles and chirps.
Scientists even think they can find their way back to breathing holes in the dark, using the light from above, and their memory.
Weddell seal pups can swim within around *two weeks* of being born.
Mothers lose a lot of weight while nursing, not eating for weeks.
A seal that thrives in one of the harshest places on Earth.
