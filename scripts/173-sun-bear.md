title: The Bear With The Longest Tongue
subject: sun bear | Helarctos malayanus
mood: curious

This bear's tongue can be up to *25 centimeters* long.
Meet the *sun bear*, the smallest bear in the world.
It uses that long tongue to lick honey and insects out of tree holes and *beehives*.
That's why it's sometimes called the *honey bear*.
Each sun bear has a golden patch on its chest, shaped a bit like a *rising sun*.
And every patch is unique.
Sun bears are excellent climbers, with long, curved claws.
They even build nests high in trees to *sleep*.
Their skin is very loose, so if a predator grabs them, they can twist around and *bite back*.
In 2019, a video of a sun bear at a zoo in China went viral.
People thought it looked so human, standing upright, that it must be a person in a *costume*.
The zoo had to explain it was a real bear.
Sun bears are now *vulnerable*, threatened by forest loss and poaching.
