This spider has been found hiding in *bananas*.
Meet the *Brazilian wandering spider*.
Its legs span up to *15 centimeters*.
And unlike most spiders, it doesn't build a web and wait.
It *wanders* the forest floor at night, hunting.
When threatened, it raises its front legs high in the air, showing off red-banded fangs.
That's your warning.
Its venom is one of the most potent of any spider.
It can cause intense pain, sweating, and in rare cases, *death*.
But here's the strangest side effect.
In men, a bite can cause a long, painful *erection*.
Scientists actually studied the toxin as a possible *medicine* for that.
Its scientific name, *Phoneutria*, means *murderess* in Greek.
Thankfully, most bites are *dry*, with little or no venom, and good antivenom exists.
Still, if your bananas come from South America, maybe give them a *shake*.
