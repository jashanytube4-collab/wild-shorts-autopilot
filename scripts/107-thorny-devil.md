This lizard can drink water through its *skin*.
Meet the *thorny devil*, from the deserts of Australia.
It's covered in spikes, and it looks terrifying.
But it's only about *20 centimeters* long, and completely harmless.
Water is rare in the desert, so it has a clever trick.
Its skin is covered in tiny grooves between the scales.
When it touches damp sand or dew, water gets pulled into those grooves and flows toward its *mouth*.
It works like a network of tiny *straws*.
It eats only ants, often thousands in a single meal, flicking them up one by one.
On the back of its neck is a spiky bump called a *false head*.
When threatened, it tucks its real head down and presents the fake one.
It also changes color, from pale yellow to dark brown, depending on temperature and mood.
Spiky outside, *gentle* inside.
