This octopus can squeeze its huge body through a surprisingly *tiny hole*.
The *giant Pacific octopus* is the biggest octopus on Earth.
Its arms can span over *four meters*, and it can weigh more than *50 kilograms*.
But it has no bones, so if its beak fits through a gap, the rest of it can follow.
It has *three hearts* and *blue blood*.
Each arm carries over *200 suckers*, and each sucker can taste what it touches.
It's also incredibly smart.
Octopuses in aquariums have unscrewed jars, escaped tanks at night, and even recognized *individual keepers*.
One octopus in New Zealand named *Inky* slipped out of his tank, crawled across the floor, and escaped down a drainpipe into the *ocean*.
Despite its size, it only lives around *three to five years*.
A mother guards her eggs for months without eating, and dies soon after they hatch.
