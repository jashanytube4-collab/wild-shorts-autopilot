Is that a *hummingbird* in Europe?
Probably not. It's a *moth*.
Meet the *hummingbird hawk-moth*.
It hovers in front of flowers, beating its wings so fast they *hum*.
Then it unrolls a long tongue to sip nectar, just like a hummingbird.
Its wings beat around *70 times* per second.
It's one of the few moths that's active during the *day*.
And it can remember which flower colors give the most nectar.
Hummingbird hawk-moths migrate from southern Europe and North Africa to northern Europe every summer.
They can fly at around *20 kilometers per hour*.
People across Europe often report seeing "tiny hummingbirds" in their gardens.
But real hummingbirds only live in the *Americas*. [ruby-throated hummingbird | Archilochus colubris]
It's an amazing example of two very different animals evolving the same way of life.
A moth that does a perfect hummingbird impression.
