The echidna is one of only *two* kinds of mammals that lay eggs.
The other is the *platypus*. [platypus | Ornithorhynchus anatinus]
It's covered in spines like a hedgehog, but it's not related to one.
It uses a long, sticky tongue to slurp up ants and termites.
The female lays a single leathery egg into a temporary *pouch* on her belly.
The baby, called a *puggle*, hatches inside the pouch and drinks milk that seeps from patches on her skin.
When its spines start to grow, mom moves it into a burrow.
Echidnas have a body temperature of around *32 degrees*, one of the lowest of any mammal.
In winter, they can hibernate for months.
And yes, the males have a four-headed *penis*.
They use two heads at a time, switching sides.
Echidnas can live for over *45 years*, which is very long for their size.
Australia put one on its *five-cent coin*.
