This shark has been found with *license plates* in its stomach.
Meet the *tiger shark*, nicknamed the *garbage can of the sea*.
It eats fish, seals, turtles, seabirds, and even other *sharks*. [green sea turtle | Chelonia mydas]
But scientists have also found tires, tin cans, and other trash inside them.
Its teeth are curved and jagged, perfect for slicing through *turtle shells*.
Young tiger sharks have dark *stripes* along their bodies, which fade with age.
They can grow over *five meters* long.
After great whites, tiger sharks are responsible for the most recorded *bites* on humans.
But attacks are still extremely *rare*.
In Hawaii, scientists found tiger sharks arrive at certain islands every year, just when young *albatrosses* take their first flights.
The young birds that land in the water don't always make it.
Clever, curious, and not picky at all.
