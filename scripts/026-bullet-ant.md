title: The Most Painful Sting In The World
subject: bullet ant | Paraponera clavata

This ant's sting feels like being *shot*.
That's why it's called the *bullet ant*.
It lives in the rainforests of Central and South America, and it's almost *three centimeters* long.
An entomologist named *Justin Schmidt* rated insect stings from one to four.
The bullet ant scored the highest possible: a *four plus*.
He described it as walking over flaming charcoal with a *nail* in your heel.
And the pain doesn't fade quickly. It comes in waves for up to *24 hours*.
Now imagine choosing to feel that hundreds of times.
In Brazil, the *Sateré-Mawé* people have a coming-of-age ritual.
Boys wear gloves woven with *hundreds* of bullet ants, stingers pointed inward.
They wear them for about *ten minutes*, and must repeat the ritual many times over months.
To them, it's a test of *courage*.
To the rest of us, it's a very good reason to watch where we put our hands.
