title: The Warthog Is Tougher Than It Looks
subject: common warthog | Phacochoerus africanus
mood: curious
tags: warthog, pumbaa, africa, wild pigs, funny animals
---
The warthog might look silly, but it's *tougher* than it looks.
Those "warts" aren't actually warts.
They're thick pads of skin that protect its face during *fights*.
Its tusks can grow over *25 centimeters*, and they're razor sharp.
Warthogs often kneel on their front legs to graze, with hard pads on their knees.
When chased, they run with their tails straight up, like a little *antenna*.
They sleep in burrows dug by aardvarks, and back in *rear first*.
That way, if a predator comes, their tusks face the entrance.
They've been known to charge at *lions* and even *leopards* to defend their young.
They also let *mongooses* pick ticks off their bodies. [banded mongoose | Mungos mungo]
Warthogs can run at around *50 kilometers per hour*.
And yes, *Pumbaa* from The Lion King was a warthog.
Hakuna matata.
