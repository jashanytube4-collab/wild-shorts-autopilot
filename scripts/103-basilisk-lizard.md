title: The Lizard That Runs On Water
subject: common basilisk | Basiliscus basiliscus
mood: curious
tags: basilisk lizard, jesus lizard, runs on water, central america, reptiles
---
This lizard can *run on water*.
It's called the *basilisk*, but locals nickname it the *Jesus Christ lizard*.
When a predator gets close, it drops into a river and sprints across the surface on its back legs.
Its feet have flaps of skin along the toes that spread out, increasing their surface area.
Each step slaps the water and pushes down, creating a pocket of *air*.
Before that pocket collapses, the lizard pulls its foot out and takes the next step.
It runs at around *1.5 meters per second*, holding its body up like a tiny *dinosaur*.
Young, lighter basilisks are even better at it than adults.
When it finally slows down and sinks, it just *swims*.
And it can stay underwater for up to around *30 minutes*.
For a human to do the same, scientists calculated we'd need to run at over *100 kilometers per hour*.
