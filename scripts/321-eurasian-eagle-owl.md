This owl is big enough to hunt *foxes* and young deer. [red fox | Vulpes vulpes]
Meet the *Eurasian eagle-owl*, one of the biggest owls in the world.
Its wings can stretch around *1.8 meters*.
And its bright orange eyes are huge.
It hunts mostly rabbits, rats, and birds.
But it can also catch other owls, hawks, and even *herons*.
Eagle-owls can live for decades.
One in captivity reportedly lived to around *68 years*.
Their deep hooting can be heard over *four kilometers* away on a quiet night.
And those "horns" on their head are just feather tufts.
In Finland, a famous eagle-owl named *Bubi* landed on the pitch during a football match in 2007.
The game was paused, and the Finnish national team was nicknamed "the Eagle-Owls" after that.
A silent giant of the night.
