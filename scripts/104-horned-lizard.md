title: The Lizard That Shoots Blood From Its Eyes
subject: Texas horned lizard | Phrynosoma cornutum
mood: curious

This lizard defends itself by shooting *blood from its eyes*.
Meet the *horned lizard*.
When a coyote or dog attacks, it raises the blood pressure in its head until tiny vessels near its eyes *burst*.
A stream of blood can shoot up to around *1.5 meters*.
And the blood tastes *terrible* to dogs, coyotes and foxes. [coyote | Canis latrans]
Scientists think it comes from chemicals in the ants it eats.
Predators shake their heads and back away.
Strangely, it doesn't use this against hawks, which don't seem bothered.
It mostly eats *harvester ants*, sometimes thousands in a day.
And it can puff itself up with air, making it harder to swallow.
Its back is covered in sharp spines, and its head has a crown of *horns*.
It's the state reptile of *Texas*, where some people call it a horny toad.
But it's not a toad at all. Just a very creative *lizard*.
