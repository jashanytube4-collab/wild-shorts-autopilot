This cat has the longest fangs, for its size, of any living cat.
Meet the *clouded leopard*, from the forests of Southeast Asia.
Its canine teeth can be around *four centimeters* long, in a skull no bigger than a dog's.
That's why some call it a *modern saber-tooth*.
It can open its jaws incredibly wide, almost *90 degrees*.
Clouded leopards are amazing climbers.
Their back ankles can rotate, letting them climb down trees *head first*.
They can even hang upside down from branches by their back feet.
Their long tail, almost as long as their body, helps them balance.
Their coat is covered in big, cloud-shaped patches, which help them vanish in the shadows of the forest.
They hunt monkeys, deer, wild pigs and birds. [pig-tailed macaque | Macaca nemestrina]
They're so secretive that scientists know very little about them in the wild.
A tree-climbing ghost with fangs.
