Hyenas are *not* the scavengers you think they are.
Spotted hyenas hunt most of their own food, around *60 to 95 percent*, depending on where they live.
In fact, *lions* often steal from them, not the other way around. [lion | Panthera leo]
They live in large groups called *clans*, which can have over *80* members.
And the clan is run by the *females*.
Even the highest-ranking male is usually below the lowest-ranking female.
Their bite can crush bones to get at the *marrow*, and their stomachs can digest bone, skin and teeth.
Their famous "laugh" isn't happiness.
It's a sound they make when they're stressed or *frustrated*, often while fighting over food.
And despite looking like dogs, hyenas are actually more closely related to *cats*.
Smart, social, and seriously misunderstood.
