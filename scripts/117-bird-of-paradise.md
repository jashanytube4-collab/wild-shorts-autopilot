This bird turns into a *smiley face* to find a mate.
Meet the *superb bird-of-paradise* from New Guinea.
At first, the male looks like an ordinary black bird.
But when a female arrives, he raises a cape of feathers around his head into a giant black *oval*.
In the middle, a bright blue band of feathers looks like a *smiling face*.
Then he hops around her in circles, snapping his feet like a *dancer*.
His black feathers are among the *blackest* in nature.
They absorb up to around *99.95 percent* of light, making the blue look even brighter.
Scientists call it *super black*.
And females are very picky.
They may reject many males before choosing one.
In the past, European traders received skins with the feet removed, and some believed these birds never landed, floating forever in *paradise*.
That's how they got their name.
