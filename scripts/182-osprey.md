title: The Bird That Dives Feet First For Fish
subject: osprey | Pandion haliaetus
mood: epic
tags: osprey, fish hawk, birds of prey, fishing, animal facts
---
This bird catches fish by diving *feet first* into the water.
Meet the *osprey*, also called the *fish hawk*.
Almost its entire diet is *live fish*.
It hovers high above the water, spots a fish, then plunges down, reaching out with its talons at the last second.
Its feet have rough, spiny pads, perfect for holding slippery fish.
And it can reverse one of its outer toes, so two toes point forward and two point *backward*.
After the catch, it turns the fish to face *forward*, so it's more aerodynamic as it flies.
Ospreys can close their nostrils when they dive.
They're found on every continent except *Antarctica*.
Some ospreys migrate thousands of kilometers between Europe and Africa every year.
One famous female in Scotland, nicknamed *Lady*, raised around 50 chicks and lived to around *29 years*.
An expert fisher with built-in fishing hooks.
