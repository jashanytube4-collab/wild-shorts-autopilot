This fish can see above and below the water *at the same time*.
Meet the *four-eyed fish*.
It doesn't actually have four eyes.
It has two, but each eye is split into *two halves*.
The top half sees above the water. The bottom half sees *below*.
It swims at the surface, with its eyes half in and half out, like a living *periscope*.
That lets it watch for birds above, while hunting insects and small fish below.
Its lenses are shaped differently on top and bottom, to focus light in air and water.
Four-eyed fish live in mangroves and river mouths in South America.
When the tide goes out, they sometimes leave the water and flop onto the mud.
And males can only mate with females from one side, left or right, depending on their anatomy.
A fish with the best of both worlds.
