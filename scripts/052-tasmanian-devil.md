For its size, this animal has one of the strongest bites of any mammal.
Meet the *Tasmanian devil*.
It's only about the size of a small dog, but its jaws can crunch through *bone*.
It eats almost everything: meat, fur, organs, and *bones*.
Early settlers named it the *devil* after hearing its bloodcurdling *screams* in the night.
When it gets stressed, its ears flush *bright red*.
It's a *marsupial*, so babies are raised in a pouch.
A mother gives birth to as many as *50* tiny babies, each about the size of a *grain of rice*.
But she only has *four* teats.
Only the fastest babies survive.
Sadly, devils face a strange threat: a contagious *face cancer* that spreads through biting.
It's wiped out the majority of wild devils.
Today, devils are being bred in sanctuaries and even returned to mainland Australia, after *3,000 years* away.
