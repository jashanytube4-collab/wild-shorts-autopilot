title: The Insect That Lives Only A Day
subject: mayfly | Ephemeroptera
mood: curious
tags: mayfly, insects, short life, rivers, nature
---
Some adult mayflies live for less than a *day*.
And some live for just *minutes*.
Mayflies spend most of their lives underwater as *nymphs*, sometimes for a year or more.
Then they emerge, all at once, in giant swarms.
Adult mayflies don't eat. Most don't even have a working *mouth*.
Their only purpose is to mate and lay eggs.
Mayflies are the only insects that molt *twice* after getting wings.
Their swarms can be so huge that they show up on *weather radar*.
In some places, piles of dead mayflies have covered bridges, cars, and roads.
Mayflies need clean water to survive.
So scientists use them as signs of healthy rivers.
Their scientific name, *Ephemeroptera*, comes from a Greek word meaning *short-lived*.
Mayflies have existed for over *300 million years*.
A whole life, in a single day.
