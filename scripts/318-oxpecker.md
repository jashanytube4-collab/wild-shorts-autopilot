title: The Bird That Drinks Blood From Rhinos
subject: red-billed oxpecker | Buphagus erythrorynchus
mood: curious
tags: oxpecker, rhino, africa, birds, symbiosis
---
This bird eats ticks off rhinos, and it also drinks their *blood*.
Meet the *oxpecker*.
It spends almost its entire day riding on the backs of large animals.
Rhinos, giraffes, buffalo, zebras, and impalas. [white rhinoceros | Ceratotherium simum]
It picks off ticks and other parasites, helping keep them clean.
But it's not always so helpful.
Oxpeckers also peck at wounds to keep them open, so they can drink *blood*.
So scientists still debate whether they're friends or *freeloaders*.
They also make a loud hissing alarm when danger comes.
In Swahili, black rhinos are sometimes called *askari wa kifaru*, meaning "the rhino's guard."
In one study, rhinos with oxpeckers spotted approaching humans much more often than rhinos without them.
That may help protect rhinos from *poachers*.
Oxpeckers even sleep on their hosts at night.
A bodyguard, a cleaner, and a little bit of a vampire.
