title: Koalas Sleep 20 Hours A Day, Here's Why

Koalas sleep up to *20 hours* a day.
And it's not because they're lazy.
They eat almost nothing but *eucalyptus leaves*, which are tough, low in energy, and *toxic* to most animals.
Their bodies spend so much energy digesting them that there's little left for anything else.
Special gut bacteria help them break down the poison.
Baby koalas get those bacteria in a strange way: by eating a special kind of their mother's *poop*, called *pap*.
Koalas aren't bears, by the way. They're *marsupials*, carrying their babies in a pouch.
Their fingerprints are so similar to human fingerprints that they could, in theory, confuse a *crime scene*.
Males make a surprisingly deep *bellow*, like a snoring pig, thanks to an extra pair of vocal cords.
Some people even say *koala* means "no drink" in an Aboriginal language.
It's a cute story, even if experts aren't sure it's true.
