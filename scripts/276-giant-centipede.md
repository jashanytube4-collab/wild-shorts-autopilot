This centipede can catch *bats* in mid-air.
Meet the *Amazonian giant centipede*, one of the largest centipedes in the world.
It can grow over *30 centimeters* long.
In a cave in Venezuela, scientists watched these centipedes climb the walls.
They hung upside down from the ceiling, and grabbed passing *bats* out of the air. [bat | Chiroptera]
They also eat mice, lizards, frogs, and tarantulas.
Its first pair of legs have turned into venomous *fangs*, called forcipules.
The venom causes intense pain, swelling and fever in humans.
And despite the name, centipedes don't have *100* legs.
This one has around 21 to 23 pairs.
Females guard their eggs, curling around them for weeks.
They're fast, aggressive, and hard to hold.
A real-life monster in the dark.
