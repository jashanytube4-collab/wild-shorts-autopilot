Crows can remember your face for *years*, and they tell their friends about you.
At the University of Washington, researchers wore a *mask* while trapping a few wild crows.
Years later, crows on campus still *screamed* at anyone wearing that mask.
Even crows that had never been trapped joined in.
They had *learned the face* from the others.
New Caledonian crows go even further. [New Caledonian crow | Corvus moneduloides]
They make *tools*, snapping twigs into hooks to fish insects out of tree bark. [New Caledonian crow | Corvus moneduloides]
A crow named *Betty* once bent a straight wire into a hook to lift a bucket of food.
Other crows have solved puzzles requiring *eight steps* in a row.
Show them a treat floating in a tube of water, and they drop in *stones* to raise it, like a *seven year old child*.
For their body size, crow brains are about as big as a *chimpanzee's*.
So be nice to the crows. They're *watching*.
