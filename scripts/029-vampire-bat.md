This animal drinks *blood*, and only blood, every single night.
Meet the *common vampire bat*.
It finds sleeping animals like cows and horses using special *heat sensors* on its nose. [cattle | Bos taurus]
It even uses them to spot where the blood runs closest to the skin.
Then it makes a tiny cut with razor-sharp teeth, and *laps up* the blood with its tongue.
Its saliva contains a chemical that stops blood from *clotting*.
Scientists even named one of those chemicals *draculin*.
Vampire bats can also *walk*, and even run, on the ground using their wings like arms.
If a bat goes two nights without feeding, it can *starve*.
So here's the touching part: bats that fed well will *share*, coughing up blood for hungry friends.
And they remember who helped them in the past.
Vampire bats have *best friends*.
