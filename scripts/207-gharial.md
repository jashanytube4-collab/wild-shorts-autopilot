title: The Crocodile With The Weirdest Snout
subject: gharial | Gavialis gangeticus
mood: curious

This crocodile has a snout that looks like a *sword*.
Meet the *gharial*, from the rivers of *India* and *Nepal*.
Its long, thin snout is lined with over *100* sharp, interlocking teeth.
It's perfect for snapping sideways at fast-moving *fish*.
Gharials mainly eat fish, and they aren't known to attack humans.
Males have a strange bulb on the tip of their snout, called a *ghara*, named after a type of Indian pot.
It helps them make loud buzzing sounds and blow *bubbles* to attract females.
Gharials can grow over *six meters* long, among the longest of all crocodilians.
But on land, their legs are weak, so they mostly slide on their bellies.
In the 1970s, there were only around *200* left in the wild.
Thanks to conservation, numbers have recovered, but they're still *critically endangered*.
In India, one male was photographed carrying *dozens* of babies on his back.
