Male elephant seals fight brutal battles for control of the beach.
The *northern elephant seal* can weigh over *2,000 kilograms*.
And that huge floppy nose isn't just for show.
It's called a *proboscis*, and it amplifies their roar into a deep, rumbling *bellow*.
During mating season, rival males rear up and slam into each other, biting and bleeding.
The winner, called the *beachmaster*, guards a group of females that can reach dozens, sometimes over a *hundred*.
Most males never get to mate at all.
Out at sea, elephant seals are diving machines.
They can dive deeper than *1,500 meters*, and stay underwater for *over an hour*.
They spend most of the year at sea, coming to land only to breed and molt.
And they sleep in short naps while sinking slowly, sometimes for just *two hours a day*.
Huge, loud, and built for the deep.
