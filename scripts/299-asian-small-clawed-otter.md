title: Otters Juggle Rocks For Fun
subject: Asian small-clawed otter | Aonyx cinereus
mood: curious

Otters *juggle rocks*, and scientists aren't completely sure why.
*Asian small-clawed otters*, the smallest otters in the world, toss pebbles between their paws and chest.
They roll them around, and catch them in their *mouths*.
One study found they may juggle more when they're *hungry*.
It might also help keep their skills sharp for opening shellfish.
Asian small-clawed otters live in family groups of up to around *15* otters.
They're very vocal, with at least *12* different calls.
Their claws are tiny, and their paws work almost like *hands*, perfect for catching crabs and snails.
They mate for life, and both parents help raise the pups.
In Singapore, wild otters became famous for roaming through the city, even *wandering* through malls and parks.
Sadly, they're threatened by habitat loss and the pet trade.
Small, smart, and endlessly playful.
