This moth has a *skull* on its back.
Meet the *death's-head hawkmoth*.
The pattern on its back looks just like a human *skull*.
For centuries, people saw it as an omen of death.
It appeared on the famous poster for the movie *The Silence of the Lambs*.
But this moth is even stranger than it looks.
When disturbed, it *squeaks*, loudly, by pushing air through its mouth.
And it raids *beehives* to steal honey. [western honey bee | Apis mellifera]
It may mimic the smell of bees, letting it sneak inside without being attacked.
It's also thought to be partly protected from stings by thick skin.
The moth's scientific name, *Acherontia atropos*, comes from Greek mythology: the river of pain in the underworld, and the Fate who cuts the thread of life.
Its caterpillars are huge, bright, and often found on potato plants.
A moth with a creepy reputation.
