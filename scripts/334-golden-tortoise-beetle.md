title: The Beetle That Changes Color Like Gold
subject: golden tortoise beetle | Charidotella sexpunctata
mood: curious
tags: golden tortoise beetle, beetles, insects, color change, gold
---
This beetle looks like a tiny drop of *gold*.
Meet the *golden tortoise beetle*.
It's only around *five millimeters* long, and shiny like *metal*.
But when it's disturbed, something amazing happens.
It can change color, from bright gold to a dull *reddish brown* with black spots.
How? Its shell has tiny layers filled with liquid.
When the beetle controls the moisture inside, the way light reflects changes.
Scientists think the change might help scare predators, making it look like a toxic *ladybug*.
Unfortunately, the gold color fades when the beetle *dies*.
So collectors can't keep it.
Its larvae have an even stranger defense.
They carry a shield on their backs made of their own *poop* and shed skins.
A living jewel, with a very weird childhood.
