The real roadrunner eats *rattlesnakes*.
Meet the *greater roadrunner*, from the deserts of the southwestern US and Mexico.
It can run at around *30 kilometers per hour*.
And it's fast enough to catch lizards, mice, scorpions, and even *rattlesnakes*. [western diamondback rattlesnake | Crotalus atrox]
Sometimes two roadrunners team up against a snake.
It grabs the snake by its head, then slams it against a rock again and again.
Then it swallows it whole, sometimes walking around with the tail hanging out of its mouth for a while.
Roadrunners can fly, but they prefer to run.
To save energy on cold desert nights, they lower their body temperature.
In the morning, they turn their backs to the sun, exposing dark skin to *warm up*.
They don't say "meep meep" like the cartoon.
They make cooing and clattering sounds.
And no, coyotes don't use rocket skates.
