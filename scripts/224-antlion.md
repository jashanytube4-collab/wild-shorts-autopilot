This insect builds a *death trap* in the sand.
Meet the *antlion* larva.
It digs a cone-shaped pit in loose, dry sand, then buries itself at the bottom.
Only its giant, curved jaws stick out.
When an ant wanders to the edge, the loose sand slips beneath its feet. [ant | Formicidae]
The ant starts sliding down.
If it tries to climb out, the antlion flicks sand at it, causing tiny *landslides*.
At the bottom, the jaws grab the prey and inject chemicals that turn its insides to *liquid*.
Then the antlion sucks it dry and flicks the empty body out of the pit.
In America, they're nicknamed *doodlebugs*, because of the wiggly trails they leave in the sand.
Antlions can survive for months without eating.
Eventually, they turn into delicate, flying insects that look like *dragonflies*.
From a monster in a pit, to a fairy in the air.
