title: Australia's Deadliest Spider
subject: Sydney funnel-web spider | Atrax robustus
mood: danger
tags: funnel web spider, australia, venomous spiders, sydney, spiders
---
This spider's fangs can pierce a *fingernail*.
Meet the *Sydney funnel-web*, often called the deadliest spider in Australia.
Its fangs are so strong they can bite through a *toenail* or soft shoes.
And it's aggressive, rearing back to show off dripping venom before it strikes.
Here's the scary part: its venom is especially dangerous to *primates*, including humans.
Cats and dogs can shrug it off.
A bad bite can kill a person within *hours*.
But since an antivenom was introduced in *1981*, nobody has died from one.
How do they make that antivenom? People *milk* the spiders.
The Australian Reptile Park even asks the public to catch funnel-webs and drop them off.
Males are the most dangerous, and they roam in summer looking for mates.
That's when they wander into houses, shoes and *swimming pools*.
They can even survive underwater for hours, trapping a bubble of *air*.
