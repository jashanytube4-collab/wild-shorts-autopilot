title: The Fish Divers Fear The Most
subject: titan triggerfish | Balistoides viridescens
mood: danger
tags: titan triggerfish, coral reef, divers, ocean, fish
---
Many scuba divers fear this fish more than *sharks*.
Meet the *titan triggerfish*.
It can grow up to around *75 centimeters* long, with strong jaws and sharp teeth.
During breeding season, it guards its nest fiercely.
Its territory is shaped like a cone, rising up from the nest.
And anything that enters, including divers, gets *attacked*.
It rushes in and bites, sometimes drawing blood.
Divers are advised to swim away horizontally, not upward, to leave the cone faster.
Triggerfish can crush shells, sea urchins and coral with their powerful jaws.
They even flip over sea urchins, by blowing jets of water at them, to reach their soft undersides.
Their name comes from a spine on their back that locks upright like a *trigger*.
They use it to wedge themselves into crevices.
Small fish, big attitude.
