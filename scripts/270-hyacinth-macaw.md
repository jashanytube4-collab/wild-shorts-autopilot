This is the biggest flying parrot in the world.
Meet the *hyacinth macaw*.
It can grow around *one meter* long, from beak to tail.
And it's a stunning, deep cobalt *blue*.
Its beak is so powerful it can crack open coconuts, and some of the hardest nuts in the world.
Hyacinth macaws mainly eat the nuts of palm trees.
Some nuts are too hard even for them.
So they wait until the nuts have passed through *cattle*, which soften them up.
Hyacinth macaws mate for life, and are almost always seen in *pairs*.
They nest in tree hollows, often in one type of tree called the manduvi.
In the 1980s, thousands were taken from the wild for the pet trade.
Today, they're protected, and conservation programs in Brazil have helped numbers grow.
A true blue giant of the parrot world.
