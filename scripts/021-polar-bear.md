Polar bears aren't actually white.
Their skin underneath is *jet black*, perfect for soaking up the sun's heat.
And their fur? Each hair is *transparent* and hollow.
It only looks white because it scatters light, just like *snow* does.
The *polar bear* is the largest land carnivore on Earth.
Big males can weigh over *600 kilograms*.
They can smell a seal from more than a *kilometer* away, even under the ice. [ringed seal | Pusa hispida]
Then they wait by a breathing hole, sometimes for *hours*, for the seal to come up for air.
They're also powerful swimmers.
One female was tracked swimming for *nine days straight*, covering almost *700 kilometers*.
Their paws can be *30 centimeters* across, working like snowshoes on ice and paddles in water.
But as the sea ice melts, they're losing the platform they hunt from.
And without ice, there are no *seals* to catch.
