This animal lives higher up than almost any other large mammal.
Meet the *yak*, from the Tibetan Plateau, the "roof of the world."
Yaks live at heights of over *4,000 meters*, where the air is thin and freezing.
They have huge lungs and hearts, and their blood carries oxygen very efficiently.
Their thick, shaggy coats keep them warm in temperatures down to around *minus 40*.
In fact, they struggle in warm weather, above around *15 degrees Celsius*.
Their scientific name means *grunting ox*, because they grunt instead of mooing.
For people in the Himalayas, yaks are everything.
They carry heavy loads through high mountain passes.
Their milk is made into butter and cheese.
Their hair becomes clothing and tents, and their dung is used as fuel.
Wild yaks are even bigger, and are now *vulnerable*. [wild yak | Bos mutus]
A shaggy giant of the high mountains.
