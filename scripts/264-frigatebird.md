This bird can *sleep while flying*.
Meet the *frigatebird*.
It can stay in the air for weeks, or even months, without landing.
In 2016, scientists put tiny brain sensors on frigatebirds.
They found the birds slept in short bursts, mostly during flight, sometimes with *half their brain* at a time.
They slept less than an *hour* a day in total.
Frigatebirds can't land on water, because their feathers aren't waterproof.
So they catch fish by swooping down and snatching them from the surface.
They also steal food from other seabirds, chasing them until they *drop* their catch.
That's why they're sometimes called *pirate birds*.
Males have a bright red throat pouch, which they inflate like a giant *balloon* to attract females.
Some ride thermals more than *4,000 meters* high.
A bird built to live in the sky.
