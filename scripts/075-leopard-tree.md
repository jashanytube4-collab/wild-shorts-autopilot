This cat can drag a kill heavier than *itself* up into a tree.
Meet the *leopard*.
It's the smallest of the big cats that roar, yet pound for pound, it's one of the *strongest*.
After a hunt, it hauls its prey high into the branches.
Up there, the meal is safe from *lions* and *hyenas*. [spotted hyena | Crocuta crocuta]
Leopards are the ultimate *survivors*.
They have the widest range of any big cat, from African deserts to Russian forests.
They'll eat almost anything: antelopes, monkeys, fish, birds, and even *dung beetles*.
Some even live on the edges of cities like *Mumbai*.
Their spots, called *rosettes*, help them vanish into the shadows.
And black panthers? Many of them are just leopards with *extra dark* pigment.
In the right light, you can still see the spots.
