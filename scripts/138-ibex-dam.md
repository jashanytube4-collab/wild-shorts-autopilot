title: The Goats That Climb Vertical Dam Walls
subject: Alpine ibex | Capra ibex

These goats climb walls that look *impossible*.
In Italy, Alpine ibex have been filmed scaling the near-vertical wall of the *Cingino Dam*, around *50 meters* high.
Why would they risk it?
For *salt*.
The stones of the dam are crusted with minerals, and the ibex lick them off.
Their hooves are perfectly built for climbing.
The outer edge is hard, while the center is soft and rubbery, gripping rock like a *climbing shoe*.
They can balance on ledges just a few centimeters wide.
Up there, predators like *wolves* can't follow. [gray wolf | Canis lupus]
Males have huge curved horns that can grow over *a meter* long.
They use them in fierce head-butting contests.
In the 1800s, Alpine ibex were nearly wiped out, with only around *100* left in one area of Italy.
Today, thanks to protection, there are tens of thousands across the *Alps*.
