Some fish can *fly* for hundreds of meters.
These are *flying fish*.
When chased by tuna or dolphins, they swim up to the surface at around *60 kilometers per hour*. [yellowfin tuna | Thunnus albacares]
Then they burst out of the water and spread their huge fins like *wings*.
Their tails keep beating against the surface, giving them extra push.
They can glide up to around *50 meters* in one flight.
And by touching down and flicking their tails again, they can chain glides together for up to *400 meters*.
One flying fish was filmed staying in the air for *45 seconds*.
They can reach heights of up to around *six meters*.
At night, they're sometimes attracted to the lights of ships, and land right on the *deck*.
In Barbados, the flying fish is the national symbol, and appears on the country's *coins*.
Not quite a bird, but not just a fish either.
