Can you spot the fish in this picture?
Most people can't, and that's what makes it so dangerous.
This is the *stonefish*, the most venomous fish in the world.
It looks exactly like a lumpy, algae-covered *rock*.
It lies still on the seafloor, and when a small fish swims past, it swallows it in a fraction of a second.
But the real danger is on its back.
It has *13 spines*, each one loaded with venom.
Step on one, and the spine injects venom under the pressure of your own *weight*.
People who've been stung say the pain is the *worst* they've ever felt.
Some even beg for the injured limb to be *amputated*.
Hot water helps, because heat breaks down some of the venom.
And it can survive *out of water* for hours, waiting in rock pools at low tide.
So in the tropics, always wear *shoes* on the reef.
