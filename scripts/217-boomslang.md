This snake's venom can make you bleed from *everywhere*.
Meet the *boomslang*, a tree snake from Africa.
It has huge eyes and excellent vision, and it's often bright green.
Its name means *tree snake* in Afrikaans.
Its fangs are at the *back* of its mouth, so it needs to open its jaws wide to bite.
Its venom works slowly, which makes it even more dangerous.
Victims might feel fine for hours.
But meanwhile, the venom is stopping their blood from clotting, causing internal *bleeding*.
In 1957, a famous snake scientist named *Karl Schmidt* was bitten by a young boomslang.
Thinking it was too small to be deadly, he kept notes about his symptoms.
He died the next day.
Thankfully, boomslangs are *shy*, and bites are rare.
An antivenom now exists.
Never underestimate a small snake.
