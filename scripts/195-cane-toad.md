title: The Toad That Took Over Australia
subject: cane toad | Rhinella marina
mood: danger
tags: cane toad, invasive species, australia, poisonous animals, toads
---
In 1935, Australia brought in around *100* toads to eat beetles.
Today, there may be *hundreds of millions*.
These are *cane toads*.
They were released to control beetles destroying sugarcane crops.
But they didn't really eat the beetles.
Instead, they ate almost everything else, and *spread* across the country.
Cane toads are poisonous, with toxin glands on their shoulders.
Animals that try to eat them, like snakes, crocodiles and native *quolls*, often *die*. [northern quoll | Dasyurus hallucatus]
A single female can lay tens of thousands of eggs at once.
They're still spreading west, at around *50 kilometers* a year.
But some native animals are learning.
Scientists train quolls to avoid toads by feeding them toad meat laced with a nausea-inducing chemical.
Some crows have even learned to flip toads over and eat them from the belly, avoiding the poison.
A mistake that Australia is still paying for.
