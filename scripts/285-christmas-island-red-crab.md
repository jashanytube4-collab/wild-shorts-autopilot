title: The Red Crab Migration
subject: Christmas Island red crab | Gecarcoidea natalis
mood: epic
tags: red crab migration, christmas island, crabs, migration, australia
---
Every year, *tens of millions* of red crabs march across an island to the sea.
This is *Christmas Island*, in the Indian Ocean.
When the rainy season begins, the red crabs leave the forest and head for the *coast*.
They cover roads, houses, beaches and cliffs, turning the island *red*.
The island closes some roads, and builds crab *bridges* and tunnels so they can cross safely.
When they reach the sea, the males dig burrows, and the crabs mate.
Then the females release their eggs into the ocean, timed with the *moon* and tides.
A few weeks later, millions of baby crabs emerge from the sea and march back into the forest.
In some years, almost all of the babies are eaten by fish and whale sharks. [whale shark | Rhincodon typus]
But in good years, the beaches are covered with tiny crabs.
The red crab migration is one of the great wonders of the natural world.
