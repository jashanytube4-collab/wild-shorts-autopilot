Not all black bears are *black*.
The *American black bear* can be brown, cinnamon, blond, bluish grey, or even *white*.
In British Columbia, there's a rare white black bear called the *spirit bear*. [Kermode bear | Ursus americanus kermodei]
It's caused by a gene that both parents must carry.
Local Indigenous people kept it a secret for generations, to protect it.
Black bears are excellent *climbers*, scrambling up trees to escape danger or find food.
Their sense of smell is among the best of any animal, much better than a bloodhound's.
They eat mostly plants, berries, nuts and insects.
And in winter, pregnant females give birth while *hibernating*.
The cubs are born tiny, around the size of a squirrel, and nurse while the mother sleeps.
Black bears are also clever, and some have learned to open car doors and *coolers*.
