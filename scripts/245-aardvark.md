This animal can dig faster than a team of *men with shovels*.
Meet the *aardvark*.
Its name means *earth pig* in Afrikaans, but it's not related to pigs at all.
Its closest living relatives include *elephants*, manatees and elephant shrews.
With its powerful claws, it can dig a hole big enough to hide in within *minutes*.
It eats ants and termites, slurping them up with a sticky tongue up to around *30 centimeters* long.
It can eat around *50,000* insects in a night.
It can close its nostrils to keep out dust and insects while digging.
Aardvarks dig huge burrows, which are later used by many other animals.
Warthogs, hyenas, porcupines, snakes, and even birds move in.
Without aardvarks, many animals would have nowhere to *live*.
Aardvarks are also the only living species in their entire *order* of mammals.
First in the dictionary, and first in digging.
