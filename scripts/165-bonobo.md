Chimpanzees go to war. This ape makes *peace*.
Meet the *bonobo*, found only in the rainforests of the *Congo*.
Bonobos share about *98 percent* of our DNA, just like chimpanzees.
But their societies are completely different.
Bonobo groups are led by *females*, who team up to keep aggressive males in check.
When tension rises, bonobos often calm things down with hugs, play, and grooming.
Scientists have even seen them *share food* with complete strangers.
Bonobos also seem to show *empathy*, comforting those who are upset.
A famous bonobo named *Kanzi* learned to communicate using hundreds of symbols on a keyboard.
He could also make stone tools and *light fires*.
Unlike chimps, wild bonobos have never been seen killing each other.
Sadly, they're *endangered*, threatened by hunting and forest loss.
If chimps show our dark side, bonobos might show our *better* one.
