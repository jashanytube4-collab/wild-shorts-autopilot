This beautiful shell hides one of the deadliest animals in the ocean.
It's a *cone snail*.
Slow, pretty, and easy to pick up.
That's exactly the problem.
Inside, it carries a hollow tooth shaped like a *harpoon*, loaded with venom.
It can fire it in any direction, even through a *wetsuit* or a pocket.
The geographic cone snail's venom contains hundreds of different toxins, and it has killed *dozens of people*.
Some victims barely feel the sting at first.
Then come numbness, blurred vision, and *paralysis*.
There's *no antivenom*.
To catch fish, some cone snails release a cloud of *insulin* into the water. [geographic cone snail | Conus geographus]
The fish's blood sugar crashes, it goes limp, and the snail swallows it *whole*.
Strangely, scientists use cone snail venom to create *painkillers*.
One drug made from it is said to be far stronger than *morphine*.
