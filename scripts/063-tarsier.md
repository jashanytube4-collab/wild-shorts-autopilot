Each of this animal's eyes is bigger than its *brain*.
Meet the *tarsier*, a tiny primate about the size of your *hand*.
Its eyes are so big that they can't move in their sockets.
So instead, it turns its head almost *180 degrees* in each direction, like an *owl*.
It's built for night hunting, catching insects, lizards and even *birds* in mid-air.
Its long back legs let it leap many times its own body length between trees.
And it can communicate using sounds so high-pitched humans *can't hear them*.
Tarsiers are extremely sensitive to stress.
In captivity, some have been reported to harm themselves by banging their heads when frightened.
That's why the Philippines protects them in *sanctuaries*, with no flash photography allowed.
Tarsiers have been around, in some form, for around *45 million years*.
Tiny, ancient, and always looking *surprised*.
