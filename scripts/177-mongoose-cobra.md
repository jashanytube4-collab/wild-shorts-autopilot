This small animal can beat a *cobra*. [spectacled cobra | Naja naja]
Meet the *mongoose*.
It's only about the size of a cat, but it's one of the few animals that fights venomous snakes and *wins*.
How? First, speed.
A mongoose dodges the cobra's strikes again and again, tiring it out.
Its thick fur can help protect against bites.
And it has a special trick in its body.
Mongooses have changed proteins on their *muscles* and nerves.
These make it much harder for cobra venom to latch on.
So even if it's bitten, it's far more likely to *survive*.
When the snake is tired, the mongoose strikes, biting the back of its head.
Rudyard Kipling's story *Rikki-Tikki-Tavi*, about a mongoose protecting a family from cobras in India, made them famous.
Small, brave, and built for battle.
