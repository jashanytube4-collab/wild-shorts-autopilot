title: How Geckos Walk On Ceilings

Geckos can walk across ceilings, and they don't use glue.
How? It's all in their *toes*.
Each toe is covered in millions of tiny hairs called *setae*.
And each hair splits into hundreds of even tinier tips.
They're so small that they get extremely close to the surface.
That lets them use weak forces between molecules, called *van der Waals forces*.
Add up billions of them, and a gecko can hang by a single *toe*.
And here's the clever part: they can turn the grip on and off in a split second by changing the angle of their toes.
So they can run up glass at full speed.
Scientists have copied this to make *gecko tape*, and even climbing gloves for humans.
The *tokay gecko* is one of the biggest geckos, and famous for its loud call, "To-kay! To-kay!"
It can also bite hard, and doesn't let go easily.
