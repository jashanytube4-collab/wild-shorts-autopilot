title: The Bird That Dives At 85 Kilometers Per Hour
subject: northern gannet | Morus bassanus
mood: epic
tags: gannet, diving birds, seabirds, ocean, fishing
---
This bird hits the water at around *85 kilometers per hour*.
Meet the *northern gannet*.
It spots fish from high above the sea, then folds its wings and dives like a *missile*.
Just before impact, it pulls its wings back, turning into a sleek *arrow*.
It has no external nostrils, so water can't rush up its nose.
Air sacs under its skin cushion the impact, like built-in *bubble wrap*.
Its eyes face forward, giving it good depth perception for judging dives.
Gannets can chase fish underwater, swimming down to around *20 meters*.
They nest in huge, noisy colonies on cliffs and rocky islands.
One colony on Bass Rock in Scotland had over *150,000* gannets.
The phrase "eating like a gannet" means eating greedily.
And gannets can swallow fish whole, sometimes several at a time.
A seabird that dives like a rocket.
