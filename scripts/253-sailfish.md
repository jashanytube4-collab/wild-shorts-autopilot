title: One Of The Fastest Fish In The Ocean
subject: Indo-Pacific sailfish | Istiophorus platypterus
mood: epic
tags: sailfish, fastest fish, ocean, hunting, fish
---
The *sailfish* is often called the fastest fish in the ocean.
Some estimates put it at over *100 kilometers per hour*, though newer research suggests that may be exaggerated.
Either way, it's incredibly fast.
It has a huge *sail* on its back, which it can raise or fold down.
When hunting, sailfish raise their sails to look bigger, and herd fish into tight balls. [Pacific sardine | Sardinops sagax]
Then they take turns slashing through the school with their long, sharp bills.
Scientists found their bills are covered in tiny *teeth* that wear down over time.
Sailfish can also flash *colors*, turning stripes on and off, possibly to signal to each other.
They grow fast, and can reach over *three meters* long.
They're a favorite of sport fishermen around the world.
But many are now released after being caught.
Sail up, speed on.
