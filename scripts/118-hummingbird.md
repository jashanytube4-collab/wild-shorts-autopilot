title: The Bird With The Fastest Heartbeat
subject: ruby-throated hummingbird | Archilochus colubris
mood: curious
tags: hummingbird, birds, fastest heartbeat, flying, animal facts
---
A hummingbird's heart can beat over *1,200 times* a minute.
That's around *20 beats every second*.
And their wings flap up to around *80 times* a second.
That's why they *hum*.
Hummingbirds are the only birds that can fly *backwards*.
They can also hover, fly sideways, and even upside down for a moment.
To power all that, they need a huge amount of energy.
They visit hundreds of flowers a day, drinking nectar with a tongue that works like a tiny *pump*.
At night, many go into a state called *torpor*, slowing their heart and dropping their body temperature to save energy.
It's almost like a nightly *hibernation*.
The *ruby-throated hummingbird* weighs just a few grams.
Yet every year, many fly nonstop across the *Gulf of Mexico*, around *800 kilometers*.
They even remember which flowers they've already emptied, and when they'll refill.
Tiny, but absolutely unstoppable.
