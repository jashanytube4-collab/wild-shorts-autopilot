title: Why Baby Seals Are Born White
subject: harp seal | Pagophilus groenlandicus
mood: curious
tags: harp seal, seals, arctic, cute animals, marine mammals
---
Baby harp seals are born with fluffy *white* coats.
Their fur helps them soak up the sun's warmth, on the Arctic ice.
These babies, called *whitecoats*, grow incredibly fast.
Their mother's milk is around *50 percent fat*.
They can gain over *two kilograms* a day.
After about *12 days*, their mother leaves them.
The pups stay on the ice, living off their fat, until they learn to swim and hunt.
Adult *harp seals* get their name from a dark, harp-shaped pattern on their backs.
They can dive down to around *400 meters*, and stay under for around *15 minutes*.
They migrate thousands of kilometers between the Arctic and their breeding grounds.
Mothers can find their own pups among thousands, by *smell*.
Hunting of whitecoat pups caused worldwide protests, and it's now banned in Canada.
Fluffy, white, and growing fast.
