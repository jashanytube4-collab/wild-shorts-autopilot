title: The Fish That Eats Sharks

This fish can swallow a *shark* in one gulp.
Meet the *Atlantic goliath grouper*.
It can grow over *two meters* long and weigh around *360 kilograms*.
It doesn't chase its prey. It *sucks* it in.
By opening its huge mouth fast, it creates a vacuum that pulls in fish, crabs, and even *sharks*. [blacktip shark | Carcharhinus limbatus]
Fishermen have filmed goliath groupers snatching hooked sharks right off their lines.
They also make loud *booming* sounds with their swim bladders, which can be felt by divers.
Goliath groupers can live for around *40 years* or more.
In the 1980s, overfishing nearly wiped them out.
In 1990, fishing for them was banned in US waters.
Since then, they've been slowly coming back.
They gather at shipwrecks to spawn, often in groups of dozens.
A gentle giant with a very big appetite.
