This bird can fly in near *complete silence*.
Meet the *barn owl*.
Its feathers have soft, comb-like edges that break up the air rushing over the wings.
That cuts down the noise a lot.
So the mouse never hears it coming. [house mouse | Mus musculus]
And the barn owl has some of the best hearing of any animal ever tested.
Its face is shaped like a *satellite dish*, funneling sound toward its ears.
Its ears are at *different heights* on its head, one higher than the other.
That lets it pinpoint sounds up and down, and left and right.
It can catch a mouse in *total darkness*, using sound alone.
A single barn owl family can eat over *1,000* rodents in a year.
That's why some farmers put up nest boxes to attract them.
Owls also can't move their eyes, so they turn their heads up to *270 degrees*.
Silent, precise, and deadly at night.
