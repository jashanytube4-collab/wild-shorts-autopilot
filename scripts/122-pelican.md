title: Pelicans Will Eat Almost Anything
subject: brown pelican | Pelecanus occidentalis
mood: curious
tags: pelican, birds, funny animals, pigeons, ocean birds
---
Pelicans will try to eat almost *anything*.
They've been filmed swallowing *pigeons*, ducklings, and even other seabirds.
Their bill pouch can hold around *11 liters* of water, about three times more than its stomach.
The *brown pelican* hunts in a dramatic way.
It flies high above the ocean, folds its wings, and dives straight into the water, head first.
Just before it hits, it rotates its body to protect its neck.
It also has air sacs under its skin, which cushion the impact and help it pop back up.
Then it scoops up fish, along with lots of water.
It drains the water out of its pouch, then swallows the fish *whole*.
Seagulls often wait nearby, trying to steal fish right out of its *mouth*.
Pelicans have been around for at least *30 million years*, and fossils show they've barely changed.
Big bills, big appetite.
