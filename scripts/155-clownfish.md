title: Clownfish Can Change From Male To Female
subject: ocellaris clownfish | Amphiprion ocellaris
mood: curious
tags: clownfish, nemo, sea anemone, coral reef, ocean
---
In real life, *Finding Nemo* would have had a very different story.
All clownfish are born *male*.
Each anemone is home to a group, led by one big *female* and her male partner.
If the female dies, her male partner changes into a *female*.
Then the next-biggest male becomes her new partner.
So after Nemo's mom died, his dad Marlin would have become a *female*.
Clownfish live inside *sea anemones*, which are covered in stinging tentacles. [magnificent sea anemone | Heteractis magnifica]
Most fish get stung, but clownfish are protected by a layer of *mucus* on their skin.
In return, they clean the anemone, chase away fish that eat it, and their waste may even feed it.
Clownfish rarely move far from their anemone their whole lives.
And they're *noisy*, making popping and clicking sounds.
Partners for life, with a very surprising family tree.
