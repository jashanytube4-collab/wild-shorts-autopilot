title: The Monkey With A Blue Face And No Nose
subject: golden snub-nosed monkey | Rhinopithecus roxellana
mood: curious
tags: golden snub nosed monkey, china, monkeys, cute animals, endangered
---
This monkey has a *blue face*, golden fur, and almost no nose.
Meet the *golden snub-nosed monkey*, from the mountain forests of *China*.
Its tiny, upturned nose might help prevent *frostbite* in the freezing winters.
Some scientists think that's how it lost the nose bridge over time.
These monkeys live in the cold mountains, where snow covers the ground for months.
In winter, they huddle together in groups to stay warm.
And they live in huge societies, sometimes hundreds strong, divided into small families.
Each family has one male, several females, and their young.
Its golden fur can be over *ten centimeters* long.
Long ago, people in China valued its fur so much that they hunted it heavily.
Today it's *endangered*, and protected in nature reserves.
Some legends say its name, roxellana, comes from a famous sultan's wife with a turned-up nose.
A golden mountain monkey, unlike any other.
