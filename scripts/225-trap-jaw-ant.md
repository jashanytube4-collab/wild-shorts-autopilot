This ant's jaws snap shut faster than almost anything in nature.
Meet the *trap-jaw ant*.
It holds its jaws wide open, locked at around *180 degrees*, like a bear trap.
Tiny hairs inside act as triggers.
When something touches them, the jaws snap shut at up to around *230 kilometers per hour*.
The whole strike takes less than a *thousandth* of a second.
That's one of the fastest movements of any animal.
They use it to grab insects instantly.
But they've found another use.
When in danger, a trap-jaw ant can slam its jaws against the ground, launching itself into the *air*.
It can flip itself over *40 centimeters*, many times its own height, to escape predators like *antlions*.
Imagine a human jumping onto the roof of a building by biting the ground.
Tiny, but powered like a spring-loaded *weapon*.
