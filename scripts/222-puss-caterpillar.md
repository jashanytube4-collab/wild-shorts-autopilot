This might look like a tiny fluffy toupee, but don't touch it.
Meet the *puss caterpillar*, one of the most venomous caterpillars in North America.
Under its soft, hairy coat are hidden venomous *spines*.
Touch it, and they break off in your skin.
The pain is intense, often described as like a *broken bone*.
It can spread up your arm, and cause swelling, nausea, and headaches.
Some victims compare it to being hit with a *baseball bat*.
Its fluffy look even inspired a nickname: the *Trump caterpillar*.
It grows into a fuzzy little moth called the *southern flannel moth*. [southern flannel moth | Megalopyge opercularis]
The moth is harmless.
Puss caterpillars live in the southern United States, often on oak and elm trees.
Kids sometimes pick them up, thinking they're cute.
If you find one, admire it, but don't touch it.
