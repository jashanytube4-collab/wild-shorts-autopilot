This bird shows off its *blue feet* to find love.
Meet the *blue-footed booby*.
During courtship, males strut in front of females, lifting their bright blue feet one at a time.
And the bluer the feet, the more attractive the male.
That blue color comes from pigments in the fresh *fish* they eat.
So bright feet show that a male is healthy and a good *hunter*.
If a male goes hungry, his feet can fade within *two days*.
Blue-footed boobies are expert divers.
They plunge into the ocean from heights of around *25 meters*, hitting the water at high speed.
Groups sometimes dive together, like a rain of *arrows*.
Their name may come from the Spanish word *bobo*, meaning *fool*, because they waddle clumsily on land.
They live along the Pacific coast, from Mexico to Peru, and in the *Galápagos Islands*.
