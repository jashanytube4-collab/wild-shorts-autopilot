This bird has the biggest wingspan of any living bird, over *three meters*.
Meet the *wandering albatross*.
It can glide for hours without flapping its wings once.
It uses a technique called *dynamic soaring*, gaining energy from the wind over the waves.
Some albatrosses fly around the *entire world* in less than *two months*.
They spend most of their lives at sea, only coming to land to breed.
Young albatrosses can spend around five years at sea before ever touching land again.
They mate for life, and couples perform elaborate *dances* when they reunite.
A female named *Wisdom*, a Laysan albatross, is the oldest known wild bird. [Laysan albatross | Phoebastria immutabilis]
She's over *70 years* old, and still laying eggs.
Sadly, many albatrosses die when they grab bait from fishing lines and get *hooked*.
Simple changes, like weighted lines, have helped save many of them.
