title: The Crab With A Four Meter Leg Span
subject: Japanese spider crab | Macrocheira kaempferi
mood: curious

This crab's legs can stretch around *four meters* from tip to tip.
Meet the *Japanese spider crab*, the largest crab on Earth by leg span.
It lives in the deep waters around *Japan*, sometimes hundreds of meters down.
Its body is only about *40 centimeters* across.
But those long, thin legs make it look like a giant *spider*.
Despite its scary look, it's surprisingly gentle.
It mostly scavenges, picking at dead animals and plants on the seafloor.
It decorates its shell with sponges and other living things to *camouflage* itself.
And if it loses a leg, it can slowly grow it back.
Japanese spider crabs may live up to *100 years*, according to some estimates.
Every spring, they move into shallower water to lay eggs.
Fishermen in Japan consider it a *delicacy*.
An ancient-looking giant, hiding in the deep.
