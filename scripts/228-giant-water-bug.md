title: The Bug That Eats Snakes And Turtles
subject: giant water bug | Lethocerus americanus
mood: danger
tags: giant water bug, toe biter, insects, predators, ponds

This insect can kill and eat *snakes*, frogs, and even small *turtles*.
Meet the *giant water bug*, also called the *toe-biter*.
It can grow up to around *12 centimeters* long.
It lies in wait in ponds, then grabs prey with its powerful front legs.
It stabs it with a sharp beak and injects saliva that turns its insides to *mush*.
Then it sucks out the liquid.
Its bite is one of the most painful of any insect, and it often bites swimmers' *toes*.
In some species, the females lay their eggs on the male's *back*.
He carries them around for weeks, keeping them safe and supplied with oxygen.
In Thailand, giant water bugs are a popular *snack*, and they're said to taste a bit like salty fruit.
They're also attracted to lights at night, which is why they're sometimes called *electric light bugs*.
Small monster of the pond.
