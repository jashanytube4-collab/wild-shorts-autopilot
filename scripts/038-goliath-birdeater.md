This is the biggest spider on Earth, and it's the size of a *dinner plate*.
Meet the *Goliath birdeater*.
Its legs can span around *30 centimeters*.
It weighs up to *175 grams*, heavier than many baby birds.
Its fangs are up to *two centimeters* long, strong enough to puncture skin.
But despite its name, it rarely eats birds.
It mostly hunts worms, insects, frogs, and *rodents*.
When threatened, it doesn't just bite.
It rubs its legs together, making a *hissing* sound you can hear meters away.
Then it flicks tiny barbed hairs off its belly straight at its attacker.
They're incredibly *itchy*, and very painful in the eyes.
And yet in parts of South America, people roast and eat it, saying it tastes a bit like *shrimp*.
Its venom is about as strong as a *wasp sting*.
So it's terrifying to look at, but not deadly.
