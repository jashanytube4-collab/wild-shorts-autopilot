How strong is a gorilla, really?
A full-grown *silverback* can weigh around *180 kilograms*.
Scientists estimate it's several times stronger than an *average adult human*.
It can bend thick bamboo, tear apart branches, and *knock down* small trees.
Its bite force is about *twice* that of a lion's.
But here's the twist: gorillas are mostly *gentle*.
They spend their days eating leaves, stems and fruit, up to *20 kilograms* a day.
The silverback leads his family, and when danger appears, he beats his chest, which can be heard up to a *kilometer* away.
Most charges are bluffs.
They're also incredibly smart.
A gorilla named *Koko* reportedly learned over *1,000 signs* in a version of sign language.
And humans share about *98 percent* of their DNA with gorillas.
Strong enough to crush you, gentle enough to *ignore you*.
