title: The Hyena That Only Eats Termites
subject: aardwolf | Proteles cristatus

This hyena doesn't hunt or scavenge. It eats *termites*.
Meet the *aardwolf*.
It's a member of the hyena family, but it's small, about the size of a fox.
Its name means *earth wolf* in Afrikaans.
And it has tiny, peg-like cheek teeth, too weak to chew meat.
Instead, it licks up termites with its long, sticky tongue.
A single aardwolf can eat around *300,000* termites in a single night.
It mostly targets one type of termite that comes out at night.
The aardwolf isn't bothered by the chemicals the termites spray in defense.
When threatened, it raises a long mane of hair along its back, making itself look much *bigger*.
Aardwolves are mostly solitary, and active at night.
They mark their territory with scent hundreds of times a night.
A gentle hyena, with a very specific diet.
