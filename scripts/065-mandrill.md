This is the most colorful mammal on Earth.
Meet the *mandrill*, the largest monkey in the world.
Males have bright *red* and *blue* faces, and a rainbow-colored rump.
And the brighter a male's colors, the higher his rank.
When a male rises to the top of the group, his colors actually get *brighter*.
If he loses his position, they *fade*.
Mandrills live in huge groups in the rainforests of Central Africa.
Some groups, called *hordes*, can include over *800* animals, among the largest of any primate except humans.
Their canine teeth can be over *six centimeters* long.
And when they "smile," showing those fangs, it's often a sign of *friendliness*, not a threat.
They store food in cheek pouches to eat later.
You might know one already, *Rafiki* from The Lion King was partly based on a mandrill.
