subject: saltwater crocodile | Crocodylus porosus

This is the largest reptile alive today.
The *saltwater crocodile* can grow over *six meters* long and weigh more than a *ton*.
It has one of the strongest bites ever measured, around *3,700 pounds per square inch*.
That's like having a small car slammed shut on you.
It can hold its breath for *over an hour* when resting.
And it doesn't chase prey. It *waits*.
It lies completely still, with only its eyes and nostrils above the water, sometimes for *hours*.
When something comes to drink, it explodes out of the water, grabs it, and drags it under.
Then it performs the *death roll*, spinning to tear its prey apart.
Despite the name, it lives in rivers too, and it's been spotted *far out at sea*.
Some have surfed ocean currents between *islands*.
They've barely changed in millions of years, because they never needed to.
