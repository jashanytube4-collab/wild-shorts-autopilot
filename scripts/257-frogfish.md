This fish catches its prey with its own *fishing rod*.
Meet the *frogfish*.
On top of its head is a long spine, with a lure at the end that looks like a worm, a shrimp, or a tiny fish.
It wiggles the lure in front of its mouth.
When a fish comes close, the frogfish opens its mouth and *sucks* it in.
It's one of the fastest strikes in the animal world, in about *six milliseconds*.
Frogfish are masters of disguise.
They can look like sponges, rocks, or coral.
Some even change color over weeks to match their surroundings.
They don't swim much.
Instead, they *walk* along the seafloor using their fins like legs.
They can also swallow prey up to *twice* their own size, by expanding their stomach.
A lazy fisherman with a perfect trap.
