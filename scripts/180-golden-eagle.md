This eagle can knock *mountain goats* off cliffs.
Meet the *golden eagle*, one of the most powerful birds of prey in the world.
It can dive at over *240 kilometers per hour*.
Its grip is so strong it can crush the bones of its prey.
It usually hunts rabbits, hares and marmots. [yellow-bellied marmot | Marmota flaviventris]
But golden eagles have been filmed attacking much larger animals, like young deer and *goats* on steep cliffs, sending them falling.
In Central Asia, people have trained golden eagles to hunt for over a thousand years.
The *Kazakh eagle hunters* of Mongolia still use them to catch foxes, and even *wolves*.
Golden eagles mate for life, and a pair may use the same nest for decades, adding to it every year.
Some nests have grown over *two meters* wide.
Its eyesight is incredibly sharp, letting it spot a rabbit from more than a *kilometer* away.
It's the national bird of *Mexico*, Germany, Albania, and many other countries.
