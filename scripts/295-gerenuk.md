title: The Antelope That Stands On Two Legs

This antelope stands on its *back legs* to eat.
Meet the *gerenuk*, from the dry lands of East Africa.
Its name means *giraffe-necked* in the Somali language.
It has a long, thin neck and long legs.
When it finds a tasty tree, it rises up onto its hind legs, using its front legs to pull down *branches*.
That lets it reach leaves that other antelopes can't.
Gerenuks rarely, if ever, need to *drink water*.
They get all the moisture they need from leaves.
That lets them live in deserts where other animals can't survive.
Only males have horns, which are thick and curved.
When threatened, gerenuks freeze and stand very still, blending in with the bushes.
A gazelle that thinks it's a giraffe.
