title: The Bird That Can Copy Any Sound
subject: superb lyrebird | Menura novaehollandiae

This bird can copy the sound of *chainsaws* and *camera shutters*.
Meet the *superb lyrebird* from Australia.
It's one of the greatest vocal mimics in the world.
Males copy the calls of dozens of other birds, so well that even the birds themselves are fooled.
Some lyrebirds near people have been reported copying car alarms, phones, and *construction sounds*.
They do it to impress females.
A male clears a small stage on the forest floor, fans his long tail feathers over his head, and *dances*.
His song can include up to around *20 different species*.
Scientists even found males sometimes copy the sound of a *mixed flock* of birds in alarm, possibly to trick females into staying close.
Its tail feathers are shaped like an ancient Greek instrument called a *lyre*.
That's where its name comes from.
It's also on the Australian *ten-cent coin*.
The forest's very own sound machine.
