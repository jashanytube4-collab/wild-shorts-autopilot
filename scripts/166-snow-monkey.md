title: The Monkeys That Take Hot Baths
subject: Japanese macaque | Macaca fuscata
mood: curious
tags: snow monkey, japanese macaque, hot springs, japan, monkeys
---
In Japan, monkeys take *hot spring baths* in the snow.
These are *Japanese macaques*, also called *snow monkeys*.
They live farther north than any other monkey, apart from humans, where winters drop well below zero.
At *Jigokudani* in central Japan, they soak in natural hot springs to stay *warm*.
Scientists found that bathing even lowers their *stress* hormones.
But the habit started by accident, in the 1960s, when a young monkey followed food into the warm water.
Others copied, and it spread through the troop.
These monkeys are famous for learning new *traditions*.
On one island, a young female named *Imo* started washing sweet potatoes in the sea.
Soon, many others did it too, and the habit was passed down for *generations*.
Some troops even make *snowballs*, apparently just for fun.
Their faces turn bright red as adults.
Clever, cozy, and surprisingly cultured.
