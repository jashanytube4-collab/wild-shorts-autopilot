title: Why Elephants Are Afraid Of Bees
subject: African bush elephant | Loxodonta africana
mood: curious
tags: elephant, african elephant, bees, smart animals, africa
---
The biggest land animal on Earth is afraid of a *tiny insect*.
An *African elephant* can weigh up to *six tons*.
But when it hears angry *bees*, it shakes its head, trumpets, and *runs*. [western honey bee | Apis mellifera]
Why? Because bees go for the softest parts: inside the *trunk* and around the *eyes*.
Scientists even discovered elephants have a special *warning call* that means "bees!"
When others hear it, they flee.
Farmers in Kenya now use this to protect their crops.
They hang *beehive fences* around their fields, and elephants stay away.
The farmers get to sell the *honey*, too.
Elephants are incredibly intelligent.
They remember watering holes they visited *decades* ago.
They seem to mourn their dead, touching the bones with their trunks.
And the trunk itself has about *40,000 muscles*.
The biggest, smartest animal on land, scared of something smaller than your *thumbnail*.
