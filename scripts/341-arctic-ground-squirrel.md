title: The Squirrel That Cools Below Freezing
subject: Arctic ground squirrel | Urocitellus parryii
mood: curious

This squirrel's body can drop *below freezing* while it hibernates.
Meet the *Arctic ground squirrel*.
During hibernation, its body temperature can fall to around *minus three degrees Celsius*.
That's the lowest body temperature recorded in any mammal.
And yet, its blood doesn't *freeze*.
Scientists are still studying how it manages to avoid ice forming in its body.
Its heart slows to just a few beats a minute.
But every few weeks, it does something strange.
It *shivers* itself back to a normal temperature for around half a day.
Then it cools down again.
Scientists think these warm-ups may help its brain and body *repair* themselves.
It hibernates for up to *eight months* a year.
NASA has even studied hibernating animals, hoping to help astronauts on long space trips.
A frozen squirrel that always wakes up.
