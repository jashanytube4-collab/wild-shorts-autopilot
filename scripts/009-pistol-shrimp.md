This tiny shrimp makes a flash nearly as *hot as the Sun's surface*.
Meet the *pistol shrimp*.
One of its claws is huge, and when it snaps shut, it shoots out a jet of water at around *100 kilometers per hour*.
That jet creates a bubble that collapses with a *bang* louder than a *gunshot*.
For a split second, the temperature inside that bubble reaches *thousands of degrees*.
The shockwave is enough to *stun or kill* small fish.
Colonies of these shrimp are so loud that during World War Two, they messed with *submarine sonar*.
But this little gunslinger has a weakness: it's nearly *blind*.
So it teams up with a fish called a *goby*. [yellow prawn goby | Cryptocentrus cinctus]
The shrimp digs and cleans a burrow they both live in.
The goby stands guard, and the shrimp keeps one *antenna* touching it at all times. [yellow prawn goby | Cryptocentrus cinctus]
At the first sign of danger, the goby flicks its tail, and they both *dive inside*.
