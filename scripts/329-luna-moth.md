This beautiful moth lives for only around *one week* as an adult.
Meet the *luna moth*, from North America.
It's pale green, with long, sweeping *tails* on its back wings.
Its wings can span around *11 centimeters*.
As an adult, it has *no mouth*.
It can't eat, so it lives off energy stored as a caterpillar.
It has just one purpose: to find a mate and lay eggs.
Females release a scent that males can detect with their feathery antennae from kilometers away.
Those long tails have a secret job.
When the moth flies, they spin and flutter, confusing bats that hunt using sound. [bat | Chiroptera]
Bats often attack the tails instead of the body.
Scientists found moths with tails were much more likely to survive bat attacks.
A short, beautiful life, with a clever defense.
