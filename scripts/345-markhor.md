This wild goat has horns shaped like giant *corkscrews*.
Meet the *markhor*, from the mountains of Central Asia.
Male horns can grow over *1.5 meters* long, twisting in spirals.
Its name may come from a Persian word often translated as *snake eater*.
Old legends said it killed snakes and ate them, but there's no evidence for that.
Markhors live on steep cliffs and rocky slopes.
In winter, they move down to lower forests to find food.
Males have long, flowing beards and shaggy manes.
They use their horns in head-butting fights during the mating season.
The markhor is the national animal of *Pakistan*.
It was once endangered, but carefully managed hunting programs, where local villages benefit, helped numbers grow.
Today, it's classed as *near threatened*.
Spiral horns, and a spiral success story.
