title: Manatees Are Related To Elephants
subject: West Indian manatee | Trichechus manatus

This gentle giant's closest living relative is the *elephant*. [African bush elephant | Loxodonta africana]
Meet the *manatee*, also called the *sea cow*.
It can weigh over *500 kilograms*, and it spends its days eating seagrass, sometimes around *ten percent* of its body weight a day.
Its teeth are constantly *replaced*, moving forward like a conveyor belt.
And its flippers have tiny *fingernails*, a reminder of its land-dwelling ancestors.
Manatees can't survive in cold water.
So in winter, hundreds gather in warm springs and even near power plants in *Florida*.
Sailors once claimed manatees were *mermaids*.
Christopher Columbus wrote that he saw three mermaids, but said they weren't as beautiful as he expected.
Historians think he probably saw *manatees*.
Manatees can stay underwater for up to *20 minutes* while resting.
Their biggest threat is boats.
Many manatees carry *propeller scars*, used by scientists to tell them apart.
