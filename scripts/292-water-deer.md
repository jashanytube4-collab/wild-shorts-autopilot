This deer has *vampire fangs*.
Meet the *water deer*, also called the *vampire deer*.
Males don't grow antlers.
Instead, they have long, curved tusks that hang down from their upper jaw.
These "fangs" can grow up to around *eight centimeters* long.
Males use them in fights over territory.
And they can move them, pulling them back when eating, and pushing them forward when threatened.
Water deer are native to China and Korea, and they love wetlands and riverbanks.
They're good swimmers, and can swim for kilometers between islands.
Strangely, there are now many water deer in *England*, where they escaped from parks and zoos.
Some scientists think a large share of the world's water deer might now live in the UK.
And when alarmed, they make a strange *barking* or screaming sound.
A deer that looks like it came from a horror movie.
