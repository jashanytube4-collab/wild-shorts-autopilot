title: The Newt That's Too Poisonous To Eat
subject: rough-skinned newt | Taricha granulosa
mood: danger
tags: rough skinned newt, poisonous animals, newt, oregon, arms race
---
This tiny newt can carry enough poison to kill *several people*.
Meet the *rough-skinned newt*, from the Pacific Northwest of North America.
Its skin contains *tetrodotoxin*, the same poison found in pufferfish.
There's a story of a man in Oregon who swallowed one on a dare, and died hours later.
But there's one animal that can eat it: the *common garter snake*. [common garter snake | Thamnophis sirtalis]
Over time, some garter snakes have evolved resistance to the poison.
In response, newts in those areas became even *more* toxic.
Scientists call it an *evolutionary arms race*.
In some areas, the newts are so toxic, and the snakes so resistant, that both seem to be locked in an endless battle.
When threatened, the newt arches its back, showing off its bright orange belly as a *warning*.
So don't eat newts. Seriously.
