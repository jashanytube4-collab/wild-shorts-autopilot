This wild dog hunts in packs that can chase off *tigers*. [tiger | Panthera tigris]
Meet the *dhole*, also called the *whistling dog*.
It lives in the forests of Asia, from India to Indonesia.
Dholes communicate using *whistles*, which help them coordinate during hunts in thick forest.
Packs can have over *12* members, and sometimes join together into groups of 30 or more.
They hunt deer, wild pigs, and even animals as big as *gaur* and *water buffalo*.
Dholes have been seen mobbing tigers and leopards, driving them away from kills.
In Rudyard Kipling's *The Jungle Book*, they're the fearsome "red dogs."
But here's a gentle side: dholes let their pups eat first at a kill.
Unlike most dogs, they have fewer molars.
Sadly, they're *endangered*, with only a few thousand left in the wild.
A whistling, red-coated hunter that even tigers respect.
