title: The Parrot That Understood Numbers
subject: grey parrot | Psittacus erithacus
mood: curious
tags: african grey parrot, alex the parrot, smart birds, parrot, intelligence
---
This parrot could *count*, name colors, and understand the idea of *zero*.
His name was *Alex*, an African grey parrot.
For around *30 years*, he worked with scientist *Irene Pepperberg*.
Alex learned over *100* words.
He could identify colors, shapes and materials.
Shown a tray of objects, he could say how many there were of each color.
He understood "bigger," "smaller," "same," and "different."
And he seemed to grasp something like *zero*, saying "none."
One famous story: Alex once asked what color he was, after seeing himself in a mirror.
He was told "grey" a few times, and learned the word.
African greys are among the smartest birds on Earth.
Alex died in 2007, and his last words to Pepperberg were reported as: "You be good. See you tomorrow. I love you."
A bird that changed how we think about animal minds.
