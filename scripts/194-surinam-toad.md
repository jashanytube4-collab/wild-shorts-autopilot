title: The Toad That Carries Babies In Its Back

This toad's babies hatch out of its *back*.
Meet the *Surinam toad*, from the rivers of South America.
It's flat, brown, and looks a bit like a dead *leaf*.
When it mates, the pair flips over and over in the water in a kind of underwater dance.
The female releases eggs, which the male fertilizes and presses onto her *back*.
Then her skin swells up around them, sealing each egg in its own *pocket*.
For weeks, the babies grow inside her back.
And then, they burst out of their pockets as tiny, fully formed *toads*.
It's often called one of the creepiest births in the animal kingdom.
Surinam toads have no tongue and no teeth.
They sense prey with star-shaped tips on their fingers, then gulp it down.
For people with a fear of holes, this one's a *nightmare*.
