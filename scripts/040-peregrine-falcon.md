The fastest animal on Earth isn't the cheetah. [cheetah | Acinonyx jubatus]
It's this bird, the *peregrine falcon*.
When it dives at its prey, called a *stoop*, it can reach over *300 kilometers per hour*.
That's faster than a Formula One car on most tracks.
At that speed, air would normally blow into its lungs with damaging force.
But it has small bony cones in its nostrils that break up the airflow.
Jet engine designers used the same idea for their *air intakes*.
It also has a third eyelid that sweeps across its eyes like a *windshield wiper*.
It hits pigeons and ducks mid-air, so hard that the prey is often knocked out on impact.
Peregrines are found on every continent except *Antarctica*.
Today, many even live in *cities*, nesting on skyscrapers and hunting city pigeons.
After nearly disappearing in the 1960s because of the pesticide *DDT*, they've made an incredible comeback.
