title: The National Bird That Steals Food
subject: bald eagle | Haliaeetus leucocephalus
mood: epic
tags: bald eagle, eagles, america, national bird, birds of prey
---
America's national bird is a bit of a *thief*.
The *bald eagle* often steals fish from other birds, especially the *osprey*. [osprey | Pandion haliaetus]
It chases them through the air until they drop their catch.
Benjamin Franklin reportedly complained about this, calling it a bird of "bad moral character."
But bald eagles are also impressive hunters.
They can snatch fish from the water with talons that grip with incredible strength.
Their nests are some of the biggest of any bird.
One in Florida was around *three meters* deep, and weighed over *two tons*.
And they're not actually bald. The name comes from an old word meaning *white-headed*.
Young eagles are brown, and don't get their white heads until they're around *five* years old.
In the 1960s, bald eagles nearly vanished from the US, poisoned by the pesticide *DDT*.
After it was banned, they made one of the greatest comebacks in American wildlife history.
