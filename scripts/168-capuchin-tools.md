title: The Monkeys That Entered The Stone Age
subject: bearded capuchin | Sapajus libidinosus
mood: curious
tags: capuchin monkey, tools, smart animals, brazil, monkeys
---
In Brazil, monkeys have been using *stone tools* for at least *3,000 years*.
These are *bearded capuchins*.
They place hard nuts on a flat rock, then lift heavy stones and smash them open.
Sometimes the stone is almost as heavy as the monkey itself.
Young capuchins spend years watching adults before they master it.
And they choose their tools carefully, testing stones for the right weight and hardness.
Scientists dug up old stone tools at their sites and found that this tradition is thousands of years old.
That makes it one of the oldest known examples of tool use outside of *humans*.
Capuchins have also been seen using sticks to dig, and to flush lizards out of holes.
In some groups, they even *bang* stones together in strange rituals scientists don't fully understand.
And they rub certain plants and insects on their fur, possibly to keep away bugs.
Small monkeys with very big skills.
