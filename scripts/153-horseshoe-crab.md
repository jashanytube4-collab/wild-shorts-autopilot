This animal's *blue blood* has helped protect millions of people.
Meet the *horseshoe crab*.
It's been around for over *400 million years*, longer than the dinosaurs.
Its blood is blue because it uses *copper* instead of iron to carry oxygen.
But that's not why it's valuable.
Its blood contains special cells that clump together around bacteria.
Drug companies use them to test vaccines, injections, and medical implants for dangerous *contamination*.
So every year, hundreds of thousands of horseshoe crabs are caught, and some of their blood is drained.
Most are released alive, but a lot of them *don't survive*.
Scientists have now created a lab-made alternative that could protect them.
And despite the name, horseshoe crabs aren't crabs.
They're more closely related to *spiders* and *scorpions*.
They have *ten eyes*, scattered around their body.
A living fossil, quietly saving human lives.
