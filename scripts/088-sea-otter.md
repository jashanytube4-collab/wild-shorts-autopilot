Sea otters sometimes hold *hands* while they sleep.
So they don't drift apart.
They also wrap themselves in *kelp*, like a seatbelt, to stay in place.
Sea otters have the *densest fur* of any animal.
Up to around *one million hairs* in a single square inch.
Unlike seals and whales, they have no blubber. Their fur traps air to keep them warm.
And they have a favorite *rock*.
Sea otters keep a special stone in a pouch of skin under their arm.
Then they float on their backs, and smash *clams* and *sea urchins* open on their chests. [purple sea urchin | Strongylocentrotus purpuratus]
They're one of the few mammals besides primates known to use *tools*.
And they're heroes of the ocean.
By eating sea urchins, they protect *kelp forests*, which shelter thousands of other species.
Where sea otters disappeared, urchins took over, and the kelp forests vanished.
Cute, clever, and holding the ecosystem together.
