This insect can be heavier than a *mouse*.
Meet the *giant weta*, from New Zealand.
One wild giant weta was found weighing around *71 grams*, while carrying eggs.
That makes it one of the heaviest insects ever recorded.
They look like giant crickets, with spiny back legs.
Weta have been around for over *190 million years*, since before many dinosaurs.
New Zealand had no native land mammals, apart from bats.
So weta filled the role that mice play elsewhere, eating leaves, seeds and insects.
But when humans brought rats, cats and stoats, weta became easy prey. [brown rat | Rattus norvegicus]
Many species are now endangered, surviving mainly on predator-free islands.
In the Maori language, the giant weta's name means *god of ugly things*.
Despite their size, they're mostly gentle, and move slowly.
