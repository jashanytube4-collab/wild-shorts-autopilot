This tiny jellyfish might be able to *live forever*.
Meet the *immortal jellyfish*.
It's smaller than a fingernail, only around *five millimeters* across.
When it gets old, injured, or stressed, it does something incredible.
It turns back into a *baby*.
It sinks to the seafloor and changes into a polyp, the earliest stage of a jellyfish's life.
Then it grows up all over again.
In theory, it could repeat this cycle *forever*.
Scientists call it *biologically immortal*.
Of course, it can still be eaten, or die from disease.
So in the wild, most don't actually live forever.
Scientists are studying its genes, hoping to learn more about *aging* and how cells can renew themselves.
Immortal jellyfish have spread around the world's oceans, possibly carried in the water of ships.
A creature that can rewind its own life.
