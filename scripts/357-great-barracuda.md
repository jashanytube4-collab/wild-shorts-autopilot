This fish strikes like a *torpedo*.
Meet the *great barracuda*.
It has a long, slim body, built for explosive bursts of speed of around *50 kilometers per hour*.
And it has fang-like teeth of different sizes, some sticking out of its mouth.
Barracudas often hang motionless in the water, then launch at their prey in a flash.
They're curious, and often follow snorkelers.
They're attracted to shiny things, which can look like the scales of fish.
That's why divers are told not to wear shiny *jewelry*.
Attacks on humans are rare, and usually happen in murky water.
Eating large barracudas can be dangerous.
They can carry a toxin called *ciguatera*, which builds up from the fish they eat.
It can make people sick for weeks.
Young barracudas often hide in mangroves and seagrass beds.
A silver missile of the reef.
