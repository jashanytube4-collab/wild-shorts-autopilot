title: The Bird That Tricks Others Into Raising Its Babies
subject: common cuckoo | Cuculus canorus
mood: curious
tags: cuckoo, brood parasite, birds, nature tricks, animal behavior
---
This bird never raises its own chicks.
Meet the *common cuckoo*.
The female sneaks into another bird's nest, and lays one of her eggs there.
She often removes one of the host's eggs to keep the numbers the same.
Her egg can even look like the host's eggs, matching their color and pattern.
When the cuckoo chick hatches, often first, it does something brutal.
It pushes the other eggs and chicks *out* of the nest.
Then it gets all the food for itself.
Soon it grows much bigger than its foster parents. [Eurasian reed warbler | Acrocephalus scirpaceus]
Yet they keep feeding it, fooled by its loud begging calls, which can sound like a whole *brood* of chicks.
Female cuckoos can lay eggs in around *20* different nests in a season.
And some host birds have started fighting back, recognizing and rejecting cuckoo eggs.
An evolutionary arms race, playing out in nests every spring.
