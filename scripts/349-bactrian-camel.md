This camel can survive both *scorching heat* and *freezing cold*.
Meet the *Bactrian camel*, the camel with *two humps*.
It lives in the Gobi Desert, where temperatures range from around *40 degrees* in summer to *minus 30* in winter.
Its thick winter coat falls off in clumps in spring.
Like other camels, its humps store *fat*, not water.
And it can drink around *100 liters* of water in one go.
Bactrian camels can even eat snow for water in winter.
Wild Bactrian camels are a separate species, and they can drink *salty water* that other animals can't. [wild Bactrian camel | Camelus ferus]
Sadly, wild Bactrian camels are *critically endangered*, with only around *1,000* left.
Domestic Bactrian camels carried goods along the ancient *Silk Road* for centuries.
They can carry heavy loads for long distances.
Two humps, and tough as nails.
