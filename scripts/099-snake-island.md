title: The Island Where Nobody Is Allowed To Go
subject: golden lancehead | Bothrops insularis
mood: danger
tags: snake island, golden lancehead, brazil, venomous snakes, ilha da queimada grande
---
There's an island off the coast of Brazil where visiting is *banned*.
It's called *Ilha da Queimada Grande*, better known as *Snake Island*.
And it's home to one of the most venomous snakes in the Americas, the *golden lancehead*.
They live nowhere else on Earth.
Thousands of years ago, rising seas cut the island off from the mainland.
The snakes that got stuck there had no ground prey to eat.
So they adapted to hunt *birds* that land on the island to rest.
Their venom became strong enough to work fast, before a bird could fly away.
Estimates of how many live there vary, but the island is so tiny that they're found almost *everywhere*.
Today, only scientists and the Brazilian navy are allowed to visit.
There's even a lighthouse on the island, and it's now *automated*.
Ironically, the snake itself is *critically endangered*.
Illegal collectors smuggle them out for huge sums of money.
