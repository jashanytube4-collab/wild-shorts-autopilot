title: The Animal With The Biggest Brain On Earth
subject: sperm whale | Physeter macrocephalus
mood: epic
tags: sperm whale, biggest brain, whales, giant squid, ocean

This animal has the biggest brain on Earth.
The *sperm whale's* brain weighs around *eight kilograms*, about six times heavier than yours.
It dives deeper than almost any other mammal, beyond *2,000 meters*, holding its breath for over an *hour*.
Down in the dark, it hunts *squid*, including giant squid. [Humboldt squid | Dosidicus gigas]
Many sperm whales carry round *scars* from the suckers of squid fighting back.
Its head is filled with a waxy oil called *spermaceti*.
Whalers once boiled it to light lamps and make candles.
And sperm whales are *loud*.
Their clicks can reach around *230 decibels*, the loudest sound made by any animal.
They use them to find prey by echo, and to talk to each other in patterns called *codas*.
Different families even have their own *dialects*.
And they sleep vertically, floating upright near the surface like giant *logs*.
Moby Dick was a sperm whale.
