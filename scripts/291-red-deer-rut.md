title: The Roaring Battles Of The Red Deer
subject: red deer | Cervus elaphus
mood: epic
tags: red deer, deer rut, stags, scotland, antlers
---
Every autumn, male red deer *roar* and fight for control of the herd.
It's called the *rut*.
Stags bellow deep, booming roars across the hills, sometimes thousands of times a day.
Females seem to prefer males with deeper *voices*.
When two stags are evenly matched, they walk side by side, sizing each other up.
Then they lock antlers and *push*.
The fights can cause serious injuries, or even death.
A big stag's antlers can weigh up to around *15 kilograms*.
And they grow back every year, among the fastest-growing tissues of any mammal, growing up to *2.5 centimeters* a day.
During the rut, stags barely eat, and can lose around *20 percent* of their weight.
After the rut, they shed their antlers in late winter, and start growing new ones.
Red deer are among the largest deer in the world.
The roar of autumn in the Scottish Highlands.
