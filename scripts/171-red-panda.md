The original *panda* isn't the black and white one.
It's the *red panda*.
It was given the name *panda* about 50 years before the giant panda. [giant panda | Ailuropoda melanoleuca]
Despite the name, the two aren't closely related.
The red panda is in a family all of its own.
But they do share something: both eat *bamboo*, and both have an extra "false thumb" on their wrist to grip it.
Red pandas live in the mountain forests of the *Himalayas*.
They spend most of their lives in trees, and sleep curled up with their bushy tails wrapped around them like a *blanket*.
When threatened, they stand up on their back legs and raise their arms to look *bigger*.
It's adorable, but they mean it.
They're also one of the few mammals that can climb down trees head first, by rotating their ankles.
Sadly, there may be fewer than *10,000* left in the wild.
And yes, the web browser *Firefox* is named after them.
