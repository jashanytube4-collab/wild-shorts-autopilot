title: The Snake With The Longest Fangs
subject: Gaboon viper | Bitis gabonica
mood: danger
tags: gaboon viper, longest fangs, snakes, africa, venomous snakes
---
This snake has the *longest fangs* of any snake in the world.
Meet the *Gaboon viper*.
Its fangs can reach around *five centimeters* long.
And it produces more venom per bite than almost any other snake.
But here's the surprise: it's incredibly *calm*.
It lies motionless on the forest floor, often for days, waiting for prey to come close.
Its pattern, a mix of browns, pinks and purples, makes it look exactly like a pile of *dead leaves*.
It's one of the best camouflaged snakes on Earth.
When something passes by, it strikes in a blur.
Unlike many vipers, it holds onto its prey until the venom works.
It also has the heaviest body of any viper in Africa.
Despite all this, bites to humans are *rare*, because it's so reluctant to strike.
It's been called the gentle giant of the snake world.
Just don't step on it.
