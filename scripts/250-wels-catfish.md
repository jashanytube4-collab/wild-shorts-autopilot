title: The Catfish That Hunts Pigeons On Land
subject: wels catfish | Silurus glanis
mood: danger
tags: wels catfish, giant catfish, pigeons, europe, freshwater fish
---
In France, giant catfish *leap out of the water* to catch pigeons. [rock pigeon | Columba livia]
These are *wels catfish*, one of the largest freshwater fish in Europe.
They can grow over *two meters* long.
On the Tarn River, scientists filmed them lunging onto the riverbank, grabbing pigeons, and sliding back in.
It's like the way *orcas* beach themselves to catch seals.
They succeeded in more than *a quarter* of their attempts.
Wels catfish have huge mouths and long whiskers that help them sense prey in murky water.
They'll eat fish, frogs, rats, and ducks.
Native to Eastern Europe and Asia, they were introduced into rivers in Western Europe.
Some anglers have caught catfish over *2.7 meters* long.
They can live over *50 years*.
Scientists worry they could harm native fish in the rivers where they've been introduced.
A river giant with surprising hunting skills.
