This wasp hunts *tarantulas*, and it doesn't kill them.
Meet the *tarantula hawk*, one of the largest wasps in the world.
When a female finds a tarantula, the fight is brutal.
She dodges the spider's fangs and stings it on the underside, *paralyzing* it in seconds.
Then she drags the living tarantula, often many times her own weight, back to a burrow.
She lays a single egg on its body, and seals the entrance.
When the larva hatches, it feeds on the still-living spider, saving the vital organs for *last*.
Its sting is also one of the most painful in the world.
One scientist described it as *blinding* and *fierce*, and advised anyone stung to just lie down and *scream*.
Luckily, the pain only lasts a few minutes.
And adult tarantula hawks are surprisingly gentle, spending most of their time sipping *flower nectar*.
Almost nothing dares to eat them.
