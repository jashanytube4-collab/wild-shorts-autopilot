title: The Fish That Can Walk On Land
subject: northern snakehead | Channa argus
mood: danger
tags: snakehead, invasive species, fish, usa, walking fish
---
This fish can survive *out of water* for days.
Meet the *northern snakehead*.
It breathes air using a special organ, so it can live in water that's low in oxygen.
Young snakeheads can even wiggle across land, from one pond to another.
It has a long body, sharp teeth, and a head that looks a bit like a *snake's*.
Snakeheads are native to Asia.
But in the US, they're an *invasive species*.
In 2002, they were found in a pond in Maryland, and the news called them *Frankenfish*.
They eat other fish, frogs, crayfish, and even small birds.
Parents are fierce, guarding their young aggressively.
Officials ask anglers who catch snakeheads to kill them, not release them.
In some places, there are even snakehead fishing tournaments.
A fish that refuses to stay in the water.
