title: The Butterfly That Travels 4,000 Kilometers
subject: monarch butterfly | Danaus plexippus
mood: epic
tags: monarch butterfly, migration, mexico, butterflies, insects
---
Every year, millions of butterflies fly up to *4,000 kilometers* to one place.
These are *monarch butterflies*.
They leave Canada and the US in autumn and fly south to the mountains of central *Mexico*.
There, they gather by the millions, covering trees so densely that the branches *bend*.
But here's the incredible part.
None of the butterflies making that trip has ever been there *before*.
The ones that arrived last year are long gone.
Their great-grandchildren find the way using the *sun* and possibly Earth's *magnetic field*.
The migrating generation lives up to *eight months*, while summer monarchs live just a few weeks.
Monarchs are also *poisonous*.
As caterpillars, they eat milkweed, which contains toxins that stay in their bodies. [common milkweed | Asclepias syriaca]
Birds that eat one often throw it up, and learn to avoid the orange pattern.
A tiny insect, guided by an ancient map no one taught it.
