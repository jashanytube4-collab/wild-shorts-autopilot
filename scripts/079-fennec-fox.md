title: The Fox With Giant Ears
subject: fennec fox | Vulpes zerda
mood: curious
tags: fennec fox, sahara, desert animals, foxes, cute animals
---
This fox's ears can be *15 centimeters* long, about half the length of its body.
Meet the *fennec fox*, the smallest fox in the world.
It lives in the *Sahara Desert*, where days are scorching and nights are freezing.
Those giant ears work like *radiators*, releasing heat to keep it cool.
They also let it hear insects and rodents moving *underground*.
Its feet are covered in thick fur, like built-in *sandals*, protecting them from burning sand.
It can survive a long time *without drinking water*, getting what it needs from its food.
During the day, it hides in burrows that can be up to *ten meters* long.
At night, it hunts insects, lizards, eggs and fruit.
Fennec foxes can jump around *60 centimeters* high and over a meter forward.
And they make sounds like purring, barking, and *screaming*.
Tiny, fluffy, and perfectly built for the desert.
