This antelope jumps straight up into the air, *for no obvious reason*.
Meet the *springbok*.
It bounces up to around *two meters* high, with its legs stiff and its back arched.
This is called *pronking*, or *stotting*.
It looks like it's just having fun.
But scientists think it may also be a message to predators: "I'm fit and fast, don't bother chasing me."
Cheetahs often give up on springboks that pronk the most. [cheetah | Acinonyx jubatus]
When pronking, springboks also fan out a white crest of fur on their back.
Springboks can run at around *90 kilometers per hour*.
They can survive without drinking water for long periods, getting water from plants.
Hundreds of years ago, migrating herds of springbok in southern Africa could have millions of animals.
The springbok is the national animal of *South Africa*, and the name of its famous rugby team.
Jumping for joy, or jumping for life.
