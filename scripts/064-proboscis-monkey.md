Why does this monkey have such a *huge nose*?
Meet the *proboscis monkey*, found only on the island of *Borneo*.
Males can have noses over *ten centimeters* long, hanging down past their mouths.
And it turns out, for females, *size matters*.
Bigger noses make louder, deeper honking calls, which seem to attract *mates*.
Males with bigger noses also tend to have bigger bodies and bigger groups of females.
They also have big round bellies, because they eat lots of leaves that slowly ferment inside their *stomach*.
To help digest them, they sometimes chew their food *twice*, like a cow.
And they're the best swimmers of all monkeys.
They have *webbed* feet and sometimes leap from trees straight into rivers, belly flopping into the water.
Sadly, as Borneo's forests are cleared, they're now *endangered*.
Nature's honking, swimming, pot-bellied monkey.
