title: The Bird That Lays A Giant Egg
subject: North Island brown kiwi | Apteryx mantelli
mood: curious
tags: kiwi, new zealand, birds, giant egg, weird animals
---
This bird lays an egg that's around *20 percent* of its own body weight.
For a human mother, that would be like having a baby that weighs around *12 kilograms*.
Meet the *kiwi*, from New Zealand.
In the final days before laying, the egg takes up so much room that the female can barely *eat*.
The kiwi is a very strange bird.
It can't fly, and its wings are tiny, almost invisible.
Its feathers look more like *hair*.
And it's the only bird in the world with *nostrils* at the tip of its beak.
It uses them to sniff out worms and insects underground.
Its bones are heavy and full of marrow, more like a mammal's.
It has a low body temperature, like a mammal too.
Kiwis are mostly *nocturnal*, and they mate for life.
They're so loved in New Zealand that the people themselves are called *Kiwis*.
