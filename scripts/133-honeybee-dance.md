title: Bees Talk By Dancing
subject: western honey bee | Apis mellifera
mood: curious
tags: honey bee, bees, waggle dance, insects, animal communication
---
Honeybees give directions to food by *dancing*.
When a bee finds a great patch of flowers, she flies home and performs a *waggle dance*.
She walks in a straight line, waggling her body, then circles back and repeats.
The angle of that line shows the direction of the flowers, compared to the *sun*.
The length of the waggle shows how *far* away it is.
Other bees watch, and fly straight to the spot.
The scientist who decoded this, *Karl von Frisch*, won a Nobel Prize for it.
To make one teaspoon of honey, it takes around *twelve* bees their *entire lives*.
A hive can have over *50,000* bees, all working together.
Honeybees can even recognize *human faces* in experiments.
And honey found in ancient Egyptian tombs, thousands of years old, was still *edible*.
Tiny dancers, sweet results.
