title: The Bird With The Longest Migration
subject: Arctic tern | Sterna paradisaea
mood: epic
tags: arctic tern, migration, birds, arctic, antarctica
---
This small bird sees more *daylight* than any other animal on Earth.
Meet the *Arctic tern*.
Every year, it flies from the *Arctic* to the *Antarctic*, and back again.
Following the summer at both ends of the planet.
Tracking devices showed some terns fly around *70,000 kilometers* a year, sometimes even more.
That's the longest migration of any animal.
And they don't fly in a straight line. They zig-zag across the oceans, following the winds.
An Arctic tern can live around *30 years*.
Over a lifetime, it might fly the distance to the *Moon* and back, around *three* times.
Despite its small size, it's fierce.
Arctic terns dive-bomb anything that comes near their nests, including *polar bears* and *humans*.
They even peck at people's heads.
A tiny bird, with the world's biggest frequent flyer miles.
