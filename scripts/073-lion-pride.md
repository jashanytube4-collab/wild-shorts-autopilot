title: Why Lions Live In Groups
subject: lion | Panthera leo
mood: epic
tags: lion, big cats, pride, africa, king of the jungle
---
Lions are the *only* big cats that truly live in groups.
A *pride* is mostly made of related females, their cubs, and a few males.
The females are the main hunters, working as a *team*.
Some spread out to surround the prey, while others wait to *ambush* it.
Together, they can bring down buffalo, zebras, and even young *elephants*. [plains zebra | Equus quagga]
A lion's roar can be heard up to *eight kilometers* away.
It tells other lions: this land is *ours*.
Males defend the territory, but they're often pushed out after just a few years by younger rivals.
And when new males take over, they sometimes kill the cubs of the previous males.
It sounds brutal, but it brings the females back into breeding sooner.
Lions also sleep a lot, up to *20 hours* a day.
And despite the nickname, they don't live in the *jungle*.
They live in grasslands and *savannas*.
