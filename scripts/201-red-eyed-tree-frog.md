Why does this frog have such *bright red eyes*?
Meet the *red-eyed tree frog*.
During the day, it sleeps on leaves, closing its eyes and tucking in its colorful legs.
It turns into a plain green blob, almost invisible.
But if a predator touches it, its eyes *snap open*.
That flash of red can startle the predator for a split second, giving the frog time to *leap away*.
Scientists call this *startle coloration*.
And here's something wild about its babies.
The eggs are laid on leaves above ponds.
If a snake attacks the eggs, the embryos can sense the vibrations, and *hatch early* to escape, dropping into the water.
They can tell the difference between a snake attack and harmless rain.
These frogs also communicate by shaking branches.
A splash of color, with a clever defense.
