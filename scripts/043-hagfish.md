title: The Slimiest Animal In The World
subject: Pacific hagfish | Eptatretus stoutii

This animal can fill a bucket with *slime* in seconds.
Meet the *hagfish*.
When a shark bites one, it instantly releases slime that expands in water to around *10,000 times* its original volume.
The shark's gills clog up, and it has to spit the hagfish out, *gagging*.
The slime is made of super-thin fibers, stronger than *nylon*.
Scientists are studying it to make bulletproof materials.
To clean itself off, the hagfish ties its body into a *knot*, and slides the knot down its body.
It uses the same trick to tear chunks off dead whales on the seafloor.
It has no jaws, so it bites with two rows of tooth-like plates on its tongue.
It can absorb food through its *skin*.
In 2017, a truck carrying hagfish crashed on a highway in Oregon.
The road ended up covered in *slime*.
And hagfish have barely changed in around *300 million years*.
