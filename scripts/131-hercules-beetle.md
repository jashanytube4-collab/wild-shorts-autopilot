This beetle is one of the strongest animals on Earth, for its size.
Meet the *Hercules beetle*.
It can grow up to around *17 centimeters* long, including its giant horn.
That makes it one of the longest beetles in the world.
It's often said it can carry many times its own body weight on its back, though the exact number is hotly debated.
Males use their huge horns to fight.
They grab rivals between their two horns, lift them up, and *throw* them to the ground.
The winner gets to mate.
And its shell can change color with the *humidity*.
In dry air, it's yellow or olive green.
When it gets damp, it turns almost *black*.
Before becoming an adult, the beetle spends up to two years as a giant grub, munching on rotting wood.
Some grubs can weigh over *100 grams*.
A real-life *superhero*, named after one.
