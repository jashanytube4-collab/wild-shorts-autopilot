title: Ravens Can Plan For The Future
subject: common raven | Corvus corax
mood: curious
tags: raven, smart birds, corvids, intelligence, birds
---
Ravens can plan for the *future*, something once thought only humans and apes could do.
In a 2017 experiment, ravens learned to use a tool to open a box with food.
Then the box was taken away.
Later, they were offered a choice of objects, including the tool.
They picked the tool, and saved it, knowing the box would come back *later*.
Ravens also hide food, and if another raven is watching, they move it somewhere else.
They can even pretend to hide food, to *trick* others.
They play.
Ravens have been seen sliding down snowy roofs, again and again, just for fun.
In the wild, they sometimes call wolves to animal carcasses they can't open themselves. [gray wolf | Canis lupus]
The wolves tear it open, and the ravens share the meal.
In many cultures, ravens appear in legends as clever *tricksters*.
Very clever birds indeed.
