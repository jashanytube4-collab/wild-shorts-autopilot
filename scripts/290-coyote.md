title: The Animal That Conquered Every American City
subject: coyote | Canis latrans

Coyotes now live in almost every major city in North America.
Including *New York*, Los Angeles, and Chicago.
In Chicago, scientists believe there are *thousands* of coyotes living in the city.
They've learned to cross roads safely, even *looking both ways* at traffic.
They mostly eat rodents, rabbits, fruit and garbage.
A hundred years ago, coyotes lived mostly in the western plains.
Since then, they've spread across almost the whole continent.
Why? Wolves were wiped out from much of their range, leaving space for coyotes.
And coyotes are *adaptable*. They'll eat almost anything, live almost anywhere.
Some coyotes in the east carry *wolf* DNA, making them bigger than western coyotes.
Coyotes can run at around *65 kilometers per hour*.
Their song of yips and howls makes a few coyotes sound like a big pack.
The ultimate urban survivor.
