title: The Frog That's Too Poisonous To Touch
subject: golden poison frog | Phyllobates terribilis

This frog is so toxic that just *touching it* could be dangerous.
Meet the *golden poison frog*, one of the most poisonous animals on Earth.
It's only about *five centimeters* long.
Yet its skin holds enough *batrachotoxin* to kill *ten grown adults*.
The poison locks the body's nerves and muscles in the *on position*, and the heart can fail.
For centuries, the *Emberá people* of Colombia rubbed their blowgun darts on its back, and those darts stayed deadly for *years*.
That's where the name *poison dart frog* comes from.
And that bright yellow color? It isn't for hiding.
It's a *warning sign* that says don't eat me.
But here's the twist.
The frog doesn't make this poison itself.
It gets it from the *insects* it eats in the wild.
Raised in captivity, on a different diet, these frogs become *completely harmless*.
