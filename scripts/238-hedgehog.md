title: Hedgehogs Can Eat Venomous Snakes
subject: European hedgehog | Erinaceus europaeus
mood: curious
tags: hedgehog, cute animals, europe, snakes, animal facts
---
This cute animal can eat *venomous snakes*.
The *European hedgehog* has some resistance to snake venom, and it's been known to eat adders. [common European adder | Vipera berus]
It's covered in around *5,000 to 7,000* spines.
When threatened, it rolls into a tight, spiky *ball*.
Hedgehogs also do something strange called *self-anointing*.
When they find a new smell, they chew it, make a foamy saliva, and spread it all over their spines.
Nobody knows exactly why.
Hedgehogs walk around *two kilometers* a night, looking for insects, worms and slugs.
In winter, they *hibernate*, their heart rate dropping from around *190* beats a minute to around *20*.
Sadly, hedgehogs are declining in Britain.
People help by cutting small "hedgehog highways" in garden fences, so they can roam.
And don't give them milk. They can't digest it.
