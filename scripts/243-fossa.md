Madagascar's top predator looks like a *cat*, but it isn't one.
Meet the *fossa*.
It's related to the mongoose, and it's the largest meat-eater on the island.
Its main food? *Lemurs*. [ring-tailed lemur | Lemur catta]
It's a superb climber, with flexible ankles that let it run down trees *head first*.
It uses its long tail for balance as it leaps from branch to branch.
It can catch lemurs right out of the trees.
Fossas are mostly solitary, but some males have been seen hunting in *groups*.
Females have a strange phase when they're young, where they look a lot like males.
Scientists think it may protect them from aggressive adult males.
The fossa appeared in the movie *Madagascar* as the villains, chasing the lemurs.
Sadly, it's now listed as *vulnerable*, because of forest loss.
Madagascar's very own mini panther.
