subject: whale shark | Rhincodon typus

The biggest fish in the world is a *gentle giant*.
Meet the *whale shark*.
It can grow around *18 meters* long, about the size of a bus.
Its mouth can be over *a meter* wide, and lined with around *3,000 tiny teeth*.
But it doesn't use them.
It eats *plankton*, filtering huge amounts of water through its gills.
Every whale shark has a unique pattern of white spots, like a *fingerprint*.
Scientists use star-mapping software, designed for astronomers, to identify them.
Whale sharks travel thousands of kilometers across the oceans.
Their skin can be up to about *10 centimeters* thick, one of the thickest of any animal.
And they've even been seen covering their eyes with tiny *teeth*, called dermal denticles.
Divers can swim right next to them.
The biggest fish in the ocean, and it wouldn't hurt a *fly*.
