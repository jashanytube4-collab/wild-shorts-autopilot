title: The Greatest Migration On Earth
subject: blue wildebeest | Connochaetes taurinus
mood: epic

Every year, over *one million* wildebeest walk in a giant loop across East Africa.
It's called the *Great Migration*.
They follow the rains through the *Serengeti* in Tanzania and the *Masai Mara* in Kenya, searching for fresh grass.
Along with hundreds of thousands of zebras and gazelles, they travel around *2,900 kilometers* a year. [plains zebra | Equus quagga]
But the journey is deadly.
The worst part is crossing the *Mara River*.
Huge Nile crocodiles wait in the water. [Nile crocodile | Crocodylus niloticus]
Thousands of wildebeest crowd onto the banks, then suddenly leap in all at once.
Many drown or are caught.
Yet they keep going.
About half a million calves are born each year within a few weeks.
Many can stand within *minutes* of birth, and run with the herd soon after.
It's the biggest movement of land animals on the planet.
