Australia built the longest fence in the world, to keep out *dingoes*.
The *Dingo Fence* stretches over *5,000 kilometers* across south-eastern Australia.
It was built to protect sheep from these wild dogs. [sheep | Ovis aries]
Dingoes arrived in Australia thousands of years ago, possibly with ancient seafarers.
Unlike most dogs, they rarely bark. They *howl*.
They can rotate their wrists, letting them climb and even open some doors and gates.
They can turn their heads almost *180 degrees* backwards.
Dingoes are Australia's largest land predator.
They hunt kangaroos, wallabies, rabbits, and even *feral cats* and foxes.
Some scientists think dingoes help protect native animals by keeping those invasive predators in check.
Dingoes usually breed only once a year, unlike pet dogs which can breed twice.
On K'gari island, dingoes are protected, but visitors must stay alert.
A wild dog with an ancient story.
