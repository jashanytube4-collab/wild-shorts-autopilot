This animal punches so hard it can *crack aquarium glass*.
Meet the *mantis shrimp*.
It's only about the size of your hand, but its club-like arms strike at over *80 kilometers per hour*.
The whole punch is over in less than *three thousandths of a second*.
It's so fast it rips the water apart, creating bubbles that collapse with a flash of *light* and *heat*.
So even if it misses, the *shockwave* can still stun its prey.
Snails, crabs, clams, it smashes straight through their shells.
And its eyes are just as wild.
Humans see color using *three* types of color receptors.
The mantis shrimp has *twelve*.
It can see *ultraviolet* and even *polarized light*, which is invisible to us.
Each eye moves on its own, and each one can judge depth by itself.
And despite the name, it isn't really a shrimp.
It belongs to an ancient group that has been smashing things for over *300 million years*.
