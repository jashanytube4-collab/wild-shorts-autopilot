This eagle used to be called the *monkey-eating eagle*.
Meet the *Philippine eagle*, one of the largest and most powerful eagles in the world.
Its scientific name, *Pithecophaga*, literally means *monkey eater*. [long-tailed macaque | Macaca fascicularis]
It does hunt monkeys, but also flying lemurs, civets, snakes and hornbills.
Its shaggy crest of feathers makes it look like a *lion*.
And its blue-grey eyes can spot prey from far away.
Pairs mate for life, and raise just *one chick* every two years.
The chick stays with its parents for over a year.
Each pair needs a huge territory of rainforest to survive.
Sadly, it's *critically endangered*, with only a few hundred pairs left.
In the Philippines, killing one can mean years in *prison*.
It's the country's national bird.
A rare, regal hunter of the rainforest.
