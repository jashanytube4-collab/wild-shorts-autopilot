This scorpion's venom is one of the most expensive liquids on Earth.
Meet the *deathstalker*.
It lives in the deserts of *North Africa* and the *Middle East*.
It's a pale yellow and only about *eight centimeters* long, but it's one of the most dangerous scorpions in the world.
Its sting causes extreme pain, fever, and in children or the elderly, sometimes *death*.
So why would anyone pay for its venom?
Because scientists found one of its toxins latches onto *brain tumor* cells.
Researchers turned it into a glowing dye that helps surgeons see *cancer* during surgery.
Each scorpion produces only a tiny drop of venom at a time.
That's why a liter, if anyone collected that much, would cost *millions of dollars*.
And like most scorpions, it glows under *ultraviolet light*. [scorpion | Scorpiones]
Small, pale and deadly, yet it might help *save lives*.
