title: The Animal That Head-Butts For Hours
subject: bighorn sheep | Ovis canadensis
mood: epic
tags: bighorn sheep, head butting, rocky mountains, rams, wildlife
---
Two male bighorn sheep can slam their heads together at over *30 kilometers per hour*.
The crack can be heard over a *kilometer* away.
These battles can last for *hours*, until one ram gives up.
So how don't they get brain damage?
Their skulls have a double layer of bone with spongy, honeycomb-like layers, acting like a *shock absorber*.
Their horns can weigh around *14 kilograms*, more than all the bones in their body combined.
The horns grow throughout their life, and the rings can be counted to estimate their *age*.
Bighorn sheep live in the mountains and canyons of western North America.
Their hooves have rough soles that grip steep, rocky slopes.
Scientists have studied their skulls to design better *helmets*.
In the 1800s, there were millions of them.
Hunting and diseases from domestic sheep made them rare, but they're making a comeback.
Hard heads, and a big comeback.
