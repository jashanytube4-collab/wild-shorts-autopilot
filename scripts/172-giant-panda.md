title: Pandas Eat For 14 Hours A Day
subject: giant panda | Ailuropoda melanoleuca
mood: curious
tags: giant panda, bamboo, china, bears, cute animals
---
A giant panda can spend up to *14 hours* a day eating.
And almost all of it is *bamboo*.
The problem? Pandas have the digestive system of a *carnivore*.
Their bodies only absorb a small fraction of what they eat.
So they need to eat around *12 to 38 kilograms* of bamboo every single day.
To save energy, they move slowly and rest a lot.
Pandas are *bears*, and they still occasionally eat meat in the wild.
A newborn panda is about the size of a stick of *butter*, around 1/900th of its mother's weight.
That's one of the smallest newborns, compared to its mother, of any mammal.
Pandas have a special wrist bone that works like a *thumb* to hold bamboo.
And they do handstands to scent-mark trees, aiming their pee as high as possible to seem *bigger*.
In 2016, giant pandas were moved from *endangered* to *vulnerable*, thanks to protection.
