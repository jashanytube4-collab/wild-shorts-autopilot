This eagle may have hunted our *ancient ancestors*.
In 1924, scientists found the skull of a young human ancestor in South Africa, called the *Taung Child*.
It had strange marks on its eye sockets.
Years later, scientists compared them to marks left by modern *crowned eagles* on monkey skulls.
They matched.
The *African crowned eagle* is one of the most powerful eagles on Earth.
It regularly hunts *monkeys*, and even small antelopes many times its own weight. [vervet monkey | Chlorocebus pygerythrus]
It drops from the treetops, crushing prey with its huge talons.
If prey is too big to carry, it may tear it into pieces and carry them back one by one.
Monkeys have special alarm calls just for crowned eagles.
Young eagles stay with their parents for almost a year.
An ancient predator that may have shaped our *evolution*.
