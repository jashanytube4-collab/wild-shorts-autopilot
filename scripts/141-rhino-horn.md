title: Rhino Horn Is Made Of The Same Stuff As Your Nails
subject: white rhinoceros | Ceratotherium simum
mood: curious
tags: rhino, rhinoceros, poaching, africa, endangered
---
A rhino's horn is made of the same material as your *fingernails*.
It's called *keratin*.
Yet poachers kill rhinos for it, because some people wrongly believe it can cure diseases.
Science says it has *no proven medical value*.
The *white rhino* is the second-largest land mammal, after the elephant.
It can weigh over *two tons*.
Despite that size, it can charge at around *50 kilometers per hour*.
Rhinos have poor eyesight, but excellent hearing and smell.
They communicate using *dung piles*, leaving messages for each other about who's in the area.
And here's a sad story.
The *northern white rhino* is now down to just *two* living animals, both female.
Scientists are trying to save it using frozen cells and *IVF*, with southern white rhinos as surrogate mothers.
And the name "white" might come from a mistranslation of the word *wide*, describing its mouth.
