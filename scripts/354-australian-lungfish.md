title: The Fish That's Older Than Dinosaurs
subject: Australian lungfish | Neoceratodus forsteri
mood: curious
tags: australian lungfish, living fossil, fish, australia, oldest aquarium fish
---
The oldest fish living in an aquarium is around *90 years old*.
Her name is *Methuselah*, an *Australian lungfish*.
She arrived at the California Academy of Sciences in *1938*.
Australian lungfish are a true *living fossil*.
Their family has barely changed in around *100 million years*.
As their name suggests, they have a *lung*.
They can gulp air at the surface when the water is low in oxygen.
Their genome is one of the largest of any animal, around *14 times* bigger than ours.
Scientists study them to understand how fish first moved onto *land*.
They're one of our distant fish cousins.
Methuselah is said to enjoy fresh figs, and gentle belly rubs from her keepers.
In the wild, lungfish live in just a few rivers in Queensland, Australia.
A fish that has watched the world change for millions of years.
