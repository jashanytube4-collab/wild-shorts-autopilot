Why does every animal seem to *love* the capybara?
Birds stand on its back. Monkeys ride it. Even ducklings and turtles nap beside it.
The *capybara* is the largest rodent in the world, weighing up to *65 kilograms*, basically a giant guinea pig.
It's so calm that other animals treat it like *furniture*.
But it's also built for water.
Its feet are slightly *webbed*, and it can stay underwater for up to *five minutes* to hide from jaguars.
Like many rodents, its teeth *never stop growing*.
Capybaras also eat their own *poop*, a second pass to get all the nutrients out of tough grass.
In Venezuela, there's a tale that centuries ago, the Church allowed capybara to be eaten during *Lent*, as if it were a *fish*.
And in Japan, zoos run warm *hot spring baths* for capybaras in winter.
Honestly, we should all be more like the capybara.
