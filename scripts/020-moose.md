title: Why You Should Never Approach A Moose
subject: moose | Alces alces
mood: danger
tags: moose, dangerous animals, alaska, canada, deer
---
In Alaska, *moose* injure more people than *bears* do.
The moose is the largest deer in the world.
A big male can stand over *two meters* tall at the shoulder and weigh around *700 kilograms*.
His antlers can stretch almost *two meters* wide.
And here's the crazy part: he grows a new set *every year*.
Moose look calm, even goofy.
But when they feel threatened, especially mothers with calves, they *charge*.
And when they catch you, they stomp with their sharp *front hooves*.
Warning signs? Ears pinned back, raised hair on the hump, and *licking their lips*.
They're also surprisingly good *swimmers*.
Moose can dive several meters down to eat water plants, and stay under for close to a *minute*.
And they can run at around *55 kilometers per hour*.
So if you ever meet one, give it *plenty of space*.
