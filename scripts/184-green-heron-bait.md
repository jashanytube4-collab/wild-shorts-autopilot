This bird goes *fishing* using bait.
Meet the *green heron*.
It drops small objects, like *bread*, insects, or feathers onto the surface of the water.
Then it waits.
When a curious fish swims up to investigate, the heron *strikes*.
It's one of the few birds known to use tools to hunt.
Some green herons have even been seen picking up bread from people feeding ducks, and using it as bait.
They also adjust the bait, moving it if fish don't come.
Green herons are small, stocky birds, often hiding in reeds.
They can stretch their neck out *fast*, like a spring, to grab fish.
And they have a striking chestnut-colored neck, and bright yellow or orange legs.
The striated heron, a close relative in Asia and Africa, does the same trick. [striated heron | Butorides striata]
Patience, cleverness, and a good bit of bread.
