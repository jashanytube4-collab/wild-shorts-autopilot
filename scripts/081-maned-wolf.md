title: The Wolf That Isn't A Wolf
subject: maned wolf | Chrysocyon brachyurus
mood: curious
tags: maned wolf, fox on stilts, brazil, weird animals, south america
---
It looks like a *fox on stilts*.
Meet the *maned wolf*, the tallest wild canid in the world.
But despite its name, it isn't a wolf, and it isn't a fox either.
It's the only member of its own *genus*.
Those long legs help it see over the tall grass of the South American savanna.
Half of its diet is *fruit*, especially one called the *wolf apple*.
And by spreading its seeds, it helps that plant grow.
It's mostly *solitary* and hunts small animals at night.
Its pee smells so strongly that it's often compared to *marijuana*.
In fact, a zoo in the Netherlands once had police called because visitors thought someone was smoking it.
And it makes a loud, deep call called a *roar-bark*.
In Brazil, its face is printed on the *200 reais* banknote.
Graceful, odd, and totally unique.
