title: Orcas Hunt Great White Sharks
subject: orca | Orcinus orca

There's one animal *great white sharks* are terrified of.
And it's the *orca*.
Off South Africa, a pair of orcas nicknamed *Port* and *Starboard* hunted great whites.
They tore open the sharks and ate only one thing: the *liver*, which is rich in fat.
After they appeared, great whites fled the area for *months*.
Orcas, also called killer whales, aren't actually whales. They're the largest member of the *dolphin* family.
They hunt in family groups, and every group has its own *techniques*.
Some make waves to wash *seals* off ice floes. [Weddell seal | Leptonychotes weddellii]
Others beach themselves on purpose to snatch sea lions from the shore.
Different groups even have their own *dialects*, calls passed down through generations.
They're led by the oldest females, who can live past *80 years*.
And orcas are the only ones that hunt *great whites* like this.
At the top of the food chain, there's no one higher.
