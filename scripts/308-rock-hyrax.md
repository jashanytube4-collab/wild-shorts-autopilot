This little animal's closest living relatives include the *elephant*. [African bush elephant | Loxodonta africana]
Meet the *rock hyrax*.
It looks like a big guinea pig, but it isn't a rodent at all.
Hyraxes have tusk-like front teeth, and toenails a bit like elephants.
They live in groups on rocky hillsides in Africa and the Middle East.
They spend hours sunbathing on rocks, because they're not good at controlling their body temperature.
Their feet have rubbery, sweaty pads that work like *suction cups*, helping them climb steep rocks.
Males sing complex songs, with patterns a bit like *language*.
Hyraxes share a communal toilet, called a *midden*.
Over thousands of years, it hardens into a substance called *hyraceum*.
Scientists use it to study ancient climates.
And it's even been used in traditional medicine and *perfume*.
A tiny cousin of a giant.
