The deadliest animal on Earth isn't a shark, a snake, or a lion.
It's the *mosquito*.
Mosquitoes spread diseases like *malaria*, dengue and yellow fever.
Malaria alone kills over *half a million* people every year, mostly children.
That's far more than all other animal attacks combined.
Only female mosquitoes bite.
They need the protein in blood to make their *eggs*.
Males drink nectar.
Mosquitoes find you by sensing the *carbon dioxide* you breathe out, along with your body heat and smell.
Some people really are mosquito magnets, because of the chemicals on their *skin*.
A mosquito's wings beat around *500 times* a second, which makes that annoying whine.
Scientists are fighting back with bed nets, vaccines, and even mosquitoes infected with bacteria that stop them spreading disease.
The smallest animals can be the most *dangerous*.
