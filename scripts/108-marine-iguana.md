title: The Only Lizard That Swims In The Ocean
subject: marine iguana | Amblyrhynchus cristatus

This is the only lizard in the world that feeds in the *ocean*.
Meet the *marine iguana* of the *Galápagos Islands*.
It dives into cold seawater to eat *algae* off underwater rocks.
Big males can dive over *ten meters* deep and stay down for long stretches.
But eating seaweed means swallowing a lot of *salt*.
So it has special glands near its nose, and it *sneezes* the salt out.
That's why their heads are often crusted in white.
After diving, they're cold, so they bask on black lava rocks to warm back up.
Here's the most incredible part.
During food shortages, like El Niño years, marine iguanas can actually *shrink*.
Their bodies get shorter, partly by absorbing *bone*.
When food returns, they grow back.
*Charles Darwin* called them "imps of darkness," but they're a perfect example of how life adapts.
