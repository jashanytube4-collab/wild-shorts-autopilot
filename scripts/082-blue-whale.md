title: How Big Is A Blue Whale, Really?
subject: blue whale | Balaenoptera musculus
mood: epic
tags: blue whale, largest animal, whales, ocean, animal facts
---
This is the largest animal that has *ever lived*.
Bigger than any dinosaur.
A *blue whale* can grow around *30 meters* long and weigh up to *150 tons* or more.
Its tongue alone can weigh as much as an *elephant*.
Its heart is about the size of a small *car*, and beats as slowly as *two times per minute* during a dive.
Yet it feeds on some of the smallest animals in the ocean, tiny shrimp-like creatures called *krill*. [Antarctic krill | Euphausia superba]
In one gulp, it can swallow a mouthful of water bigger than its own body, then push the water out through bristles called *baleen*.
A single blue whale can eat millions of krill a *day*.
Its calls are among the loudest sounds made by any animal, and can travel *hundreds of kilometers* underwater.
Hunting nearly wiped them out in the twentieth century.
Today they're protected, and slowly coming back.
