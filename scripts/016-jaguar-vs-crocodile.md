In South America, there's a cat that hunts *crocodiles*.
Meet the *jaguar*, the biggest cat in the Americas.
Most big cats kill by biting the *throat*.
The jaguar goes straight for the *skull*.
For its size, it has the strongest bite of any big cat, strong enough to crunch through *turtle shells*.
In Brazil's Pantanal wetlands, jaguars stalk *caimans* basking on the riverbank. [yacare caiman | Caiman yacare]
They slip into the water, then launch, biting the back of the caiman's head.
The fight is usually over in *seconds*.
And unlike most cats, jaguars *love water*.
They're powerful swimmers that hunt fish and turtles.
Its name is often said to come from a Native word meaning *he who kills with one leap*.
Every jaguar's coat is covered in rosettes, many with small spots in the middle, a pattern as unique as a *fingerprint*.
And some jaguars are so dark they look *completely black*, but look closely, and the spots are still there.
