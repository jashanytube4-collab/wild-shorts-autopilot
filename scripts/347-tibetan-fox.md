This fox looks like it has a *square face*.
Meet the *Tibetan fox*, from the high plateaus of Tibet.
Its face looks wide and boxy, thanks to thick fur on its cheeks.
That fur keeps it warm in the freezing, windy mountains.
Its small, narrow eyes give it an unimpressed look.
Tibetan foxes live at heights of over *3,500 meters*.
They mainly eat *pikas*, small furry animals related to rabbits. [plateau pika | Ochotona curzoniae]
They often follow brown bears as the bears dig for pikas, catching the ones that escape.
They're shy, and spend much of their time in burrows.
Their unusual faces have made them an *internet favorite*.
People share their photos as a perfect "meh" or "unimpressed" reaction.
A fox that always looks like it's judging you.
