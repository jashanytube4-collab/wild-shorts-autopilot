In the 1980s, a beluga whale started making sounds like *human speech*.
His name was *Noc*.
Divers at a US Navy program heard what sounded like people talking underwater.
One diver came up asking, "Who told me to get out?"
It was Noc, mimicking human voices.
Belugas are so chatty that sailors nicknamed them the *canaries of the sea*.
They whistle, chirp, click, and squeal.
That bulge on their forehead is called a *melon*, and they can change its shape to focus their *sonar*.
Unlike most whales, belugas can turn their heads, because their neck bones aren't fused.
They're born *grey*, and turn white as they grow older, blending in with the *Arctic ice*.
Each summer, thousands gather in river mouths to molt their skin.
They rub against gravel on the riverbed to scrub off the old layer.
The ocean's chattiest whale.
