This animal kills more people in Africa than *lions* do.
And it's a *vegetarian*.
Meet the *hippopotamus*.
An adult male can weigh over *three tons*, yet it can outrun a human, hitting around *30 kilometers per hour*.
Its jaws open almost *150 degrees*, wide enough to fit a small person inside.
And its tusk-like teeth can grow *half a meter* long.
Hippos are fiercely *territorial*, and they'll charge boats that drift too close.
Here's a strange one: despite living in water, hippos *can't actually swim*.
They walk and bounce along the river bottom, holding their breath for about *five minutes*.
They also sweat a *red liquid* that works like natural *sunscreen*.
And their closest living relatives? Not pigs or horses.
It's *whales*. [humpback whale | Megaptera novaeangliae]
So next time you see a hippo yawning, remember, that's not a yawn.
It's a *warning*.
