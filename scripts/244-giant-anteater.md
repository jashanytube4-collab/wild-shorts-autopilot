This animal can eat around *30,000 ants* in a single day.
Meet the *giant anteater*.
It has no teeth.
Instead, it has a tongue that can stretch around *60 centimeters*, covered in tiny spines and sticky saliva.
It flicks it in and out up to around *150 times* a minute.
It only stays at each ant nest for about a minute, before ants start stinging.
That way, it never destroys the nest, and can come back later.
It has huge front claws, up to around *ten centimeters* long.
It walks on its knuckles to keep them sharp.
And those claws are dangerous.
Giant anteaters can defend themselves against *jaguars*. [jaguar | Panthera onca]
Babies ride on their mother's back, and their stripes line up with hers, making them almost invisible.
Giant anteaters have one of the lowest body temperatures of any land mammal.
A long nose, a longer tongue, and serious claws.
