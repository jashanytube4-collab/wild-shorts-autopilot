title: Raccoons Are Smarter Than You Think
subject: raccoon | Procyon lotor
mood: curious
tags: raccoon, trash panda, smart animals, cities, north america
---
Raccoons can remember how to solve puzzles for up to *three years*.
These "trash pandas" are incredibly clever.
In experiments, they've opened complex locks and latches, often in just a few tries.
Raccoon hands have five long fingers, and they're extremely *sensitive*.
They often seem to wash their food, which is how they got the name *lotor*, meaning "washer" in Latin.
But they're not really cleaning it.
Getting their paws wet may make their sense of touch even *stronger*.
In cities like Toronto, raccoons have become champions at raiding garbage bins.
The city designed new "raccoon-proof" bins, and some raccoons still figured them out.
Raccoons can also run, swim, and climb down trees *head first*, by turning their back feet around.
They're found from Canada to Panama, and were introduced to Europe and Japan.
Those masked faces really do suit them.
