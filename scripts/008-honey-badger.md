This animal picks fights with *lions*, and walks away.
Meet the *honey badger*, once named the *most fearless animal* in the world by Guinness World Records.
It weighs about as much as a medium dog, around *12 kilograms*.
But its secret weapon is its *skin*.
It's thick, rubbery and so loose that if a predator grabs it, the badger can twist around inside it and *bite back*.
Bee stings barely slow it down, so it tears open hives to eat the honey and the *larvae*.
It hunts *cobras* and *puff adders*.
One honey badger was filmed collapsing after a snake bite, sleeping for two hours, then *waking up to finish its meal*.
In South Africa, a captive honey badger named *Stoffel* became famous for escaping every enclosure built for him.
He stacked rocks, used rakes, and even *rolled mud into balls* to climb the walls.
Size isn't everything. *Attitude* is.
