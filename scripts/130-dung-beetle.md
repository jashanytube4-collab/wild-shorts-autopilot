title: The Beetle That Navigates Using The Milky Way
subject: dung beetle | Scarabaeus satyrus
mood: curious

This beetle uses the *Milky Way* to find its way.
It's a *dung beetle*.
It rolls balls of animal poop, sometimes many times its own weight.
To escape competitors, it needs to roll in a straight line.
And at night, scientists found something amazing.
Some dung beetles use the glow of the Milky Way to keep their *direction*.
In an experiment, researchers put tiny *hats* on beetles to block their view of the sky.
The beetles got lost, rolling in circles.
They're the first animals known to navigate by the *galaxy*.
Dung beetles are also incredibly strong.
One species can pull over *1,000 times* its own body weight.
That's like a person pulling six *double-decker buses* full of people.
Ancient Egyptians saw them as sacred, linked with the god *Khepri*, who rolled the sun across the sky.
Poop rollers, and astronauts too.
