title: The Living Fossil With A Third Eye
subject: tuatara | Sphenodon punctatus
mood: curious
tags: tuatara, living fossil, new zealand, reptiles, third eye

This animal looks like a lizard, but it isn't one.
Meet the *tuatara* from New Zealand.
It's the last surviving member of a group of reptiles that lived alongside the *dinosaurs*.
All its relatives died out around *60 million* years ago.
Tuataras have a *third eye* on top of their head.
It's covered with skin in adults, but it can sense *light*, and may help set their body clock.
Their teeth aren't separate. They're bony points of the *jaw* itself.
Tuataras grow incredibly slowly, and can live over *100 years*.
One male at a New Zealand museum, named *Henry*, became a father for the first time at around *111 years old*.
They can stay active at temperatures that would make most reptiles sluggish.
And they often share burrows with *seabirds*.
A living window into the age of the dinosaurs.
