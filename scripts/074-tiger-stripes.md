If you shaved a tiger, it would still have *stripes*.
The stripes aren't just on its fur, they're on its *skin*.
And just like fingerprints, no two tigers have the same pattern.
The *tiger* is the largest wild cat in the world.
Some Siberian tigers can weigh over *300 kilograms*.
It can eat around *35 kilograms* of meat in a single meal.
Unlike most cats, tigers *love water*, swimming several kilometers, and cooling off in rivers on hot days.
They hunt alone, usually at dusk or night, stalking prey to within a few meters, then *pouncing*.
But they fail far more than they succeed, maybe *nine times out of ten*.
Orange might seem easy to spot, but deer don't see colors the way we do. [chital | Axis axis]
To them, an orange tiger looks *green*, blending into the grass.
Thanks to protection in countries like *India*, wild tiger numbers have finally started to *rise* again.
