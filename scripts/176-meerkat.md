Meerkats take turns being the *lookout*.
While the group digs for food, one meerkat stands guard on a high spot.
It watches the sky for eagles and the ground for jackals. [black-backed jackal | Lupulella mesomelas]
And it uses different alarm calls for different dangers.
One call means "eagle, hide underground."
Another means "snake, gather together."
Meerkats live in the deserts of southern Africa, in groups called *mobs*.
And they teach.
Adults bring live scorpions to pups, often removing the *stinger* first, so the young can practice.
As the pups get better, adults give them more dangerous prey.
Meerkats have dark patches around their eyes, which work like *sunglasses* against the glare.
And they're immune to some types of *venom*.
But meerkat life isn't always cute: the dominant female sometimes kills the pups of others.
Teamwork, with a dark side.
