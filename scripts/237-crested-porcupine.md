This rodent can kill a *lion*. [lion | Panthera leo]
Meet the *crested porcupine*.
It's covered in thousands of sharp, hollow *quills*, some over *30 centimeters* long.
When threatened, it raises them and rattles its hollow tail quills like a *warning siren*.
If that doesn't work, it doesn't shoot its quills.
That's a myth.
Instead, it runs *backwards* into the attacker.
The quills detach easily, and stick into the predator's face, paws, or mouth.
They're hard to remove, and wounds can get infected.
Lions and leopards have been found dead or starving, with porcupine quills stuck in their faces.
Some big cats that became man-eaters were found to have old quill injuries, which may have stopped them hunting normal prey.
Porcupines are mostly *vegetarian*, eating roots, bark and fruit.
Don't mess with the spiky one.
