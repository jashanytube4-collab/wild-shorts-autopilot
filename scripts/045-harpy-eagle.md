This eagle snatches *monkeys* and *sloths* straight out of the trees.
Meet the *harpy eagle*, one of the most powerful birds of prey on Earth.
Its back talons can grow around *12 centimeters* long, about the size of a *grizzly bear's claws*.
It can grip with enough force to crush bones.
Its wings are short and broad, letting it weave between branches in thick rainforest at around *80 kilometers per hour*.
And its favorite meal? *Sloths*. [brown-throated sloth | Bradypus variegatus]
It also hunts monkeys, opossums, and porcupines.
When it's alert, the feathers on its head rise up into a *crown*, and its face feathers may help funnel sound to its ears.
Harpy eagles mate for life.
They raise just *one chick* every two to three years, feeding it for over a year.
It's named after the *harpies* of Greek myth, half-woman, half-bird monsters.
And honestly, it looks the part.
