Can cockroaches really survive a *nuclear bomb*?
Not exactly.
They can survive much more *radiation* than humans, around *ten times* more in some tests.
But some insects, like certain *fruit flies* and wasps, can handle even more.
And no cockroach would survive the blast or heat near the explosion.
Still, they're incredibly tough.
A cockroach can live for around a *week* without its head.
It breathes through small holes in its body, so it doesn't need its head to breathe.
It eventually dies of *thirst*.
They can hold their breath for around *40 minutes*.
And they can squeeze through a gap about as thin as a *coin*, by flattening their bodies.
The American cockroach can run at about *50 body lengths per second*, one of the fastest insects for its size.
Cockroaches have been around for over *300 million years*.
They were here before the *dinosaurs*.
