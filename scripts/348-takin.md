This strange animal may have inspired the myth of the *Golden Fleece*.
Meet the *takin*, from the eastern Himalayas.
It looks like a mix of a moose, a goat, and a bison.
Golden takins have shiny, golden coats, which some say may have inspired the Greek legend.
Takins have a large, arched nose, which helps warm the freezing air before it reaches their lungs.
They secrete an oily substance from their skin, which works like a natural *raincoat*.
In Bhutan, the takin is the national animal.
Legend says a famous holy man, the Divine Madman, created it by sticking a goat's head onto a cow's skeleton.
Takins can weigh up to around *350 kilograms*.
And they move in herds, sometimes of up to a hundred animals in summer.
They're strong climbers, and can stand on their back legs to reach leaves.
Sadly, they're now *vulnerable*, threatened by habitat loss and hunting.
A golden mountain mystery.
