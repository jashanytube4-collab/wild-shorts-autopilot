This bird isn't an *owl*, and it pretends to be a *branch*.
Meet the *tawny frogmouth*, from Australia.
It looks like an owl, but it's more closely related to nightjars.
During the day, it sits still on a tree branch.
When threatened, it stretches its body, points its beak up, and closes its eyes to slits.
Its grey and brown feathers blend in perfectly, so it looks like a *broken branch*.
At night, it hunts insects, worms, and small animals.
It has a huge, wide *mouth*, like a frog.
That's where its name comes from.
Frogmouths often sit with their mouths open, and insects fly right in.
Pairs stay together for life, and often perch side by side.
They became famous online for their grumpy *faces*.
A master of disguise, with a permanent frown.
