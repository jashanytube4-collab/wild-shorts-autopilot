title: Puffins Can Carry 10 Fish At Once
subject: Atlantic puffin | Fratercula arctica
mood: curious
tags: puffin, seabirds, iceland, cute animals, birds
---
A puffin can carry over *ten fish* in its beak at once.
The record? Over *60* small fish in a single beakful.
How? Its tongue pins the fish against spines on the roof of its mouth, so it can keep catching more without *dropping* any.
Meet the *Atlantic puffin*, sometimes called the *clown of the sea*.
That colorful beak isn't permanent.
In winter, the outer layer *sheds*, and the beak becomes smaller and duller.
Scientists also found that puffin beaks *glow* under ultraviolet light.
Puffins beat their wings up to around *400 times* a minute.
They're also great swimmers, diving up to around *60 meters* deep.
They return to the same burrow, and the same partner, year after year.
In Iceland, some children save young puffins that get lost in town lights, and release them back over the *sea*.
A tiny seabird with a big, glowing personality.
