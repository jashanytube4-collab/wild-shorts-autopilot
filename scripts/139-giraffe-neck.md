title: Giraffes Fight With Their Necks
subject: giraffe | Giraffa camelopardalis
mood: curious
tags: giraffe, tallest animal, africa, necking, animal facts
---
Male giraffes fight by swinging their *necks* like hammers.
It's called *necking*.
They swing their heads, which can weigh around *ten kilograms* or more, and slam them into each other's bodies.
Fights can be so violent that a giraffe gets knocked out cold.
Giraffes are the tallest animals on Earth, reaching up to *5.5 meters*.
Yet their necks have the same number of bones as ours: just *seven*.
Each one can be over *25 centimeters* long.
Their hearts have to pump blood all the way up to the brain.
So their blood pressure is around *twice* as high as ours.
Their tongues can be around *50 centimeters* long, and dark in color, which may protect them from *sunburn*.
They sleep very little, often just a few hours a day, in short naps.
And baby giraffes start life with a *two-meter drop*, because mothers give birth standing up.
Welcome to the world, little one.
