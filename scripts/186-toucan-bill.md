Why does the toucan have such a *giant bill*?
For the *toco toucan*, the bill can make up around *one third* of its body length.
But it's surprisingly *light*.
It's made of keratin, with a honeycomb of bone and air inside.
So what's it for?
Scientists discovered it works like a *radiator*.
Toucans can send more blood to the bill to release heat when they're hot.
And reduce blood flow to keep warm when it's cold.
It also helps them reach fruit at the end of thin branches.
Toucans toss fruit into the air and catch it in their throats.
Their tongues are long and thin, with frayed edges like a *feather*.
They sleep by turning their heads backwards and tucking their bills under their *wings*.
And they live in small flocks, playing by "fencing" with their bills.
A built-in air conditioner, with style.
