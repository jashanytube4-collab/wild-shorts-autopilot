title: The Only Venomous Primate
subject: Sunda slow loris | Nycticebus coucang
mood: danger
tags: slow loris, venomous primate, cute animals, asia, primates
---
This adorable animal is the only *venomous* primate in the world.
Meet the *slow loris*.
On the inside of its elbows, it has small glands that produce an oily liquid.
When threatened, it licks those glands, mixing the oil with its saliva.
That turns its bite *venomous*.
A bite can cause intense pain, swelling, and in rare cases, a dangerous allergic shock in humans.
Slow lorises mostly use this venom to fight *each other*.
When threatened, it raises its arms above its head, perhaps to look like a *cobra*.
And those big eyes? They help it see at night, when it hunts insects and sips tree sap.
Sadly, videos of "cute" lorises being tickled made them popular as pets.
But scientists say the raised arms are actually a sign of *fear*.
Many pet lorises have their teeth pulled out, and suffer badly.
Please don't share loris "tickle" videos.
