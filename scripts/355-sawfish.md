title: The Fish With A Chainsaw For A Nose
subject: sawfish | Pristidae
mood: danger
tags: sawfish, rays, ocean, endangered, weird fish
---
This fish has a *chainsaw* for a nose.
Meet the *sawfish*.
Its long, flat snout is lined on both sides with tooth-like spikes.
It's called a *rostrum*.
And it's covered in sensors that detect the electric fields of hidden fish.
The sawfish swings its saw from side to side, slashing through schools of fish.
Then it pins them to the seafloor and eats them.
Sawfish are actually *rays*, not sharks.
Some species can grow over *six meters* long.
Sadly, those saws get tangled easily in fishing nets.
Today, sawfish are among the most endangered fish in the world.
And scientists discovered that female sawfish in Florida can have babies *without a male*.
It's called virgin birth, and it was the first time it was found in a wild vertebrate.
A living chainsaw, fighting to survive.
