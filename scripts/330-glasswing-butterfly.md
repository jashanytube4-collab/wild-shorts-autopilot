This butterfly has wings like *glass*.
Meet the *glasswing butterfly*, from Central and South America.
Its wings are almost completely *transparent*, except for a thin border.
That makes it hard for birds to spot it as it flies through the forest.
How do its wings stay clear?
They're covered in tiny pillars of different sizes that stop light from *reflecting*.
That's an anti-glare effect, like the coating on good glasses.
Scientists are copying the design for phone screens and solar panels.
Glasswing caterpillars feed on toxic plants.
The toxins stay in the adult's body, making it taste bad to predators.
Males gather in groups, called leks, to attract females.
A butterfly you can almost see right through.
