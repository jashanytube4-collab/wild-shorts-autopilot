title: The Ostrich's Eye Is Bigger Than Its Brain
subject: common ostrich | Struthio camelus
mood: curious
tags: ostrich, biggest bird, africa, birds, fastest bird
---
An ostrich's eye is bigger than its *brain*.
At around *five centimeters* across, it's the largest eye of any land animal.
The *ostrich* is the biggest bird alive, standing up to *2.7 meters* tall and weighing over *100 kilograms*.
It can't fly, but it can run at around *70 kilometers per hour*, faster than any other bird on land.
It has only *two toes* on each foot, and one has a thick claw that can deliver a dangerous *kick*.
And no, ostriches don't bury their heads in the sand.
That myth may come from the way they lower their heads to turn their eggs in their ground nests.
Their eggs are the biggest of any living bird, equal to around *24 chicken eggs*.
The shell is so strong that an adult human can *stand* on it without breaking it.
Giant eyes, tiny brain, and a kick you never want to meet.
