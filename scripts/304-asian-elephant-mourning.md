title: Elephants Mourn Their Dead
subject: Asian elephant | Elephas maximus
mood: curious
tags: asian elephant, elephants, emotions, india, smart animals
---
Elephants appear to *mourn* their dead.
In India, scientists found records of Asian elephants burying their dead calves in drainage ditches.
They found calves carefully placed on their backs, with legs pointing up, covered in soil.
Elephants also return to the bones of dead family members, touching them gently with their trunks.
Elephants are among the smartest animals on Earth.
They recognize themselves in *mirrors*.
In Africa, elephants were even shown to tell human *languages* apart, reacting more to groups that hunted them.
Asian elephants are smaller than African elephants, with smaller ears.
And only some males grow tusks.
They use their trunks for breathing, smelling, drinking, and grabbing things.
An elephant can suck up around *eight liters* of water in its trunk at once.
Asian elephants are *endangered*, with fewer than *50,000* left in the wild.
Gentle giants with deep feelings.
