title: Lemurs Have Stink Fights
subject: ring-tailed lemur | Lemur catta
mood: curious

Ring-tailed lemurs settle arguments with *stink fights*.
Males rub their long, striped tails on scent glands on their wrists.
Then they wave their tails at each other, wafting the smell toward their rival.
The one who backs off first *loses*.
Ring-tailed lemurs live only on the island of *Madagascar*.
Their groups are led by *females*, who often get first pick of food.
Every morning, they sit in the sun with their arms spread wide, like a *yoga pose*.
They're warming up after the cold night.
They spend more time on the ground than most lemurs, walking with their tails held high like *flags*.
That helps the group stay together.
Their tails have around *13* black and white rings.
And they can't hang from them like monkeys do.
Sun worshippers, stink fighters, and female-led.
