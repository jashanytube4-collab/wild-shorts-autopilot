This insect digs through soil like a *mole*.
Meet the *mole cricket*.
Its front legs are shaped like little shovels, just like a mole's *paws*. [European mole | Talpa europaea]
It uses them to dig tunnels underground, where it eats roots, insects and worms.
Mole crickets can also swim and even *fly*.
But the strangest thing is how they sing.
Males build special burrows shaped like a megaphone.
Then they sing at the opening, and the burrow *amplifies* their song.
Some species can be heard from hundreds of meters away.
Females follow the sound to find a mate.
Some mole crickets are crop pests, damaging lawns and golf courses.
Scientists even used a parasitic wasp and a nematode worm to control them in Florida.
