title: Reindeer Eyes Change Color In Winter
subject: reindeer | Rangifer tarandus
mood: curious
tags: reindeer, caribou, arctic, christmas, animal facts
---
A reindeer's eyes change *color* with the seasons.
In summer, the back of their eyes reflects *golden* light.
In the long, dark Arctic winter, it shifts to *deep blue*.
That helps them see better in the dim light.
Reindeer can also see *ultraviolet* light.
In the snow, things like lichen and predator fur stand out in UV, while snow reflects it.
Reindeer are the only deer species where *both males and females* grow antlers.
And here's the Christmas twist.
Males shed their antlers in early winter.
Females keep theirs until spring.
So Santa's reindeer, with antlers in December, might be *females*.
Their knees make a *clicking* sound as they walk, which may help the herd stay together in blizzards.
Some herds migrate thousands of kilometers each year, among the longest land migrations on Earth.
