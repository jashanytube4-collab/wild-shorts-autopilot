When threatened, this animal *shoots out its guts*.
Meet the *sea cucumber*.
Some species can push their internal organs out through their rear end.
The sticky organs can tangle up and confuse predators.
Then, over a few weeks, the sea cucumber *regrows* them.
Sea cucumbers are related to starfish and sea urchins.
They crawl along the seafloor, eating sand and mud, and digesting tiny bits of food.
In doing so, they clean the seafloor, like *vacuum cleaners*.
Some even breathe through their *rear end*.
And some tiny fish, called *pearlfish*, live inside them, in their rear end. [pearlfish | Carapus]
Sea cucumbers can also turn their bodies from firm to soft, almost *liquid*, and back.
In parts of Asia, they're considered a *delicacy*, and some sell for very high prices.
Weird, wonderful, and surprisingly useful.
