Pigs might be smarter than *dogs*.
In experiments, pigs have learned to play simple *video games*, using joysticks with their snouts.
They can recognize themselves in a *mirror*, and even use it to find hidden food.
The wild ancestor of the pig is the *wild boar*.
It lives across Europe, Asia and North Africa, and has spread to the Americas and Australia.
Wild boars are tough, with thick skin and sharp *tusks*.
They can run at around *40 kilometers per hour* and swim across rivers.
They have an amazing sense of smell.
In France and Italy, pigs were traditionally used to sniff out *truffles*, buried underground.
Wild boars roll in mud to keep cool, because they can't sweat much.
In the city of Berlin, thousands of wild boars roam the parks and gardens.
And in Fukushima, wild boars moved into abandoned towns after the nuclear disaster.
Clever, adaptable, and often misunderstood.
