title: The Birds That Build Giant Apartment Blocks
subject: sociable weaver | Philetairus socius
mood: curious
tags: sociable weaver, bird nest, africa, kalahari, birds
---
These birds build the *biggest nests* of any bird.
Meet the *sociable weaver*, from the deserts of southern Africa.
They work together to build giant nests in trees, and even on telephone poles.
Some nests weigh over a *ton*.
They can house around *100* pairs of birds, each with their own room.
Some nests can last for over a *hundred years*, used by many generations.
The nests keep the birds cool in the scorching day, and warm on freezing desert nights.
Other animals move in too: lovebirds, finches, and even owls.
But danger lurks.
Snakes like cobras sometimes climb in to eat eggs and chicks. [Cape cobra | Naja nivea]
The weavers line the entrances with sharp straws to keep them out.
Sometimes, a nest becomes so heavy that the *tree* or pole collapses.
The ultimate bird apartment building.
