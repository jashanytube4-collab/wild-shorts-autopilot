This fish spends its life *hitchhiking* on sharks.
Meet the *remora*.
On top of its head, it has a special oval disc, which works like a *suction cup*.
It sticks onto sharks, whales, turtles, and even *boats*. [tiger shark | Galeocerdo cuvier]
Then it gets a free ride across the ocean.
It feeds on leftover scraps from the host's meals, and sometimes on parasites.
The disc is actually a modified fin.
It has rows of ridges that create suction, and it can attach or let go in a split second.
In some places, people once used remoras to fish.
They tied a line to the remora's tail, released it near a sea turtle, and pulled both back in.
Engineers have designed underwater robots with remora-inspired suction cups.
Remoras have even been seen attached to *divers*.
A free ride, and free food.
