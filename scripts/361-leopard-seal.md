This seal hunts *penguins*.
Meet the *leopard seal*, one of Antarctica's top predators.
It can grow over *three meters* long, with huge jaws that open very wide.
It waits near the edge of the ice for penguins to dive in. [Adélie penguin | Pygoscelis adeliae]
Then it chases them down underwater, grabbing them in its jaws.
It shakes them hard to tear them apart.
Leopard seals also eat other seals, fish, and krill.
And they have special teeth that work like a sieve, filtering out krill from the water.
In 2006, a photographer named *Paul Nicklen* was approached by a female leopard seal.
She tried to *feed* him penguins, again and again, as if teaching him to hunt.
He believed she was trying to help him, since he was so bad at catching them.
Leopard seals are also *vocal*, singing strange songs under the ice.
A spotted, smiling predator of the frozen south.
