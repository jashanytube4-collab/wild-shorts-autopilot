This is the largest *wild cat* on Earth.
Meet the *Siberian tiger*, also called the *Amur tiger*.
Large males can weigh over *200 kilograms*, and stretch over *three meters* long, including the tail.
It lives in the snowy forests of the Russian Far East, where winters fall below *minus 30*.
It has a thicker coat and more fat than tigers living in hot places.
It can travel over *20 kilometers* a day, searching for food.
It hunts wild boar, deer, and sometimes even *bears*. [Asiatic black bear | Ursus thibetanus]
In the 1940s, there may have been only around *40* left in the wild.
Russia became the first country to give tigers full protection.
Today, there are over *500*.
Each tiger's territory can be huge, sometimes over *1,000 square kilometers*.
Some tigers have even been tracked crossing into China.
A giant of the snowy forests, back from the edge.
