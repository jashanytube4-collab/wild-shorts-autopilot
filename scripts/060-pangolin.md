This is the only mammal covered in *scales*.
Meet the *pangolin*.
Its scales are made of *keratin*, the same stuff as your fingernails.
When threatened, it rolls up into a tight, armored *ball* that even lions struggle to open. [lion | Panthera leo]
It has no teeth.
Instead, it slurps up ants and termites with a sticky tongue almost as long as its *body*.
That tongue is attached near its *pelvis*.
A single pangolin can eat up to *70 million* insects a year.
It even has muscles to close its ears and nose to keep the ants out.
Sadly, those scales make it a target.
Pangolins are believed to be the most *trafficked* wild mammal on Earth, hunted for meat and for scales used in traditional medicine.
Scientists say the scales have no proven medical value.
They're the same as chewing your own *fingernails*.
