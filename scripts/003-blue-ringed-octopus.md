This tiny octopus carries enough venom to kill *26 adults*.
And it fits in the palm of your hand.
The *blue-ringed octopus* is barely the size of a *golf ball*.
Most of the time, it's a dull brown, hiding in rock pools.
But when it feels threatened, it flashes dozens of *electric blue rings*.
That's its final warning.
Its bite is so small it's often *painless*.
But its saliva contains *tetrodotoxin*, a nerve poison far deadlier than *cyanide*.
Within minutes, your muscles stop working.
You can't move, can't speak, and eventually, you *can't breathe*.
The terrifying part? You stay *fully conscious* the whole time.
There is *no antivenom*.
The only treatment is to keep the victim breathing, sometimes for *hours*, until the toxin wears off.
So if you ever see those blue rings in a rock pool, *don't touch it*.
