This giant fish looks like it's missing its *back half*.
Meet the *ocean sunfish*, or *mola mola*.
It's the heaviest bony fish in the world.
One sunfish was found weighing around *2,700 kilograms*, as much as a pickup truck.
Instead of a normal tail, it has a stubby, rounded flap called a *clavus*.
It swims by flapping its tall top and bottom fins, side to side.
Sunfish often lie flat at the surface, as if *sunbathing*.
That's how they got their name.
They may be warming up after deep dives into cold water, and letting seabirds pick parasites off their skin.
A single female can produce around *300 million* eggs at once, more than any other known animal with a backbone.
Babies start out tiny, and can grow around *60 million* times bigger by adulthood.
They mostly eat *jellyfish*, and lots of them.
A floating head, the size of a car.
