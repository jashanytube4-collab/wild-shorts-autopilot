Legend said this animal was born in *fire*.
Meet the *fire salamander*.
In medieval Europe, people saw them crawling out of burning logs, and believed they were made of *flames*.
In reality, they had been hiding inside the logs before they were thrown on the fire.
Fire salamanders are *poisonous*.
Their bright yellow spots are a warning.
They can spray toxins from glands on their backs, sometimes over a distance of a meter or more.
The poison can cause muscle cramps and breathing problems in predators.
Unlike most amphibians, many females give birth to *live larvae*, or even fully formed babies.
Some can store sperm for years.
Fire salamanders can live over *50 years* in captivity.
Sadly, they're now threatened by a deadly fungus spreading through Europe.
A creature of legend, with a very real defense.
