title: Why You Should Never Fight A Kangaroo

A male kangaroo can kick hard enough to *break bones*.
The *red kangaroo* is the largest marsupial on Earth.
Big males stand around *1.8 meters* tall and are packed with muscle.
When they fight, they lean back on their thick tail and kick with *both feet* at once.
Kangaroos also box with their front arms, grabbing and pushing rivals.
They can hop at over *50 kilometers per hour*, and clear around *eight meters* in a single bound.
And they can't easily walk *backwards*, which many say is why they're on Australia's coat of arms, always moving *forward*.
Baby kangaroos, called *joeys*, are born the size of a *jellybean*.
They crawl into the pouch and stay there for months.
A mother can have a joey in her pouch, an embryo on hold, and an older one at her side, all at *once*.
