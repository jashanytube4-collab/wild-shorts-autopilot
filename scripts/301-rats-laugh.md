title: Rats Laugh When You Tickle Them
subject: brown rat | Rattus norvegicus
mood: curious
tags: rats, laughing rats, smart animals, science, animal emotions
---
Rats *laugh* when you tickle them.
Scientists discovered it in the 1990s.
When tickled, rats make high-pitched *chirps*, too high for humans to hear without special equipment.
They chase the scientist's hand, asking for more.
Rats even *jump* for joy, like happy little hops.
They also seem to have ticklish spots, especially on their bellies.
Rats are incredibly smart.
They can learn their names, solve mazes, and remember routes for a long time.
In experiments, rats freed trapped friends, and even *shared* chocolate with them.
Some rats have been trained to sniff out *landmines* in Africa.
One rat, named *Magawa*, found over *100* landmines and explosives in Cambodia, and won a gold medal for bravery.
Rats can also squeeze through holes about the size of a *coin*.
Smart, social, ticklish, and very misunderstood.
