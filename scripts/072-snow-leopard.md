This cat is so rarely seen that people call it the *ghost of the mountains*.
Meet the *snow leopard*.
It lives high in the mountains of Central Asia, often above *3,000 meters*.
Its smoky grey coat blends perfectly with the rocks and snow.
Its huge paws work like natural *snowshoes*.
And its tail is almost as long as its body.
It uses it for balance on steep cliffs, and wraps it around its face like a *scarf* when it sleeps.
Snow leopards can leap up to *15 meters* across rocky ravines.
They hunt wild sheep and goats on cliffs where one slip could be fatal. [Siberian ibex | Capra sibirica]
Surprisingly, it can't *roar*.
Instead, it makes a puffing sound called a *chuff*, along with mews and growls.
There may be only around *4,000 to 6,500* left in the wild.
Some photographers have waited months just to get a single picture.
