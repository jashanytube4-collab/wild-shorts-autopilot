title: The World's Smartest Troublemaker Parrot
subject: kea | Nestor notabilis
mood: curious
tags: kea, parrot, smart birds, new zealand, funny animals
---
This parrot is famous for *destroying cars*.
Meet the *kea*, the world's only *alpine parrot*, from the mountains of New Zealand.
Kea love to rip off windshield wipers, pull out rubber seals, and steal things from backpacks.
Tourists even have to protect their cars in some parking lots.
But this isn't just mischief. Kea are incredibly *smart*.
They solve puzzles, use tools, and work in *teams*.
In one study, kea even understood *probabilities*, choosing the jar more likely to give them a treat.
They have a special *laughing call* that makes other kea start playing.
It's one of the few known cases of contagious "laughter" in birds.
Kea are curious about everything, especially anything new.
Sadly, they're *endangered*, with only a few thousand left.
Clever, cheeky, and always up to something.
