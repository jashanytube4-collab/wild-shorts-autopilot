title: Africa's Most Feared Snake
subject: black mamba | Dendroaspis polylepis
mood: danger
tags: black mamba, snakes, venomous snakes, africa, fastest snake
---
This snake isn't named for its skin. It's named for its *mouth*.
The *black mamba* is actually grey or brown.
But inside, its mouth is *inky black*, and it shows it off as a warning.
It's one of the fastest snakes on Earth, reaching around *16 kilometers per hour* in short bursts.
And it's long, often over *two and a half meters*.
When cornered, it rears up, opens that black mouth, and spreads a narrow *hood*.
If that doesn't work, it strikes, often *several times* in a row.
Its venom attacks the *nervous system*.
Before antivenom existed, a serious bite was almost *always fatal*.
Today, with fast treatment, most victims *survive*.
Despite its reputation, the black mamba doesn't hunt people.
It eats rodents and birds, and returns to the same *home den* for years.
Like most snakes, it would much rather *escape* than fight.
