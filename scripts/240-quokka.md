This might be the *happiest-looking* animal on Earth.
Meet the *quokka*, a small marsupial from Western Australia.
It always seems to be *smiling*.
And that's made it famous for selfies, with tourists flocking to *Rottnest Island* to take pictures.
Quokkas are about the size of a house cat.
They hop like tiny kangaroos, and carry their babies in a pouch.
When the first Dutch explorers saw them in 1696, they thought they were giant *rats*.
They named the island "Rotte nest," meaning *rat's nest*.
Quokkas can climb small trees, and survive long periods without water by getting moisture from plants.
But don't feed or touch them.
Touching quokkas on Rottnest Island can lead to a *fine*.
And human food can make them sick.
They're listed as *vulnerable*, mostly because of foxes and cats on the mainland. [red fox | Vulpes vulpes]
Always smiling, even when they shouldn't be.
