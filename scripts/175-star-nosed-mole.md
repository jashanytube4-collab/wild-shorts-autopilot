This might be the fastest eater on the planet.
Meet the *star-nosed mole*.
It can find and eat a piece of food in around *a quarter of a second*.
That's faster than your eyes can follow.
It's almost blind, so it uses that weird pink "star" on its nose.
It has *22 fleshy tentacles*, covered in around *25,000* tiny touch sensors.
That makes it one of the most sensitive touch organs of any mammal.
It touches up to around *ten* different spots every second, building a picture of the world.
And it can even *smell underwater*.
It blows out tiny bubbles onto objects, then sniffs them back in.
It lives in wet soil and marshes in North America, hunting worms and insects.
A tiny animal with a superpower right on its face.
