This tiny sea slug *steals venom* from other animals.
Meet the *blue dragon*.
It's only around *three centimeters* long, and it floats upside down on the ocean surface.
It eats some of the most dangerous animals in the sea, including the *Portuguese man o' war*. [Portuguese man o' war | Physalia physalis]
It's immune to their stings.
And it stores their stinging cells in the tips of its own finger-like body.
That makes its sting even more *concentrated*.
Its blue color helps it blend in with the ocean when seen from above.
Its silver back blends in with the surface when seen from below.
Sometimes, storms wash blue dragons up on beaches, especially in *Australia*.
They look cute, but don't pick them up.
Their sting can be very *painful*.
A tiny, beautiful dragon, armed with stolen weapons.
