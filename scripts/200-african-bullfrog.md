This frog will attack almost anything that threatens its *babies*.
Meet the *African bullfrog*, one of the largest frogs in the world.
Males can weigh around *two kilograms*.
And they have tooth-like spikes in their lower jaw.
When the rains come, females lay thousands of eggs in shallow pools.
The male stays to *guard* them.
If the pool starts drying up, he digs a channel to connect it to deeper water, saving his tadpoles.
He attacks anything that comes too close, including snakes, birds, and even humans.
African bullfrogs also eat almost anything: insects, mice, snakes, and even other *bullfrogs*.
In the dry season, they bury themselves underground and wrap themselves in a cocoon of shed skin.
They can stay like that for months, or even years, waiting for rain.
The ultimate tough dad.
