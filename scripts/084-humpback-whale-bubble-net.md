title: How Humpback Whales Hunt With Bubbles
subject: humpback whale | Megaptera novaeangliae
mood: epic
tags: humpback whale, bubble net feeding, whales, ocean, alaska
---
Humpback whales hunt by building *nets* out of bubbles.
A group of whales dives below a school of fish.
Then one whale swims in a spiral, blowing bubbles from its blowhole.
The rising bubbles form a circular wall, a *bubble net*, trapping the fish inside.
Other whales make loud calls that drive the fish upward.
Then the whales shoot up through the middle with their mouths wide open, swallowing *thousands* of fish at once.
It's one of the most complex group hunting techniques in the animal kingdom.
Humpbacks are also famous *singers*.
Males sing long, complex songs that can last for *hours*.
And whales across an ocean region often sing the *same song*, which slowly changes over the years.
Humpbacks have also been seen protecting *seals* and other whales from *orcas*. [orca | Orcinus orca]
Nobody knows exactly why they do it.
Some call them the *heroes* of the sea.
