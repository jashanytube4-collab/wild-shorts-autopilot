This bird can learn around *200* different songs.
Meet the *northern mockingbird*.
Its scientific name means *many-tongued mimic*.
It copies the songs of other birds, frogs, and even car alarms and phones.
And it keeps learning new songs throughout its life.
Some males sing all night, especially unmated males in spring, trying to attract a partner.
Mockingbirds are fearless defenders of their nests.
They dive-bomb cats, dogs, hawks and even *people* who come too close.
Studies found they can recognize individual humans who've threatened their nests, and attack them, not others.
The northern mockingbird is the state bird of *five* US states, including Texas and Florida.
It's also featured in the famous novel *To Kill a Mockingbird*.
A small bird with a big voice, and a big attitude.
