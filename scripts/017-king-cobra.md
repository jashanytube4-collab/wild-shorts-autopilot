This snake's main food is *other snakes*.
Meet the *king cobra*, the longest venomous snake in the world.
It can grow over *five meters* long.
Its scientific name, *Ophiophagus*, literally means *snake eater*.
It hunts rat snakes, pythons, and even other *venomous cobras*.
When threatened, it can raise up to a third of its body off the ground, enough to look a grown human *in the eye*.
Then it spreads its hood and lets out a deep *growl*, a hiss so low it sounds like a dog.
One bite can deliver enough venom to kill an *elephant*. [Asian elephant | Elephas maximus]
And it's the only snake in the world known to build a *nest*.
The female gathers leaves into a mound, lays her eggs inside, and *guards it* for weeks.
The good news? King cobras are generally *shy*.
They'd much rather slither away than fight.
