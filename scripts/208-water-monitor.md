title: The Giant Lizard Living In Bangkok Parks
subject: Asian water monitor | Varanus salvator
mood: curious
tags: water monitor, monitor lizard, thailand, reptiles, city animals
---
In Bangkok, giant lizards roam the city *parks*.
These are *Asian water monitors*, the second-heaviest lizards in the world after the Komodo dragon.
They can grow over *two meters* long.
In Lumpini Park, hundreds of them swim in the lakes and lie on the grass beside joggers.
They're excellent swimmers, and can stay underwater for around *30 minutes*.
Water monitors eat almost anything: fish, frogs, birds, rats, eggs, and dead animals.
That makes them useful clean-up crews in the city.
They also have *mild venom* in their mouths.
In Thailand, calling someone a "hia," the local word for monitor lizard, is a serious *insult*.
In 2016, the city moved some of the lizards out of the park to other places.
Dinosaurs in the city, just going about their day.
