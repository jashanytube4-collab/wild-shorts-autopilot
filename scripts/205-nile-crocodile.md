Every year, Nile crocodiles are blamed for *hundreds* of deaths across Africa.
The *Nile crocodile* can grow over *five meters* long and weigh around *700 kilograms*.
It lives in rivers, lakes and swamps across much of the continent.
It hunts fish, antelopes, zebras, and even *buffalo*. [African buffalo | Syncerus caffer]
And it can wait patiently for the right moment, even for weeks, at the river crossings where animals come to drink.
When it strikes, it drags its prey into the water to *drown* it.
Crocodiles can't chew, so they tear off chunks by twisting their bodies in a *death roll*.
But Nile crocodiles are also surprisingly caring parents.
Mothers guard their nests for around *three months*.
When the babies call from inside the eggs, she digs them out.
Then she carries them to the water, gently, in her *jaws*.
The same jaws that can crush a zebra.
