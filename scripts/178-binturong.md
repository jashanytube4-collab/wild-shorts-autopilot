This animal smells exactly like *buttered popcorn*.
Meet the *binturong*, also called the *bearcat*.
But it's neither a bear nor a cat.
It's related to civets, and it lives in the forests of *Southeast Asia*.
That popcorn smell comes from its pee.
Scientists found it contains the same chemical that gives *popcorn* its smell.
Binturongs use it to mark their territory, and to say "I was here."
It has a long, gripping tail that works like an extra *hand*.
It's one of only two carnivores in the world with a tail like that.
It spends most of its time in trees, eating mostly *fruit*.
And by spreading the seeds of fig trees, it helps the forest grow.
It's one of the few animals that can easily digest the hard seed coat of certain *figs*.
A shaggy, fruit-loving bearcat, that smells like a movie theater.
