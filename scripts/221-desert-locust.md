title: When Grasshoppers Turn Into A Plague
subject: desert locust | Schistocerca gregaria
mood: danger
tags: locust, locust swarm, plague, insects, africa
---
A single locust swarm can contain *billions* of insects.
These are *desert locusts*.
Most of the time, they're shy, solitary grasshoppers.
But when it rains a lot, and their numbers grow, something changes.
When they're crowded, and keep bumping into each other, their brains release *serotonin*.
Within hours, they change their behavior.
Over generations, they even change *color*, and start gathering in huge swarms.
A swarm the size of a city can eat as much food in a day as *millions* of people.
They can fly over *100 kilometers* in a day, crossing countries and even seas.
In 2020, swarms swept across East Africa and South Asia, threatening the food of millions.
Locust plagues have been feared for thousands of years, and appear in ancient texts, including the Bible.
Scientists now use satellites and weather data to predict and stop swarms early.
A grasshopper, with a hidden dark side.
