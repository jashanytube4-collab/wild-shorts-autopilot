This animal climbs cliffs that would terrify a *mountain climber*.
Meet the *mountain goat*, from the Rocky Mountains of North America.
It lives on steep, rocky slopes, high above the tree line.
Its hooves have a hard outer rim and soft, grippy pads, perfect for gripping rock.
It can balance on ledges just a few centimeters wide.
And it can jump around *three meters* in a single leap.
Mountain goats aren't true goats.
They're more closely related to antelopes and other wild goat-like animals.
Their thick white coats keep them warm in temperatures as low as *minus 45*, and winds of around *160 kilometers per hour*.
In Glacier National Park, they lick salt left by human sweat on trails.
Babies can climb and jump within *hours* of being born.
But it's dangerous up there: avalanches and falls kill many goats every year.
Life on the edge, literally.
