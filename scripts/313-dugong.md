Sailors may once have mistaken this animal for a *mermaid*.
Meet the *dugong*, a cousin of the manatee.
Its tail is shaped like a dolphin's, making it the only sea cow with a whale-like *fluke*.
Dugongs spend their days grazing on *seagrass*, up to around *40 kilograms* a day.
They leave long trails across the seafloor where they've been feeding.
They have bristly snouts that help them grab the grass.
Dugongs can stay underwater for around *six minutes*.
They can live for around *70 years*.
But they reproduce very slowly, with females having a calf only every few years.
Mother and calf stay close, and calves sometimes ride on mom's back.
Some scientists believe dugongs helped inspire *mermaid* legends.
Today, they're threatened by boats, fishing nets, and loss of seagrass.
Large populations survive in places like Australia's *Shark Bay*.
A gentle grazer of underwater meadows.
