The deadliest cat in the world isn't a lion or a tiger. [lion | Panthera leo]
It's this one, the *black-footed cat*.
It's one of the smallest wild cats on Earth, weighing only about *two kilograms*.
But it succeeds in around *60 percent* of its hunts.
Lions? Only about *20 to 25 percent*.
It lives in the dry grasslands of southern Africa and hunts all night long.
In a single night, it can kill *10 to 14* rodents or small birds.
That's around one kill every *50 minutes*.
It walks up to *30 kilometers* a night searching for food.
And it's tough. These tiny cats have been seen standing up to animals much bigger than themselves.
Some locals even tell legends that it can take down a *giraffe* by biting its neck.
That's not true, but it shows how fierce its reputation is.
It's shy, rarely seen, and *impossible to tame*.
Cute? Yes. Harmless? Not if you're a mouse.
