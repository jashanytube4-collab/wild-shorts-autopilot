title: The Turtle That Eats Jellyfish
subject: leatherback sea turtle | Dermochelys coriacea

The biggest turtle on Earth eats *jellyfish*.
Meet the *leatherback sea turtle*.
It can grow over *two meters* long and weigh around *500 kilograms* or more.
Unlike other turtles, it has no hard shell.
Its back is covered in thick, rubbery skin over a layer of small bones.
Its mouth and throat are lined with backward-pointing *spines*.
They let jellyfish in, but stop them from slipping back out. [lion's mane jellyfish | Cyanea capillata]
Leatherbacks can dive deeper than *1,000 meters*, one of the deepest dives of any reptile.
They migrate thousands of kilometers across oceans, between feeding grounds and nesting beaches.
And unlike most reptiles, they can keep their bodies warmer than the cold water around them.
Sadly, plastic bags in the ocean look a lot like *jellyfish*.
Many leatherbacks have been found with plastic in their stomachs.
So every bag kept out of the sea could help save one.
