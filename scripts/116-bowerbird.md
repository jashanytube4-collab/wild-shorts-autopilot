title: The Bird That Decorates To Find Love
subject: satin bowerbird | Ptilonorhynchus violaceus
mood: curious

This bird builds and decorates a *house* to find love.
Meet the *satin bowerbird* from Australia.
The male builds a structure called a *bower*, made of sticks arranged into a little tunnel.
Then he decorates the area around it, but only with *blue* things.
Blue flowers, blue feathers, blue berries.
Near cities, he collects blue *bottle caps*, straws and clothes pegs.
He arranges them carefully, and even moves things around if they're out of place.
Some bowerbirds even *paint* their bowers, using chewed plants as paint.
When a female arrives, he dances, puffs out his feathers, and mimics other birds.
If she's impressed, she'll mate with him, then leave to raise the chicks *alone*.
The bower isn't a nest. It's a place to *show off*.
Other bowerbird species arrange objects in size order, creating an illusion that makes them look *bigger*.
Artists, architects, and hopeless romantics.
