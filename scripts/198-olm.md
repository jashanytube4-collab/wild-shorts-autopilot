People once believed this animal was a baby *dragon*.
Meet the *olm*, a blind salamander that lives in dark underground caves in Europe.
When floods washed olms out of caves in *Slovenia*, locals thought they were dragon babies.
Olms are pale pink, with no working eyes.
They live in total darkness, finding food using smell, hearing, and *electric* senses.
And they can live a very long time, possibly over *100 years*.
They're also champions of doing *nothing*.
One olm was observed not moving from the same spot for over *seven years*.
They can survive up to around *ten years* without food.
Their metabolism is incredibly slow.
Olms keep their baby gills their whole lives, just like axolotls.
In Slovenia, the olm is a national symbol, even appearing on *coins*.
A real-life baby dragon, living in the dark.
