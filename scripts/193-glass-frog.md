title: The Frog With See-Through Skin

You can see this frog's *heart* beating through its skin.
Meet the *glass frog*, from the rainforests of Central and South America.
Its belly is so transparent that you can see its *organs*, bones and even its *eggs*.
But scientists found an even crazier trick.
When it sleeps during the day, the frog becomes up to *61 percent* more transparent.
How? It pulls almost all the red blood cells out of its circulation, and packs them into its *liver*.
That makes its body nearly see-through, so predators can't spot it on a leaf.
When it wakes up, the blood flows back out.
Normally, that much clumped blood would cause deadly clots.
Scientists are studying how glass frogs avoid that, hoping to help with *human medicine*.
Glass frog dads also guard their eggs, sometimes for *weeks*.
A frog that can almost turn *invisible*.
