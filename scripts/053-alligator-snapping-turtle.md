title: The Turtle That Fishes With Its Tongue

This turtle catches fish using its *tongue* as bait.
Meet the *alligator snapping turtle*, one of the largest freshwater turtles in the world.
It can weigh over *80 kilograms*, with a spiky shell that looks like a *dinosaur*.
It lies motionless on the river bottom with its mouth wide open.
Inside, on its tongue, is a little pink flap that wiggles just like a *worm*.
When a fish swims in to take a bite, the jaws slam shut.
And those jaws are strong enough to cause serious injury to a human hand.
It can stay underwater for around *50 minutes* before coming up to breathe.
These turtles may live for over *100 years*.
Their family goes back *tens of millions* of years.
Sadly, they were hunted heavily for turtle soup.
Today, they're protected in many US states.
A living fossil, with a fishing rod built in.
