title: The Longest Snake In The World
subject: reticulated python | Malayopython reticulatus
mood: danger

This is the longest snake in the world.
The *reticulated python* can grow over *six meters*, and the longest reliably measured was over *seven and a half meters*.
It lives in Southeast Asia, from rainforests to rice fields and even *cities*.
Its pattern is a complex net of shapes, which is where the name *reticulated*, meaning net-like, comes from.
It's an ambush hunter.
It waits motionless, sometimes for days, then grabs prey with backward-curved *teeth*.
It eats rats, birds, pigs, deer, and occasionally *larger* prey.
And it's an excellent *swimmer*, which may be how it spread across so many islands.
Pythons have heat-sensing pits along their lips.
These let them "see" the body heat of warm-blooded prey, even in total *darkness*.
Females wrap around their eggs and shiver their muscles to keep them *warm*.
A giant snake that can live almost anywhere.
