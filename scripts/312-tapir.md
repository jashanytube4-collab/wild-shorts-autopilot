title: The Animal That Hasn't Changed In 20 Million Years
subject: lowland tapir | Tapirus terrestris
mood: curious
tags: tapir, living fossil, south america, weird animals, rainforest
---
Tapirs have looked much the same for around *20 million years*.
They look like a mix of a pig and an anteater.
But their closest relatives are *horses* and *rhinos*. [white rhinoceros | Ceratotherium simum]
That flexible snout is a short *trunk*.
They use it to grab leaves and fruit, and even as a snorkel when swimming.
Tapirs love water, and they can walk along river bottoms.
Baby tapirs are covered in white stripes and spots, like a *watermelon*.
It helps them hide in the dappled light of the forest.
The stripes fade as they grow older.
Tapirs are important seed spreaders, helping forests grow.
Scientists sometimes call them the *gardeners of the forest*.
They're shy and mostly active at night.
Sadly, all tapir species are threatened, by hunting and forest loss.
An ancient animal, still wandering the rainforests.
