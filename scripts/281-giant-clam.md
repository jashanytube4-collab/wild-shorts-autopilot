This clam can weigh over *200 kilograms*, and live for over *100 years*.
Meet the *giant clam*, the largest clam in the world.
It can grow over *1.2 meters* across.
Old stories said giant clams could trap a diver's *foot* and drown them.
That's a myth. They close too slowly, and many can't close completely.
Giant clams have a secret partner: tiny *algae* living in their tissues.
The algae use sunlight to make food, and share it with the clam.
That's why giant clams live in shallow, sunny water.
Their colorful lips can glow with blue, green and purple patterns.
Scientists think those colors protect the algae from too much sunlight.
Once a giant clam settles on the reef, it stays in the same spot for the *rest of its life*.
Sadly, overharvesting has made them rare in many places.
A gentle giant with a garden inside.
