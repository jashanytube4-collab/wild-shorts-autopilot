title: The Animal That Doesn't Seem To Age
subject: naked mole-rat | Heterocephalus glaber
mood: curious
tags: naked mole rat, weird animals, longevity, africa, rodents
---
This animal barely seems to *age*.
Meet the *naked mole-rat*.
Most rodents its size live two or three years.
The naked mole-rat can live over *30 years*.
And as it gets older, its risk of dying doesn't really go up, something almost unheard of in mammals.
It rarely gets *cancer*.
It can survive *18 minutes* with no oxygen at all, by switching its body to burn a different kind of sugar, like a *plant*.
Its skin barely feels some kinds of pain, even from *acid*.
They live underground in colonies of up to *300*, ruled by a single *queen*, just like bees or ants.
Only she has babies.
Everyone else digs, guards, or keeps the pups warm.
And when the queen dies, the females may *fight* to take her place.
It might be one of the ugliest animals alive.
But scientists study it hoping to unlock the secrets of *staying young*.
