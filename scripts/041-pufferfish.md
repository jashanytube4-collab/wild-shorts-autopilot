title: Why Eating This Fish Can Kill You
subject: pufferfish | Takifugu rubripes
mood: danger
tags: pufferfish, fugu, poisonous fish, japan, tetrodotoxin
---
In Japan, chefs train for *years* just to be allowed to serve this fish.
Because if they make a mistake, you *die*.
This is the *pufferfish*, and the dish is called *fugu*.
Its organs, especially the liver and ovaries, contain *tetrodotoxin*.
It's far more toxic than *cyanide*, and there's no known antidote.
It paralyzes the muscles while the victim stays conscious, until breathing stops.
That's why chefs must pass a strict exam before they can prepare it.
And the liver is so dangerous that it's *banned* from being served.
But why is the pufferfish toxic in the first place?
It's defense.
When threatened, it gulps water and inflates into a *spiky ball* several times its normal size. [porcupinefish | Diodon holocanthus]
Anything that still tries to eat it gets a mouthful of poison.
And like some poison frogs, pufferfish get their toxin from *bacteria* in their food.
Farmed fugu raised on a safe diet are *nontoxic*.
