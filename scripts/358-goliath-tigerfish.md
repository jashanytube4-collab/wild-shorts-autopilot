This fish has teeth like a *monster*.
Meet the *goliath tigerfish*, from Africa's *Congo River*.
It can grow over *1.5 meters* long, and weigh around *50 kilograms*.
And it has *32* huge, sharp teeth, about the same size as a great white shark's.
Some locals say it attacks people, though that's rare and hard to prove.
It hunts other fish in fast-flowing, powerful rivers.
It's even been known to catch fish the size of a small person's forearm.
Its relative, the African tigerfish, has been filmed leaping out of the water to catch *birds* in flight. [African tigerfish | Hydrocynus vittatus]
That was the first time scientists recorded a freshwater fish catching birds in flight.
Goliath tigerfish are prized by sport fishermen.
They're incredibly strong, and very hard to catch.
A river monster, made real.
