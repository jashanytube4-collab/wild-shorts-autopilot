subject: chambered nautilus | Nautilus pompilius
mood: curious

This animal has barely changed in around *500 million years* of family history.
Meet the *chambered nautilus*.
It's related to octopuses and squid, but it lives inside a beautiful *spiral shell*.
The shell is divided into chambers.
The nautilus lives in the newest, biggest one.
It controls the gas and liquid in the older chambers to float up and sink down, like a *submarine*.
In fact, the first submarines were named after it.
Nautiluses have around *90 tentacles*, with no suckers.
They grip with ridges and sticky surfaces instead.
They live in deep water by day, rising closer to the surface at night to feed.
They can live over *20 years*, much longer than most octopuses and squid.
Sadly, they're hunted for their beautiful shells.
A living fossil that has survived mass extinctions.
