Why is the termite queen so *huge*?
Because she's basically an *egg factory*.
In some species, her body swells to many times the size of the workers.
She can lay *thousands* of eggs a day.
Workers feed her, clean her, and carry her eggs away.
She can live for decades, one of the longest-living insects known.
And she's not alone. Termite colonies have a *king* too, who stays with her for life.
Some African termites build mounds over *seven meters* tall.
Inside, there's a clever system of tunnels that helps keep the air fresh and the temperature steady.
Architects even copied the idea to design a building in *Zimbabwe* that stays cool without air conditioning.
Some termites farm *fungus* too, just like leafcutter ants.
Termites might damage houses, but in the wild, they're vital recyclers, breaking down dead wood and returning *nutrients* to the soil.
