This spider *dances* to stay alive.
Meet the *peacock spider*, a tiny jumping spider from Australia.
It's only about *five millimeters* long, smaller than a grain of rice.
But the male has a secret.
On his back is a colorful flap, painted in shimmering *blue*, *red* and *orange*.
When he finds a female, he raises it up like a *peacock's tail*.
Then he waves his legs and dances from side to side.
If she likes the show, she'll mate with him.
If she doesn't, she might *eat him*.
So the dance really matters.
Jumping spiders have excellent eyesight, with *eight eyes*, two of them huge and forward-facing.
They can see in color, and judge distances precisely to *pounce*.
Tiny, adorable, and fighting for their lives on the dance floor.
