title: The Ugliest Bird In The World?

This bird is often called the *undertaker*.
Meet the *marabou stork*.
With its bald head, hunched back, and black cape of feathers, it really looks like one.
It's one of the biggest flying birds on Earth, with wings spanning over *three meters*.
That's among the largest wingspans of any land bird.
It's a scavenger, often following vultures to carcasses.
Its bald head helps it stay clean while digging into *dead animals*.
That pink sack hanging from its neck? It's connected to its breathing system and may be used in *display*.
Marabou storks have also adapted to modern life.
In cities like Kampala, they eat at *garbage dumps*, and sometimes nest on buildings.
And they'll eat almost anything, from fish and frogs to small birds, and even *shoes*, in some reports.
Ugly? Maybe. Useful? *Definitely*.
They help clean up the environment.
