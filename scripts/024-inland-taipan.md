This snake has the most toxic venom of any snake on Earth.
Meet the *inland taipan*.
One bite holds enough venom to kill around *100 grown men*.
Yet almost nobody has ever died from one.
Why? Because it lives in the remote deserts of *central Australia*, where very few people live.
And it's surprisingly *shy*.
It has another name, the *fierce snake*, but it usually escapes rather than fights.
Its venom evolved for one main target, *rats*.
It strikes and lets go, and the rat collapses within moments.
Here's something wild: it changes color with the seasons.
In winter it turns darker, to soak up more *heat*, and in summer, it turns *paler*.
Scientists believe it's an adaptation to the desert's extreme temperatures.
The deadliest snake in the world is also one of the *least seen*.
