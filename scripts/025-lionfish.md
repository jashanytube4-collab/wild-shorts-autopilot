title: The Beautiful Fish Destroying The Ocean
subject: red lionfish | Pterois volitans
mood: danger

This beautiful fish is wiping out reefs on the other side of the world.
Meet the *lionfish*.
It's covered in long, frilly spines, and those spines are *venomous*.
A sting can cause intense pain, sweating and swelling for *hours*.
Lionfish are native to the Pacific and Indian Oceans.
But in the 1980s, they started showing up off the coast of *Florida*.
Scientists believe aquarium owners released them.
They've since spread across the *Caribbean* and the *Gulf of Mexico*.
Here, almost nothing eats them.
And a single female can release around *two million eggs* a year.
They devour small reef fish, and a single lionfish can cut young fish on a reef by about *80 percent* in a few weeks.
So people are fighting back the tastiest way possible.
They hold *lionfish hunting* tournaments, and eat them.
Once the spines are removed, they're actually *delicious*.
