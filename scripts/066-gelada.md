This monkey looks like it has a *bleeding heart* on its chest.
Meet the *gelada*, found only in the highlands of *Ethiopia*.
That bright red patch of bare skin is how they show off.
In males, the redder the patch, the more *dominant* he is.
And they have huge, terrifying fangs.
When a gelada flips its upper lip inside out, showing off its teeth and gums, it's sending a *warning*.
But here's the surprise: geladas are almost *vegetarian*.
They're the only primates that live mainly on *grass*, eating it all day long.
They live on high cliffs and grassy plateaus, sleeping on steep rock ledges to stay safe from predators.
Groups can join together into herds of *hundreds*.
And they're incredibly chatty.
Scientists found their calls follow some of the same patterns found in *human language*.
A fearsome face, for a grass-munching chatterbox.
