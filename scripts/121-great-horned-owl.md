This owl regularly hunts *skunks*.
Meet the *great horned owl*, one of the most powerful owls in the Americas.
It has a terrible sense of *smell*.
So a skunk's spray, the thing that keeps most predators away, doesn't bother it at all. [striped skunk | Mephitis mephitis]
Its talons grip with enormous force, strong enough to crush the spine of its prey.
It hunts rabbits, rats, ducks, and even other *owls* and *hawks*.
Those "horns" on its head aren't horns or ears, they're feather tufts.
They may help with *camouflage*, breaking up its outline against tree bark.
Great horned owls are some of the earliest nesting birds in North America.
They sometimes lay eggs in mid-winter, keeping them warm through *snowstorms*.
They don't build their own nests, they take over nests made by hawks, crows or *squirrels*.
Their deep hooting, *hoo-h'HOO-hoo-hoo*, is the classic owl sound in movies.
