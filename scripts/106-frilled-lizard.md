title: The Lizard With A Dinosaur Frill
subject: frilled lizard | Chlamydosaurus kingii
mood: curious

When this lizard gets scared, it turns into a *monster*.
Meet the *frilled lizard* from northern Australia.
Most of the time, its frill lies folded around its neck like a cape.
But when threatened, it opens its mouth wide and the frill *snaps open* like an umbrella.
It can be around *30 centimeters* across, making the lizard look many times bigger.
It hisses, lashes its tail, and sometimes *charges*.
And if that doesn't work, it runs away on its *back legs*.
That upright sprint looks so much like a tiny dinosaur that it inspired a creature in *Jurassic Park*.
The Dilophosaurus in the movie, with its frill and spit, borrowed the idea.
Frilled lizards spend most of their time up in trees, coming down to hunt insects after rain.
Australia even put one on its *two-cent coin*.
The ultimate bluff, in lizard form.
