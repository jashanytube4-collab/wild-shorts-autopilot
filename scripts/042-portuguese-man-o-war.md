title: This Isn't A Jellyfish, It's A Colony
subject: Portuguese man o' war | Physalia physalis
mood: danger

---
This might look like a jellyfish, but it isn't one animal at all.
The *Portuguese man o' war* is a colony of tiny creatures working together as *one*.
Some form the float, a gas-filled sail that catches the *wind*.
Others form the tentacles, others digest the food, and others handle reproduction.
None of them can survive *alone*.
Its tentacles usually reach around *ten meters*, but some stretch up to *30 meters*.
That's as long as a *blue whale*.
They're covered in stinging cells that deliver *excruciating pain* to anyone who touches them.
And here's the scary part: even when it's dead and washed up on the beach, it can still *sting*.
It can't swim. It just drifts wherever the wind and currents take it.
So sometimes, thousands wash up on beaches at once.
If you see a blue balloon on the sand, admire it from a *distance*.
