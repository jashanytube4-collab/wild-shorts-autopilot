subject: green anaconda | Eunectes murinus

This is the heaviest snake on Earth.
The *green anaconda* can weigh over *200 kilograms*.
And it can grow longer than *seven meters*.
It lives in the swamps and rivers of South America, hiding in murky water with only its eyes and nostrils showing.
When prey comes to drink, it strikes, wraps its body around it, and *squeezes*.
For years, people thought constrictors killed by suffocation.
But scientists found they mostly cut off *blood flow*, stopping the heart and brain within minutes.
Anacondas can swallow capybaras, deer, and even *caimans* whole. [spectacled caiman | Caiman crocodilus]
Their jaws aren't fused at the front, so their mouth can stretch around prey much wider than their head.
After a big meal, an anaconda may not eat again for *weeks*.
Females are much bigger than males.
And sometimes, they *eat* the male after mating.
In 2024, scientists announced that there are actually *two* species of green anaconda, not one.
