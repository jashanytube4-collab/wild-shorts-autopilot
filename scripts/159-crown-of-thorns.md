title: The Starfish That Eats Coral Reefs
subject: crown-of-thorns starfish | Acanthaster planci
mood: danger
tags: crown of thorns starfish, coral reef, great barrier reef, ocean, venomous
---
This starfish can eat its way across an entire *coral reef*.
Meet the *crown-of-thorns starfish*.
It's covered in sharp, *venomous* spines that can cause painful wounds.
It can grow to around *35 centimeters* across, with up to *21 arms*.
To eat, it pushes its stomach out of its *mouth*, onto the coral.
Then it digests the soft coral animals, leaving behind white skeletons.
In normal numbers, they're part of a healthy reef.
But sometimes their population *explodes*.
On Australia's *Great Barrier Reef*, outbreaks have been one of the major causes of coral loss.
A single female can release *millions* of eggs.
Divers fight back, injecting them with *vinegar* or other solutions that kill them.
Even robots have been developed to hunt them.
And if you cut one in half, it may regrow.
A reef's worst nightmare, in starfish form.
