title: The Hornet That Kills 30,000 Bees In Hours

A few dozen of these hornets can kill *30,000 bees* in a few hours.
This is the *Asian giant hornet*, the largest hornet in the world, some calling it the *murder hornet*.
Queens can grow over *five centimeters* long.
Its stinger is about *six millimeters*, long enough to go through a beekeeper's suit.
When a scout finds a beehive, it marks it with a scent, and its sisters arrive.
They rip the heads off the bees, then carry the larvae home to feed their young.
But Japanese honeybees fight back in a genius way. [Japanese honey bee | Apis cerana]
Hundreds of them swarm the hornet and form a tight *ball* around it. [Japanese honey bee | Apis cerana]
Then they vibrate their flight muscles, heating the ball to around *46 degrees Celsius*.
The bees can survive that heat.
The hornet *can't*.
It's literally *cooked alive* by the bees it came to kill.
