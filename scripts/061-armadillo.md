title: The Armadillo That Always Has Quadruplets
subject: nine-banded armadillo | Dasypus novemcinctus
mood: curious
tags: armadillo, weird animals, quadruplets, texas, animal facts

This animal almost always gives birth to *identical quadruplets*.
Meet the *nine-banded armadillo*.
A single fertilized egg splits into four, so every baby in a litter is the *same sex* and genetically identical.
It's the only mammal known to do this so regularly.
Its shell is made of bony plates covered by *skin*.
And despite the myth, this species *can't* roll into a ball.
Only the three-banded armadillo can. [three-banded armadillo | Tolypeutes matacus]
When startled, the nine-banded armadillo jumps straight up, about a *meter* into the air.
Unfortunately, that's exactly the height of a car's bumper.
It can hold its breath for around *six minutes*, letting it walk across the bottom of streams.
Or it gulps air to inflate its stomach and *float* across.
And it's one of the few animals that can carry *leprosy*.
Its low body temperature makes it a perfect host, so scientists have used armadillos to study the disease.
