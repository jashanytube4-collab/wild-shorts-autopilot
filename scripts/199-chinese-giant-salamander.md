This is the biggest amphibian on Earth.
The *Chinese giant salamander* can grow up to around *1.8 meters* long.
It lives in cold mountain streams in China.
It's been around in a similar form for millions of years, making it a true *living fossil*.
It breathes mostly through its *skin*, which is wrinkly to increase surface area.
It has tiny eyes and poor eyesight, so it hunts using sensors that detect vibrations in the water.
It strikes in a fraction of a second, sucking in fish, frogs and crabs.
Its cries sound like a *crying baby*, which is why in China it's sometimes called the *baby fish*.
Sadly, it's *critically endangered*.
It's hunted for food and farmed in huge numbers, and many wild populations have disappeared.
Scientists recently found that there may be several different species of Chinese giant salamander.
An ancient giant that needs our help.
