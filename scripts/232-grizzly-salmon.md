title: How Grizzly Bears Get Fat For Winter
subject: grizzly bear | Ursus arctos horribilis
mood: epic
tags: grizzly bear, salmon run, alaska, bears, hibernation
---
Before winter, a grizzly bear can eat around *30 salmon* in a single day.
Every summer, salmon swim upstream to lay their eggs. [sockeye salmon | Oncorhynchus nerka]
And grizzly bears wait at the waterfalls.
Some stand at the top, catching jumping salmon right out of the air.
Others wade in the water and pin them down with their paws.
Experienced bears often eat only the richest parts, the skin, brain and *eggs*, and leave the rest.
They can gain over *one kilogram* of fat a day.
In winter, they sleep in dens for months without eating, drinking, or going to the bathroom.
Their bodies recycle waste into *protein*, so they barely lose muscle.
And the leftover salmon? Scientists found it fertilizes the forest.
Trees near salmon rivers can grow *faster*, thanks to nutrients from fish carried by bears.
A grizzly can run at around *55 kilometers per hour*.
So don't ever try to outrun one.
