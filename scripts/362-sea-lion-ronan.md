title: The Sea Lion That Can Dance To Music
subject: California sea lion | Zalophus californianus

This sea lion can *dance* to music.
Her name is *Ronan*, a *California sea lion*.
Scientists trained her to bob her head to the beat.
Then they played her songs she had never heard before.
She kept the beat, adjusting to different tempos.
Before Ronan, scientists thought only humans, and some birds, could do this.
California sea lions are fast, playful, and smart.
They can swim at around *40 kilometers per hour*.
Unlike seals, they can walk on land using their *flippers*.
They're very loud, barking constantly.
In San Francisco, hundreds of sea lions gather on the docks at *Pier 39*.
They first showed up in 1989, and became a famous tourist attraction.
Some sea lions also work with the US Navy, trained to find objects underwater.
A sea lion with rhythm.
