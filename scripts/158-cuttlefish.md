This animal can change its color in less than a *second*, and it's colorblind.
Meet the *cuttlefish*.
Its skin contains millions of color cells it controls directly with its *brain*.
It can match rocks, sand, seaweed, and even make dark waves ripple across its body, hypnotizing prey.
Scientists still debate how it matches colors it can't see.
Some cuttlefish males disguise themselves as *females*, sneaking past bigger males to reach a mate.
Cuttlefish have *three hearts*, and blue-green blood.
And they're surprisingly smart.
In one study, cuttlefish passed a version of the *marshmallow test*.
They waited for a better snack instead of eating the first one, something even many young children struggle with.
They have *W-shaped* pupils, which may help them control the light coming into their eyes.
And they shoot two tentacles out to grab prey in a fraction of a second.
A colorblind artist with a mind of its own.
