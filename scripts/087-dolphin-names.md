title: Dolphins Call Each Other By Name
subject: common bottlenose dolphin | Tursiops truncatus
mood: curious
tags: dolphins, smart animals, signature whistle, ocean, dolphin facts
---
Dolphins have *names*, and they call each other by them.
Each bottlenose dolphin develops its own *signature whistle* when it's young.
Other dolphins copy that whistle to get its attention.
And dolphins can remember the whistles of old friends for over *20 years*.
That's the longest social memory known in any animal besides humans.
They sleep with only *half their brain* at a time, keeping one eye open, so they never stop breathing.
In Australia, some dolphins wear *sea sponges* on their beaks to protect them while digging for food on the seafloor.
Mothers teach this trick to their *daughters*.
Dolphins have been seen helping lost whales find their way, and protecting swimmers from sharks.
They also see with *sound*, sending out clicks and listening for echoes.
It's so precise they can detect an object the size of a *golf ball* from over *70 meters* away.
And they recognize themselves in a *mirror*.
Clever, social, and always talking.
