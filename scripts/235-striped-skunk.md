A skunk's spray can be smelled up to around *a mile* away.
Meet the *striped skunk*.
It sprays an oily liquid from glands under its tail, and it can aim at a target up to around *three meters* away.
The spray contains sulfur chemicals, similar to those in garlic and *rotten eggs*.
It can cause temporary blindness if it hits the eyes.
But skunks don't want to spray.
It takes them days to refill, so they give *warnings* first.
They stamp their feet, hiss, and raise their tail.
Spotted skunks even do a *handstand* before spraying. [western spotted skunk | Spilogale gracilis]
Tomato juice doesn't really remove the smell.
A mix of hydrogen peroxide, baking soda and dish soap works much better.
Only one animal regularly hunts skunks: the *great horned owl*, which has a terrible sense of smell. [great horned owl | Bubo virginianus]
Black and white means *back off*.
