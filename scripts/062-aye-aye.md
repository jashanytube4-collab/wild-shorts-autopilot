title: The Creepiest Primate In The World
subject: aye-aye | Daubentonia madagascariensis
mood: curious
tags: aye aye, madagascar, lemur, weird animals, primates
---
In Madagascar, some people believe this animal is an *omen of death*.
Meet the *aye-aye*, a lemur that looks like a mix of a bat, a rat, and a *gremlin*.
It has huge eyes, giant ears, and one very strange finger.
Its middle finger is long, skinny and *bony*.
At night, it taps on tree bark with that finger, listening for hollow tunnels made by grubs.
Then it gnaws a hole and uses the same finger to *hook* them out.
It's the only primate known to find food this way, a bit like a *woodpecker*.
Scientists even found it has a tiny sixth *pseudo-thumb* on each hand.
Because of local superstitions, some aye-ayes have been *killed on sight*.
Combined with forest loss, they're now *endangered*.
And here's a weird one: scientists recently filmed an aye-aye sticking its finger up its nose and *eating* what it found.
Creepy, clever, and completely one of a kind.
