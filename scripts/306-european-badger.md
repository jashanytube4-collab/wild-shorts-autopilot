Some badger homes are over *100 years* old.
*European badgers* live in underground tunnel systems called *setts*.
Some setts are used by badger families for many generations, maybe even centuries.
They can have dozens of entrances, and hundreds of meters of tunnels.
Badgers are clean animals.
They regularly change their bedding of grass and leaves, dragging it out to air in the sun.
They dig separate toilet pits outside the sett.
A badger's front claws are long and powerful, perfect for *digging*.
They eat earthworms, lots of them, sometimes hundreds in a night.
They also eat insects, fruit, and roots.
Sometimes, badgers even share their setts with *foxes*. [red fox | Vulpes vulpes]
Badgers can live in towns and cities too.
Shy, nocturnal, and expert architects of the underground.
