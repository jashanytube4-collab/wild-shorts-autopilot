This bird's kick can *slice open* a predator.
Meet the *cassowary*, often called the most dangerous bird on Earth.
It stands nearly *two meters* tall and can weigh as much as an adult human.
On each foot, it carries a *dagger-like claw* up to *12 centimeters* long.
It can sprint at *50 kilometers per hour*, leap high into the air, and it's even a strong *swimmer*.
That helmet on its head is called a *casque*.
Scientists still aren't completely sure what it's for.
Looking at those feet, it's easy to believe birds descended from *dinosaurs*.
In 2019, a man in Florida was killed by a cassowary he kept on his property.
But here's the surprising part: dads do the parenting.
The male sits on the bright *green eggs* for around *50 days*, then raises the chicks alone.
And the rainforest needs them, because some seeds only grow after passing through a *cassowary's gut*.
