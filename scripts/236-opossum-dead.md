When this animal is scared, it *plays dead*, and it can't control it.
Meet the *Virginia opossum*, North America's only *marsupial*.
When threatened, it falls over, goes stiff, drools, and lets its tongue hang out.
It even releases a smell like *rotting flesh*.
Predators that don't eat dead animals often lose interest.
This can last for minutes, or even *hours*.
It's not a choice. It's an automatic response, like fainting.
Opossums are also surprisingly helpful.
They eat ticks, insects, snails, and even *venomous snakes*, being highly resistant to snake venom.
Scientists are studying their blood for a possible universal *antivenom*.
They rarely get rabies, because their body temperature is lower than most mammals.
They have *50 teeth*, more than any other land mammal in North America.
A mother can carry up to around a dozen babies on her back.
Not a pest, but a free cleanup crew.
