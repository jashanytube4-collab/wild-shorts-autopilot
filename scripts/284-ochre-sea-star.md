title: The Starfish That Can Regrow Its Arms
subject: ochre sea star | Pisaster ochraceus
mood: curious
tags: starfish, sea star, regeneration, ocean, animal facts
---
If a starfish loses an arm, it can grow it *back*.
And for some species, the lost arm can even grow into a whole *new starfish*.
Starfish aren't actually fish. Scientists prefer the name *sea stars*.
They have no brain and no blood.
Instead, they pump seawater through their bodies to move.
On the bottom of their arms are hundreds of tiny *tube feet*.
They use them to walk, and to pull open clams and mussels.
Then they push their *stomach* out through their mouth, into the shell, and digest the food outside their body. [California mussel | Mytilus californianus]
The *ochre sea star* is famous in science.
When researchers removed them from a stretch of shoreline, mussels took over, and many other species disappeared.
It led to the idea of a *keystone species*, one that holds an ecosystem together.
Each arm has a tiny *eye* at its tip.
Five arms, no brain, and a lot of power.
