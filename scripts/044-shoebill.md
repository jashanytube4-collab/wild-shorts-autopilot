This bird looks like it walked straight out of the *dinosaur age*.
Meet the *shoebill*.
It stands up to *1.5 meters* tall, with a huge bill shaped like a wooden *shoe*.
And that bill ends in a sharp hook.
It lives in the swamps of East Africa, where it can stand *completely still* for hours.
Then it lunges, collapsing forward onto its prey with its whole body.
It eats fish, frogs, baby crocodiles, and its favorite, the *lungfish*. [marbled lungfish | Protopterus aethiopicus]
Instead of singing, it claps its bill together, making a sound like a *machine gun*.
Its long, unblinking stare made it go viral as the *scariest bird* online.
In hot weather, it scoops up water in its bill and pours it over its eggs to keep them *cool*.
But there's a dark side.
Shoebills usually lay two eggs, yet often only one chick survives.
The parents tend to raise only the *stronger* one.
