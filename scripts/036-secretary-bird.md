This bird kills *venomous snakes* by kicking them.
Meet the *secretary bird*, a bird of prey with the legs of a supermodel.
It stands over a meter tall and walks up to *30 kilometers* a day through the African grasslands.
When it finds a snake, it stamps down with a force about *five times* its own body weight.
And each kick lasts just *15 thousandths of a second*, faster than the blink of an eye.
That speed matters, because a single bite could be fatal.
Thick scales protect its legs, and it fans its wings out to confuse the snake.
It also hunts lizards, rodents, and big insects.
Its name may come from the feathers on its head, which look like old-fashioned *quill pens* tucked behind a clerk's ear.
It's so loved in Africa that it appears on the coat of arms of *South Africa* and *Sudan*.
Legs for running, feet for kicking, and zero fear of snakes.
