This animal walked alongside *woolly mammoths*, and it's still here.
Meet the *musk ox*.
It survived the end of the Ice Age while mammoths and saber-toothed cats disappeared.
Its secret is its wool.
Under its long outer hair is a soft underlayer called *qiviut*.
It's said to be up to *eight times* warmer than sheep's wool.
That lets musk oxen survive Arctic winters at *minus 40* and below.
When wolves attack, adults form a *circle* with their horns facing outward, and the calves hide in the middle. [gray wolf | Canis lupus]
Bulls charge each other at full speed, crashing their heads together with a crack heard far away.
They have a thick bony shield to protect their brains.
And despite the name, they don't produce *musk*.
The smell comes from males during the mating season.
A true survivor of the frozen past.
