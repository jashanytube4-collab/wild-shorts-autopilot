title: The Bird That Looks Like A Tree Branch
subject: common potoo | Nyctibius griseus
mood: curious
tags: potoo, ghost bird, camouflage, birds, rainforest
---
This bird hides by pretending to be a *broken branch*.
Meet the *potoo*.
During the day, it sits perfectly still on a tree stump, with its head pointed up.
Its feathers look exactly like *tree bark*.
Even its eyes have tiny slits in their eyelids, so it can watch for danger with its eyes *closed*.
At night, it wakes up and catches insects in its huge, wide *mouth*.
Its mouth is so big that it looks almost like a frog's.
Potoos are famous for their eerie, haunting calls in the night.
In parts of South America, people call them *ghost birds*.
Legends say the call belongs to a sad spirit.
Potoos don't build nests.
They lay a single egg in a small dip on a branch, and balance it there.
A ghost in the trees, hiding in plain sight.
