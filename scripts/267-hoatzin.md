This bird's chicks have *claws* on their wings, like a *dinosaur*.
Meet the *hoatzin*, from the swamps of the Amazon.
Baby hoatzins have two claws on each wing.
When danger comes, they drop from their nest into the water below.
Then they climb back up, using their wing claws to grip the branches.
The claws disappear as they grow up.
Hoatzins are also called *stinkbirds*.
They eat mostly leaves, which they ferment in their gut, like a cow.
That gives them a smell a lot like *manure*.
Because of this, people rarely hunt them for food.
They're terrible flyers, and they look a bit clumsy, with spiky crests and blue faces.
Scientists still debate where the hoatzin fits in the bird family tree.
A living reminder that birds came from dinosaurs.
