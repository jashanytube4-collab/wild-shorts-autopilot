There's a fungus that turns ants into *zombies*.
It's called *Ophiocordyceps*, and it inspired the monsters in *The Last of Us*.
It starts when a spore lands on a carpenter ant and drills through its armor. [carpenter ant | Camponotus]
Inside, the fungus spreads through the ant's body, wrapping around its *muscles*.
Then the ant starts acting strangely.
It leaves its colony, climbs up a plant, and bites down hard on the underside of a *leaf*.
It always seems to pick the perfect spot, with just the right temperature and humidity for the fungus.
Then, it *dies*, locked in place.
A few days later, a stalk grows out of the ant's *head*.
It rains spores down onto the forest floor below, infecting new ants.
But ants fight back.
Healthy workers recognize infected ones, and carry them far away from the colony.
And don't worry. This fungus can't infect *humans*.
