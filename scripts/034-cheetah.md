title: Why Cheetahs Can't Roar
subject: cheetah | Acinonyx jubatus
mood: epic
tags: cheetah, fastest land animal, big cats, africa, wildlife
---
The fastest land animal on Earth can't even *roar*.
Instead, the *cheetah* meows, chirps, and *purrs*, just like a house cat.
It can reach around *100 kilometers per hour*, and go from zero to *60 miles per hour* in about three seconds.
That's quicker than most sports cars.
Its spine bends like a *spring*, letting each stride cover around *seven meters*.
Its claws don't fully pull in, giving it grip like *running spikes*.
And its long tail works as a *rudder* for sharp turns.
But all that speed comes at a cost.
A cheetah can only keep up a full sprint for about *30 seconds*.
After a chase, it's so exhausted it needs to rest, and lions or hyenas often *steal its meal*. [spotted hyena | Crocuta crocuta]
Those black lines under its eyes act like a football player's eye black, cutting the *sun's glare*.
Built for speed, but nothing else.
