title: The Otter That Hunts Caimans

In the Amazon, there's an otter that hunts *caimans*.
Meet the *giant river otter*, the longest otter in the world, reaching almost *two meters*.
Locals call it the *river wolf*.
And just like wolves, they live and hunt in *family packs*.
A single otter can eat around *three to four kilograms* of fish a day.
Piranhas, catfish, whatever it can catch. [red-bellied piranha | Pygocentrus nattereri]
But when a family works together, it can take on much bigger prey, including *caimans* and even young *anacondas*. [spectacled caiman | Caiman crocodilus]
They're incredibly loud, too.
Scientists have recorded at least *22 different calls*, from whistles and hums to *screams* that warn of danger.
Every giant otter has a unique pale patch on its throat, as individual as a *fingerprint*.
Sadly, they were hunted for their fur so heavily that they nearly vanished.
Today they're *endangered*, with only a few thousand left in the wild.
