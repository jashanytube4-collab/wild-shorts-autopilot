This animal can kill a human in *under five minutes*.
And you'd probably never even see it coming.
Meet the *box jellyfish*, the most venomous creature in the ocean.
Its body is almost *completely transparent*.
It trails up to *60 tentacles*, each one up to *three meters* long.
Every tentacle is loaded with thousands of microscopic *harpoons*.
The moment they touch skin, they fire, injecting venom that attacks the *heart* and the *nervous system*.
Survivors describe the pain as *unbearable*.
But here's the strangest part.
It has no brain, yet it has *24 eyes*.
Some of them even have lenses, letting it *steer around obstacles*.
And unlike most jellyfish, it doesn't just drift.
It actively *swims*, hunting small fish and shrimp.
That's why some Australian beaches put up *stinger nets* every summer, and keep bottles of *vinegar* by the water.
