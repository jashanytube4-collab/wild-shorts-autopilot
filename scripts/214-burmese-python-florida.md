title: Giant Snakes Are Taking Over Florida
subject: Burmese python | Python bivittatus
mood: danger
tags: burmese python, invasive species, florida, everglades, snakes
---
Giant snakes from Asia are taking over the *Florida Everglades*.
They're *Burmese pythons*, and they can grow over *five meters* long.
They probably arrived through the pet trade, released by owners or escaping after hurricanes.
Now there may be tens of thousands in Florida.
With almost no natural predators, they've wiped out many animals.
In some areas, sightings of raccoons, opossums and rabbits have dropped by over *90 percent*. [raccoon | Procyon lotor]
They've even been found eating *alligators* and deer.
Florida now pays hunters to remove them.
There's even a yearly *Python Challenge*, where people compete to catch the most.
Scientists have also used "Judas snakes," males fitted with trackers, to lead them to breeding females.
In 2022, a record python was caught weighing around *98 kilograms*, carrying over *120* eggs.
An invasion that's very hard to stop.
