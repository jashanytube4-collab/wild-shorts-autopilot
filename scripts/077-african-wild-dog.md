Africa's most successful hunter isn't a lion. [lion | Panthera leo]
It's the *African wild dog*.
Packs succeed in up to around *80 percent* of their hunts.
They chase prey over long distances, tiring it out at speeds of up to *60 kilometers per hour*.
And they work as a team, taking turns to lead the chase.
Each dog's coat has a unique pattern of *patches*, that's why they're also called *painted wolves*.
But here's the amazing part: before a hunt, the pack seems to vote.
Dogs make short *sneezes*, and the more sneezes, the more likely they'll go.
After a kill, they let the *pups* and injured members eat first.
They even bring food back by coughing it up for the ones who stayed home.
Sadly, they're *endangered*, with only around *6,600* left in the wild.
Teamwork, democracy, and one of the best hunting records on Earth.
